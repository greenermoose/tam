Welcome to Tamlinux

Tamlinux is an independent, continuing Linux workstation aimed at the best
possible experience on any hardware. It is named for the tamarack larch: hardy,
golden in October, and always improving.

Finding your way

Most things start from the keyboard. SUPER + RETURN opens a terminal, and SUPER
+ K shows the keybindings when you forget one.

Learning, maintaining, improving

- Learn — read the man guide and the pipeline guide.
- Maintain — keep the system updated and know how to roll back, covered in
  installing and maintaining.
- Improve — explore the shell, plugins and local AI tools that ship with the
  workstation.
