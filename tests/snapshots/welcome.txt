Guide / Welcome to Tamlinux

Welcome to Tamlinux

Tamlinux is an independent, continuing Linux workstation aimed at the best
possible experience on any hardware. It is named for the tamarack larch: hardy,
golden in October, and always improving.

Reading this guide

This guide opens in a keyboard-driven pager. There is no mouse support, so use
the keys below to move around and to follow links.

- Scrolling: j / k or the up and down arrow keys scroll a line; Space / PgDn and
  b / PgUp scroll a screen; g / Home and G / End jump to the top or bottom. When
  the whole page already fits on the screen, none of these keys do anything.
- Links: Tab / Shift-Tab select the next or previous link, Enter or the right
  arrow follows it, u or the left arrow goes back, and q or Esc quits.
- Breadcrumb: the first line of every page is a breadcrumb trail. Its earlier
  parts are links, so you can jump back up to a section or to the guide index.
- Output: redirect the output (for example tam show --pipe) to read the guide as
  plain text with a numbered list of links.

Using your workstation

Your workstation is keyboard-driven too, but those shortcuts are separate from
this guide. SUPER + K lists the keybindings when you forget one, and SUPER +
RETURN opens another terminal when you need one. Explore more in the desktop
guide.

Learning, maintaining, improving

- Learn — read the shell guide, the pipeline guide, and the man guide.
- Maintain — keep the system updated and monitor health, covered in installing
  and maintaining and resources and performance.
- Improve — explore the shell, plugins and local AI tools that ship with the
  workstation.
