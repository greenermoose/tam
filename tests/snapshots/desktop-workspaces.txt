Workspaces and Multi-Display Layout

Workspaces provide separate virtual desktops so you can group related tasks
together without visual clutter.

Navigating workspaces

Keybinding              Action                                         
----------------------  -----------------------------------------------
SUPER + 1.. 9           Switch directly to workspace 1 through 9       
SUPER + Shift + 1.. 9   Move the active window to workspace 1 through 9
SUPER + [ /]            Cycle to previous or next workspace            

Synchronized workspace sets

In multi-monitor environments, Tamlinux supports synchronized workspace sets.
Switching a set switches both the primary and auxiliary displays in tandem,
keeping project contexts paired.

Press SUPER + Keypad 1.. 9 to switch the entire display set together across all
connected monitors.

The scratchpad

The scratchpad is a hidden tray for temporary utility windows (like a quick
scratchpad terminal, music player, or notes editor):

- SUPER + Shift + Minus — send the active window to the scratchpad.
- SUPER + Minus — toggle and cycle scratchpad windows into view.
