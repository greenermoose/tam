illumos

illumos continues the OpenSolaris code after Oracle stopped publishing Solaris source in 2010. It is a shared core, the kernel and base system, that several distributions package. Solaris introduced ZFS, zones (lightweight isolated environments), DTrace (live tracing of a running system) and SMF, the Service Management Facility, which supervises services and records their dependencies.

For Tamlinux, illumos is a horizon: studied for ideas, not considered as a base. Its distributions share one core, so they are not counted as separate kernels.

OpenIndiana

  • Known for: a community-driven continuation of the OpenSolaris desktop, usable as a workstation.
  • Tamlinux role: idea donor for illumos workstation integration.

OmniOS

  • Known for: a minimal server distribution with signed packages, long-term support releases, ZFS, zones, DTrace and SMF.
  • Tamlinux role: idea donor for a disciplined server life cycle. Its suitability as a desktop has not been tested.

SmartOS

  • Known for: booting from a read-only live image and running everything else in zones and virtual machines.
  • Tamlinux role: idea donor for virtualization. Its current governance and activity have not been checked.
