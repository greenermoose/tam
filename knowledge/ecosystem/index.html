The Tamlinux Ecosystem and Its Choices

This page introduces the software that makes up Tamlinux and the ideas behind it.

Tamlinux is designed for laptop and desktop computers running Linux. These pages describe its parts, the choices behind them, and questions that remain open.

Core principles

  1. User freedom: Tamlinux's own software is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later, protecting your right to run, study, change, and share it.
  2. Resource efficiency: event-driven, bounded work with no polling loops and no unnecessary resident daemons.
  3. Hardware longevity: choose a graphical or terminal profile by a machine's capability, not its age alone.
  4. Repairability: patches declare how they retire; nothing becomes permanent by neglect.
  5. Terminal first: most things can be done from a terminal with a keyboard; menus and bar widgets call the same commands.
  6. Offline help: this guide lives on disk and works without a network.
