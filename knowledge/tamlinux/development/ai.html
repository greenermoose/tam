Tamlinux uses AI assistants under human direction and records tool, model and verification in public provenance files.

How does Tamlinux treat AI assistance?

Tamlinux uses AI tools as assistants during development while keeping human judgment in charge of every design decision, release stage, and public record.

Why it matters

AI tools can speed up drafting, research, and testing, but recording clear public provenance ensures readers and contributors always know what was assisted and who verified the result.

How to think about it

Think of an AI assistant like a research clerk in an engineering office: the clerk can draft notes and check references quickly, while the engineer remains responsible for reviewing, signing off, and deciding what ships.

If it goes wrong

If an upstream project does not accept AI-assisted work, publish a fix on a public fork rather than submitting it upstream.

Check yourself

Does a model name establish that review happened?

No. Review and verification are separate recorded evidence.

Provenance boundaries

Public records carry tool identity, model identity, guiding prompts and commit trailers. Private session identifiers and transcript locations stay in the private ledger. Older records are read-only history; each new session writes one work record and revises the handoff file. Upstream engagement follows the receiving project's policy.
