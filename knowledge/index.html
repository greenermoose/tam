Using this guide

In the pager, use j and k (or the up and down arrow keys) to scroll the screen a line at a time, Space or PgDn and b or PgUp to scroll a whole screen, g or Home and G or End to jump to the top or bottom, Tab or Shift-Tab to select the next or previous link, Enter or the right arrow to follow it, u or the left arrow to go back to the previous page, ` (backtick) to return to this index, and q or Esc to quit. When the whole page already fits on the screen, the scrolling keys do nothing.

Every page below this index begins with a breadcrumb trail showing where it sits in the guide, from this index down to the page itself. The trail's earlier parts are links, so you can jump back up to a section or to this index.

When you redirect the output, for example tam show | head, the guide is printed as plain text with a numbered list of links.

Most pages also carry a beginner, intermediate, or expert depth. Read a page at one depth with tam show shell/pipelines?level=expert, or list the topics offering a depth with tam explain --level expert.
