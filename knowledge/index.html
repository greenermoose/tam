Welcome to the Tamlinux guide

Using this guide

In the pager, use j and k (or the arrow keys) to scroll the screen a line at a time, Space or PgDn and b or PgUp to scroll a whole screen, g or Home and G or End to jump to the top or bottom, Tab or Shift-Tab to select the next or previous link, Enter or the right arrow to follow it, u or the left arrow to go back to the previous page, and q or Esc to quit.

Every page begins with a breadcrumb trail showing where it sits in the guide, from this index down to the page itself. The trail's earlier parts are links, so you can jump back up to a section or to this index.

When you redirect the output, for example tam show | head, the guide is printed as plain text with a numbered list of links.
