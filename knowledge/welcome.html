Welcome to Tamlinux

Hello, and welcome to Tamlinux, a resource-efficient workstation environment for Linux. Tam exists to extend the useful life of Fred's computers. Maybe it could help yours work better, too!

Efficiency is not only a kindness to old machines. Eliminating code bloat, resident daemons and polling loops lets a brand-new computer run blazingly fast on less power, which is good for everyone and our planet.

Why "Tam"?

The tamarack (Larix laricina) is a hardy conifer that drops its needles every fall to grow fresh ones in spring. Tamlinux works the same way. It sheds old packages to make room for new ones. "Tam" can also mean "the absolute max," or "teaching and making." Pick your favorite.

Where things stand now

Tamlinux is a growing project; Fred develops in public and uses it every day on his own workstation. Today it runs on Arch Linux with Hyprland; a move to Sway on a smaller base system is planned. There is no installable image yet. This guide marks anything still being built as planned.

Navigating this guide

Use your keyboard, not your mouse. Down Arrow and Up Arrow move the attention line. Right Arrow follows a selected hyperlink; Left Arrow brings you back. Tab selects the next link, and Shift-Tab selects the previous link. The backtick (`) jumps to the Guide's index page. For help, press ?.

Press Esc to quit tam and return to your terminal. If you wander off, type tam welcome (or simply tam hi) in any terminal to come back here.

Navigating your desktop

The SUPER key (on many keyboards the one with the Windows logo on it) is your super power in Tamlinux. Here are three keybindings to try. (The desktop guide covers the rest.)

  • SUPER + SPACE to open the application launcher.
  • SUPER + RETURN to open a terminal window.
  • SUPER + K to list every keybinding.
