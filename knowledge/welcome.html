Welcome to Tamlinux

Hello!

Tamlinux empowers you to get the absolute maximum performance from your computer.

Ready to start using your Tamlinux workstation?

Or do you want a little more help? Here's how to get around this guide.

The title row is highlighted. Press Down Arrow to move to a row with an interactive element (such as a link), then Right Arrow to activate.

If you don't like the Down Arrow key, you can press d to move down and f to follow a link. Down Arrow and d do the same thing, i.e. they are "synonyms." Most keys have synonyms, so you can choose the most comfortable way for you to do things.

Key press navigation, instead of mouse clicks, provides faster, easier, more reliable, and safer control of your computer. Learn a few principles and practice to acquire muscle memory, then you'll fly through tasks on your computer without having to think about them.

How do you use arrow keys to read this guide?
  • Down move attention line down
  • Up move attention line up
  • Right take an action or follow a link
  • Left go back

How do you use left hand keys to read this guide?

Put your left hand on the middle row of your keyboard.

  • middle finger d move attention line down
  • ring finger s move attention line up
  • pointer finger f follow a link or take an action
  • pinky finger a go back

How do you use right hand keys to read this guide?

Put your right hand on the middle row of your keyboard.

  • middle finger k move attention line down
  • ring finger l move attention line up
  • pointer finger j take an action or follow a link
  • pinky finger ; go back

Message from Fred, the Developer

I hope Tamlinux helps you get more done in less time, keeping your computers running smoothly for decades and protecting your well-being.

Repetitive mouse movements and un-ergonomic keybindings cause errors and injuries. Tamlinux has ergonomic keybindings so you can rest your hands comfortably on your keyboard in a natural position and do work with less stress on your tendons.
