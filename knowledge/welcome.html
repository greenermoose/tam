Welcome to Tamlinux

Hello! Welcome to Tamlinux, a high-performance workstation environment that runs on Linux, designed for people who care for our Earth. Its small code size and super efficiency enable you and AI agents to get more work done using less power and with less wear and tear on components.

We hope that with Tamlinux, you can keep your computers running smoothly for decades, and put older hardware back into valuable use and not into landfills or incinerators. Too often, older devices stop working because the software they run is no longer supported by the manufacturer, not because the hardware is defective or inadequate.

In Tamlinux, the underlying operating system is called the rootstock, which the cultivar software depends on.

Navigating this guide

Use your keyboard, not your mouse.

KeyPurpose

For help, press h.

Press Esc to quit tam. When you wander off in a terminal shell, just type hi (an alias for tam hi) to come back here.
