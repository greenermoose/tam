Welcome to Tamlinux

Hello! Welcome to Tamlinux, a high-performance workstation environment that runs on Unix-like operating systems, designed for people who care for our Earth and need to get work done. Its small code size and super efficiency enable you and your AI agents to get more value per second and per watt spent on computing, with less wear and tear on battery cells, non-volatile memory chips, and other components.

As the developer of this software, thank you for trying it. I hope you can use Tamlinux to keep your computers running smoothly for decades, and keep older hardware out of landfills and incinerators. Too often, older devices stop working because the software they run becomes outdated, not because the hardware is defective or inadequate.

If you'd like to know more about me, the person developing Tamlinux, please read the Fred Horch page. I supervise AI agents to write code and this guide; it's slightly less weird to tell an AI agent to write about you in the third person than it is to do it yourself.

Navigating this guide

Use your keyboard, not your mouse.

KeyPurpose
Up Arrow, Down Arrowmove the attention line up or down
Right Arrowfollow a selected hyperlink
Left Arrowbring you back
] or Tabjump to and select the next hyperlink
[ or Shift+Tabjump to and select the previous link
Backtick (`)go to the Guide's index page

For help, press h.

Press Esc to quit tam. When you wander off in a terminal shell, just type hi (an alias for tam hi) to come back here.
