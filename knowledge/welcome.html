Navigating Around

Use your keyboard, not your mouse.

KeyPurpose
Up Arrow, Down Arrowmove the attention line up or down
Right Arrowfollow a selected hyperlink
Left Arrowbring you back
] or Tabjump to the next hyperlink
[ or Shift+Tabjump to the previous link
0 1 2 3show all, novice-only, learner-only, expert-only text
Backtick (`)go to the Guide's index page
hhelp
Esc or qquit

Even if you are reading this guide on a graphical desktop, clicking on a link with your mouse will not follow it. You'll always need to use your keyboard to visit other pages.

Wherever you wander off exploring in a terminal, just type hi to show this page of the Tamlinux guide.
