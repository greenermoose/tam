Open this Guide

From the desktop, open the Start Menu and press Return or Enter. From a text login, run tam-start to reach the same menu and choose whether to launch the desktop, open this Guide, or run a terminal tool.

Press Down Arrow to move the highlight over a link (or any other interactive element), then activate with Right Arrow.

Arrow keys not good for you? Press d to move down and f to follow a link. Down Arrow and d do the same thing. Most keys have synonyms; choose the most comfortable way for you to do things.

Key presses, instead of mouse clicks, provide faster, easier, more reliable, and safer control of your computer. Learn a few principles and practice to build muscle memory, then you'll fly through computer tasks without having to think about them.

How do you use arrow keys to read this guide?
  • Down move attention line down
  • Up move attention line up
  • Right take an action or follow a link
  • Left go back

How do you use left hand keys to read this guide?

Put your left hand on the middle row of your keyboard.

  • middle finger d move attention line down
  • ring finger s move attention line up
  • pointer finger f follow a link or take an action
  • pinky finger a go back

How do you use right hand keys to read this guide?

Put your right hand on the middle row of your keyboard.

  • middle finger k move attention line down
  • ring finger l move attention line up
  • pointer finger j take an action or follow a link
  • pinky finger ; go back

Message from Fred Horch, the Tamlinux developer

I hope Tamlinux helps you get more done in less time, keeping your computers running smoothly for decades and protecting your well-being.

Repetitive mouse movements and un-ergonomic keybindings cause errors and injuries. Tamlinux has ergonomic keybindings so you can rest your hands comfortably on your keyboard in a natural position and do work with less stress on your tendons.

As you use Tamlinux, enjoy a large selection (depending on local disk storage space) of essential knowledge from the whole of human civilization. It's always at your fingertips without needing any network connection or cloud account.

Thanks for using Tamlinux. Have fun, do good work, and be in touch!

- Fred
