Welcome to Tamlinux

Hello!

Tamlinux turns affordable computers into super-efficient personal and business workstations, so you get the absolute maximum performance from your hardware.

Rely on this guide to learn about your Tamlinux workstation and the technology it embodies. Navigate with key presses, not mouse clicks, for faster, easier, more reliable, and less injury-prone control.

Here's how to get around. The title row is highlighted. Press Down Arrow to move the highlight to the Answer line, then Right Arrow to show your choices.

Or use d (left hand) or k (right hand) to move down, then f or j.

How do you use arrow keys to read this guide?
  • Down move attention line down
  • Up move attention line up
  • Right take an action or follow a link
  • Left go back
How do you use left hand keys to read this guide?

Put your left hand on the middle row of your keyboard.

  • middle finger d move attention line down
  • ring finger s move attention line up
  • pointer finger f take an action or follow a link
  • pinky finger a go back
How do you use right hand keys to read this guide?

Put your right hand on the middle row of your keyboard.

  • middle finger k move attention line down
  • ring finger l move attention line up
  • pointer finger j take an action or follow a link
  • pinky finger ; go back

Message from Fred, the Developer

I hope Tamlinux empowers you to get more done in less time, keeping your computers running smoothly for decades and protecting your well-being.

Repetitive mouse movements and un-ergonomic keybindings are error-prone and injury-causing. Tamlinux assigns ergonomic keybindings so you can rest your hands comfortably on your keyboard in a natural position and do work with less risk of tendon injury.

As you explore Tamlinux, enjoy a large selection (depending on local disk storage space) of essential knowledge from the whole of human civilization. It's always at your fingertips without reliance on any network connection, search engine, or large language model operated by profit-seeking corporations.

Thanks for using Tamlinux. Have fun and be in touch!
