Welcome to Tamlinux

Tamlinux is an independent, continuing Linux workstation aimed at the best possible experience on any hardware. It is named for the tamarack larch: hardy, golden in October, and always improving.

Finding your way

Most things start from the keyboard. SUPER + RETURN opens a terminal, and SUPER + K shows the keybindings when you forget one. Explore more in the desktop guide.

Learning, maintaining, improving

  • Learn — read the shell guide, the pipeline guide, and the man guide.
  • Maintain — keep the system updated and monitor health, covered in installing and maintaining and resources and performance.
  • Improve — explore the shell, plugins and local AI tools that ship with the workstation.
