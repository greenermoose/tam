Welcome to Tamlinux

Hello, and welcome to Tamlinux, a high-performance workstation environment that runs on Linux. Tam exists to extend the useful life of Fred's computers. Maybe it can help yours work better, too!

Eliminating code bloat, resident daemons, and polling loops is not only a kindness to creaky old machines. It lets a brand-new computer run blazingly fast on less power, which is good for everyone and our planet.

Navigating this guide

Use your keyboard, not your mouse.

KeyPurpose
Up Arrow, Down Arrowmove the attention line up or down
Right Arrowfollow a selected hyperlink
Left Arrowbring you back
] or Tabjump to and select the next hyperlink
[ or Shift+Tabjump to and select the previous link
Backtick (`)go to the Guide's index page

For help, press h.

Press Esc to quit tam. When you wander off in a terminal shell, just type hi (an alias for tam hi) to come back here.

Navigating your desktop

The SUPER key (on many keyboards it has a Windows logo on it) is your super power in Tamlinux. Here are three keybindings to try.

  • SUPER + SPACE opens your application launcher.
  • SUPER + RETURN opens a terminal window.
  • SUPER + K lists every keybinding.

See the desktop guide for more.
