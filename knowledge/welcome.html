Welcome to Tamlinux

Hello!

If you teach and make things and want the absolute maximum performance from your computer, Tamlinux is for you.

This guide is keyboard controlled; navigate with key presses, not mouse clicks. With practice, you'll see why this is faster and easier.

Here's how to get around this guide. The title row is highlighted. Press Down Arrow to move the highlight to the Answer line, then Right Arrow to show the answer choices.

Or use d (left hand) or k (right hand) to move down, and g (for "go").
