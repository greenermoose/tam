Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with A / APNs (Apple Push Notification service)

APNs (Apple Push Notification service): Apple's service for sending small wake-up notifications to apps on Apple devices.

APNs is the service an app uses to wake an iPhone or iPad. The notification is usually a small alert, not the whole chat message.

The app receives a device token from the operating system and gives it to its provider. The provider sends a notice through APNs; the app then fetches the conversation itself.

Apple's documentation describes HTTP/2 token-based connections. A regular payload is limited to 4096 bytes; a `200` means APNs accepted the notice, and `410` means the token is no longer active. Those are transport responses, not app-level delivery proof.
