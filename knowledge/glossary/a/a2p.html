Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with A / A2P (Application-to-Person)

A2P (Application-to-Person): messages sent by a business or application to a person's phone number.

A2P messaging is when an organisation texts people, such as a bank sending a code or a clinic sending an appointment reminder.

The receiver sees a phone number or a sender name. Replies may be impossible if that visible sender is only a display label.

A2P traffic is often submitted through SMPP or a provider API. Registration, rate limits and reputation checks try to reduce abuse; the underlying SMS address fields are not cryptographic proof of who sent the message.
