Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with A / APN (Access Point Name)

APN (Access Point Name): the carrier setting that chooses which data network path a phone uses.

An APN is a data profile that tells a phone which carrier network path to use. Without it, a phone may have signal but no working data service.

Multimedia message retrieval often needs a carrier APN. That is why a text notice can arrive while the picture cannot be fetched.

An APN identifies a packet data network gateway, with authentication and addressing rules. Roaming may require a visited network’s APN even though the subscription belongs to a home network.
