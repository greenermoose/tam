Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with A / Availability

Availability describes whether a service is there to answer a request when someone needs it.

It is the difference between a shop being open when you arrive and finding a closed door.

Replication and failover improve availability. A system may still be available while returning stale data, so availability alone says nothing about correctness.

Availability is often stated as a percentage of successful requests over time. Network partitions force a trade-off with consistency; a repair after failure is not the same as being available throughout it.
