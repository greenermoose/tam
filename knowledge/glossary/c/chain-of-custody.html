Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with C / Chain of custody (documented custody transfers)

Chain of custody is the documented record of who holds material as it passes from one party to the next.

It is like parcel tracking for discarded electronics: each handover is written down so the owner of the material is known at every step.

Custody is documented from the generator through the transporter and recycler to final disposition. R2v3 and e-Stewards define where that chain starts and ends and require downstream tracking.

R2v3 tracks downstream material to final disposition or registration with SERI, while e-Stewards v4.1 defines its custody endpoints and applies the Basel Annex VII hazardous e-waste export ban as if ratified everywhere. Data security is part of the handover too: e-Stewards has required NAID AAA since 1 July 2022, and sanitisation practice is described in NIST SP 800-88.
