Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with C / Chroma subsampling

Storing color detail at lower resolution than brightness detail.

In 4:2:0, one pair of chroma samples ordinarily corresponds to a two-by-two group of luma samples.

4:4:4 retains full chroma resolution; 4:2:2 halves horizontal chroma resolution. Sample location and filters matter.
