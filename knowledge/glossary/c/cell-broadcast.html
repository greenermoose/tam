Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with C / Cell broadcast

Cell broadcast: a carrier service that sends one message to every phone in a defined area without addressing each subscriber.

Cell broadcast sends the same notice to every phone in an area. It is used for emergency alerts and other area-wide messages.

Unlike a text to your number, a broadcast does not need to know who you are. Phones in the area listen for the channel.

3GPP TS 23.041 defines cell broadcast and public warning systems. Pages are unacknowledged; a default-alphabet page carries 93 characters, and several pages can be concatenated.
