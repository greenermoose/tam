Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with C / Charged Device Model (CDM)

A test model for discharge from a charged electronic device to a conductor.

A chip can collect charge during handling and release it suddenly when it touches a conductor. CDM tests this kind of event.

CDM differs from HBM because the device itself is charged before discharge. This can happen during automated manufacturing.

CDM pulses are very fast and depend on the device package and test setup. A CDM rating is specific to the applicable test method and is not directly interchangeable with an HBM rating.
