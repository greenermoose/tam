Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with C / Consistency

Consistency means a database does not show a state that breaks its rules, even when several operations happen at once.

It is the promise that two readers looking at the same ledger at the same moment will not see contradictory balances.

In ACID, consistency is the rule-preserving part of a transaction. With replicas, consistency also describes how up to date the copies are.

Strong consistency is costly when nodes are far apart or failing. Eventual consistency admits temporary disagreement and is acceptable for some workloads but not all.
