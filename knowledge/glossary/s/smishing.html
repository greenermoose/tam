Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with S / Smishing (SMS phishing)

Smishing (SMS phishing): a deceptive text message that tries to make a reader reveal secrets or open a harmful link.

Smishing is a fake text that tries to trick someone. It may claim to be from a bank, a courier or a friend.

Warning signs include urgency, an unexpected link and a request for a one-time code. Check through the official app instead of replying.

Smishing exploits sender labels, link obfuscation and human trust rather than breaking message encryption. Filters use reputation and reports, but independent verification remains the safest habit.
