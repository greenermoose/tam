Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with S / Spam

Spam is unsolicited bulk email sent to many people who did not ask for it.

Spam is junk mail that arrives in an inbox instead of a paper letter box. It may advertise something, try to trick the reader, or simply fill the mailbox with unwanted messages.

Senders may buy lists, guess addresses or take over machines that send mail without their owners' knowledge. Receivers use authentication, reputation lists, content filters, rate limits and user reports. An allowlist accepts only approved senders, while a denylist blocks known bad ones.

There is no single protocol that defines spam; it is a property of consent and behaviour. SPF, DKIM and DMARC authenticate domains, not content, so a passing message can still be unwanted. Filtering combines signals and must manage false positives, user privacy and appeal paths.
