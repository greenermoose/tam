Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with S / SMSC (Short Message Service Center)

SMSC (Short Message Service Center): the system that stores and forwards short messages until they can be delivered.

An SMSC is the message centre behind SMS. It accepts a short text, keeps it, and tries to deliver it when the receiver can be reached.

Submission means the centre took the message. Delivery means the receiving network accepted it. Those are different events and can happen minutes or hours apart.

3GPP TS 23.040 describes the service centre and the SMS-IWMSC and SMS-GMSC gateways. The centre is addressed with an E.164 number, queries subscriber location through MAP procedures, and applies a validity period and retry policy.
