Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with S / SPF (Sender Policy Framework)

SPF is an email authentication method that publishes which servers are allowed to send mail for a domain.

The letters stand for Sender Policy Framework. A domain owner writes a public note in DNS saying which servers may send its mail. A receiving server can check that note before trusting the sender.

The record is a TXT record beginning v=spf1 at the domain used by the SMTP envelope sender or HELO identity. A receiver compares the connecting server's address with the record. SPF does not check the visible From: header.

RFC 7208 defines SPF. Forwarding and mailing lists can break SPF because they change the envelope sender; SRS rewrites it so the forwarder becomes the sender. DMARC adds alignment between the SPF-authenticated domain and the author domain. SPF alone is not an anti-spam verdict.
