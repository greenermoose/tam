Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with S / SIM (Subscriber Identity Module)

SIM (Subscriber Identity Module): the secure card or embedded module that holds a mobile subscription identity and its secret key.

A SIM is the small card that tells a mobile network which subscription a phone is using. An eSIM does the same job but is built into the phone.

Moving the SIM to another phone moves the subscription, not the phone’s hardware identity. The card holds identifiers and a secret the network can check without exposing that secret to the phone’s normal software.

3GPP TS 31.102 defines the USIM application on the UICC. It stores the IMSI, authentication data and ancillary service information. The long-lived subscriber key stays inside the secure element and is used in authentication and key agreement.
