Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with M / MTA-STS (SMTP MTA Strict Transport Security)

MTA-STS tells sending mail servers that a domain requires a valid TLS connection for relay.

The letters stand for SMTP MTA Strict Transport Security. A domain publishes a rule that says encrypted delivery is required, not merely preferred, so an attacker cannot force a downgrade to an unencrypted connection.

A DNS TXT record at _mta-sts.domain begins v=STSv1 and carries an id. The full policy is fetched from https://mta-sts.domain/.well-known/mta-sts.txt and lists modes, expected MX hosts and a maximum age.

RFC 8461 defines MTA-STS. Modes are enforce, testing and none; an enforced policy makes a sender defer delivery when TLS cannot be validated. Unlike DANE, MTA-STS does not depend on DNSSEC; it depends on HTTPS for the policy and DNS only to signal that a policy exists.
