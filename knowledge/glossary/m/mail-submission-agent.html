Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with M / MSA (Mail Submission Agent)

An MSA is the server that accepts a newly written message from a mail client and hands it into the mail system.

The letters stand for Mail Submission Agent. It is the receiving desk for mail you are sending, where the system checks that you are allowed to send and gets the message ready for its journey.

Submission is separate from relay. A client normally authenticates to the MSA on port 587 with STARTTLS, or on port 465 with implicit TLS. The MSA applies the hosting domain's policy before passing the message to an MTA.

RFC 6409 defines message submission as a distinct service with its own authorization and policy requirements. RFC 8314 prefers implicit TLS where available. Separating submission from relay lets operators require authentication from their own users while still accepting unauthenticated mail from other servers on port 25.
