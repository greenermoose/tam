Motion (vi)

A vi command that moves the cursor across text (such as w for next word or $ for end of line) and defines the span an operator acts on.

In vi, a motion is a single-key movement command—like jumping to the next word or the end of the line—that can be used alone to move around or paired with an action like delete.

Combining an operator (like d for delete) with a motion (like w for word or $ for end of line) forms composable commands such as dw and d$.

What does combining the operator d with the motion $ do in vi?

It deletes from the cursor to the end of the current line.

vi's command grammar separates motions (characterwise or linewise address calculations) from operators, allowing O(N+M) verbs and movements to compose into N×M editing actions.
