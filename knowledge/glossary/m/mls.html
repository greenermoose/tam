Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with M / MLS (Messaging Layer Security)

MLS (Messaging Layer Security): an IETF protocol for efficient end-to-end encryption of groups that change over time.

MLS is a way to encrypt group chats so that people can join and leave without giving everyone a new secret by hand.

When a member leaves, the group moves to a new key period so the former member cannot read future messages. Old messages remain a separate question.

RFC 9420 defines MLS with KeyPackages, Proposal and Commit messages, Welcome messages, epochs and ratchet trees. It assumes a trusted Authentication Service and a largely untrusted Delivery Service, and uses HPKE for key encapsulation.
