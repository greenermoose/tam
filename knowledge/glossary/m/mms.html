Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with M / MMS (Multimedia Messaging Service)

MMS (Multimedia Messaging Service): the carrier service that carries pictures, sound and longer text through a multimedia centre.

MMS is a text service that can carry a picture, a sound or a longer note. The media is usually kept at a service centre and fetched in a second step after a small notice arrives.

A phone sends the media to its multimedia centre. The centre stores it and sends a notice to the receiver. The receiver then fetches the content over a data connection. If that data path fails, the notice can arrive without the picture.

3GPP TS 23.140 defines MMS with MM1, MM4 and MM7 interfaces, WAP and HTTP transport, MIME content and SMIL presentation. The standard does not fix one numeric size cap; operators set their own limits and adaptation rules.
