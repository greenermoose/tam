Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with M / MMSC (Multimedia Messaging Service Center)

MMSC (Multimedia Messaging Service Center): the system that stores multimedia messages and manages their retrieval.

An MMSC is the centre behind multimedia messages. It stores the picture or sound and tells the receiver that content is waiting.

The receiver’s phone fetches the content from the centre over a data connection. The centre may adapt the content to suit the receiving device.

3GPP TS 23.140 defines the MMSC with MM1 for the user, MM4 for inter-centre forwarding and MM7 for value-added applications. Delivery and read reports are separate events from the initial notification.
