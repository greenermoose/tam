Display mirroring

Showing the exact same desktop picture simultaneously on two or more screens instead of extending across them.

Display mirroring copies the exact same picture onto two screens at once—useful when presenting on a projector so the audience sees what you see.

Mirroring differs from extending (where each monitor shows different windows). If two mirrored screens have different native resolutions, one may scale or letterbox to match.

When is display mirroring more useful than extending your desktop?

During a presentation or screen share on a projector where you want both your laptop screen and the projection to show identical content.

In Wayland compositors such as Hyprland, output mirroring binds a secondary output's scanout to copy the primary output's framebuffer (configured via mirror directives or tam-hyprland-monitor-internal-mirror).
