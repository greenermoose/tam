Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with D / Delivery receipt

Delivery receipt: a report about a message delivery attempt, distinct from proof that a person read the message.

A delivery receipt is a report that says something happened to a message. It is not the same as a reply or proof that someone read it.

A short message may be accepted by a centre and later reported as delivered to a network. A read report is a separate event from the receiving app.

SMS status reports are defined in 3GPP TS 23.040 but are optional in end-to-end deployments. App receipts and push-service responses describe different layers and should not be read as equivalent.
