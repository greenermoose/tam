Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with D / Data warehouse

A data warehouse collects data from many systems so analysts can ask questions across the whole history.

It is a library archive of past reports, arranged by subject rather than by the day each form arrived.

Warehouses favour read-heavy queries, star schemas and aggregations over fast single-row updates.

Warehouse designs separate fact tables from dimension tables and may denormalize for scan speed. Refresh windows, slowly changing dimensions and query cost shape the design.
