Font family

A named group of matching font styles (regular, bold, italic) that share the same visual design.

A font family is a complete set of matching letter styles—regular, bold, and italic—under one name, such as JetBrains Mono or Inter.

When you select a font family on Tamlinux (with tam-font-set or Fontconfig rules), applications automatically pick the regular, bold, or italic face from that family as needed.

What is the difference between a font family and a single font file?

A single font file usually holds one weight or style (such as Bold Italic), while a font family groups all related styles under one name.

Fontconfig resolves family names through alias, prefer, and accept rules into ordered fallback lists of OpenType/TrueType faces matched by weight, slant, width, and Unicode coverage.
