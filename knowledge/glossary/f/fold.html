Fold

An interactive collapsible block in the tam pager used for quiz answers, hints, solutions, and first-time notes.

A fold is a hidden box inside a Guide page that stays closed until you choose to open it—letting you think of your own answer to a question before revealing the explanation.

In the tam pager, folds appear as [+] links; pressing Enter, Right Arrow, or r opens the fold, and pressing * toggles all folds on the page.

Why are quiz answers folded by default in the tam pager?

Hiding the answer encourages retrieval practice—recalling or deciding your own answer before checking it strengthens learning.

Folds are assigned sequential tam:fold:<id> URIs in src/fold.c prior to level filtering; in --pipe mode, folded bodies are collected under an Answers heading at the end of the enclosing section.
