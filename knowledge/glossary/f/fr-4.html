Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with F / FR-4 (Flame Retardant 4 laminate)

FR-4 is the common woven glass fibre epoxy, copper-clad laminate used as the rigid substrate of a printed circuit board.

The letters FR stand for Flame Retardant. If you look at a typical computer motherboard, the stiff board underneath the chips is usually FR-4.

FR-4 laminates are specified in IPC-4101. Brominated epoxy formulations exist: reported example bromine contents are around 20 wt% for TBBPA-type systems and about 50 wt% for high-brominated resin. Those figures describe particular formulations and are not universal.

Because the bromine content varies by resin system, the FR-4 designation alone is not a material declaration; a data sheet is needed for a given board. Where a brominated resin is used, it connects the substrate to the brominated flame retardant question and to RoHS assessment of homogeneous materials. Components are then attached to the finished PCB by surface-mount technology.
