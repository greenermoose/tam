FCM (Firebase Cloud Messaging): Google's service for delivering small notifications and data messages to Android and other clients.

FCM is the service many Android apps use to wake up and show a notification. It carries a small message from the app provider to the device.

A chat app can send a notification payload, a data payload, or both. The app usually fetches the encrypted conversation after the wake-up.

Firebase documentation describes the v1 API, notification and data message types, topic targeting and a 4096-byte payload limit for instant messaging data. Delivery analytics report transport outcomes, not cryptographic end-to-end delivery.
