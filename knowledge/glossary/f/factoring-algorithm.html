Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with F / Factoring algorithm

An algorithm that finds integer factors of a number, especially the prime factors of a composite integer.

It breaks a number into numbers that multiply together to make it. For example, 15 factors into 3 and 5.

Small numbers can be factored by trial division, but large integers need better methods such as Pollard's rho, elliptic curve method, quadratic sieve, or the general number field sieve.

General integer factorization has subexponential classical algorithms (GNFS) but no known polynomial-time classical algorithm; Shor's algorithm gives polynomial-time factoring on a fault-tolerant quantum computer, which is why RSA migration planning matters.
