Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with O / OTT (Over-The-Top)

OTT (Over-The-Top): a service delivered over the public internet rather than operated as part of the carrier's own messaging service.

OTT means a service runs on top of the network. A chat app that uses the internet is over the top, even when it is used on a mobile phone.

That app can add features quickly, but its provider controls the account, the conversation store and the delivery records. The carrier mainly supplies connectivity.

An OTT messaging service typically uses TLS to its provider, push notifications from the device OS and application-level state for receipts and sync. Those pieces are distinct from SMS store-and-forward and from IMS-based RCS.
