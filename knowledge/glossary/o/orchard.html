Orchard

A group of computers running Tamlinux together, such as a workstation and laptops managed as a fleet.

An orchard is a collection of trees tended together. In Tamlinux terminology, an orchard refers to multiple computers—such as a main desk machine and travel laptops—kept in sync on the same Tamlinux release.

Managing an orchard lets you test a new Tamlinux cultivar on a clean or secondary host before promoting the same pinned source revisions to your daily workstation.

What is the practical advantage of testing a cultivar on a second machine in an orchard?

You can verify clean installation, upgrades, and rollback on a non-critical host before touching your primary daily workstation.

In multi-host Tamlinux rollouts, each host builds and records the exact locked Git revisions and manifest digest independently while keeping host-local hardware and network configuration outside public repositories.
