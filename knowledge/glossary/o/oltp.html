Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with O / OLTP

OLTP describes the many small operations an application performs while people use it.

It is placing one order, changing one address or checking one account balance.

OLTP workloads need fast indexed point lookups and reliable transactions.

OLTP systems are usually normalized and write-heavy. Long analytical scans can disturb latency, so reporting is often moved to a replica or warehouse.
