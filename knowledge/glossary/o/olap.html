Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with O / OLAP

OLAP describes queries that scan and summarize lots of data to answer a business question.

It is asking "which region grew fastest last year?" rather than "what is this one order?"

OLAP queries group, filter and aggregate. They favour columnar storage, compression and parallel scans.

OLAP differs from OLTP in workload, not necessarily in product: the same engine can serve both, but mixed workloads often need isolation.
