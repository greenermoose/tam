Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with U / UFW (Uncomplicated Firewall)

A simplified command-line frontend for managing Linux packet-filtering rules, historically used on many desktops before direct nftables policies.

UFW is a beginner-friendly tool for turning a Linux firewall on and allowing or blocking network ports without writing low-level firewall syntax.

Tamlinux is transitioning hosts from transitional UFW configurations to a single owned nftables policy in /etc/nftables.conf so there is one unified firewall layer.

Why is Tamlinux moving from UFW to direct nftables policies?

Using one owned /etc/nftables.conf policy avoids layered firewall frontends and makes host rules explicit, auditable, and reproducible.

UFW wraps iptables/nftables backends with rule files under /etc/ufw/; on Tamlinux 0.6.x hosts, nftables.service with /etc/nftables.conf supersedes UFW once cutover is verified.
