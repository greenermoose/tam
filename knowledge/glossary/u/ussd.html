Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with U / USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data)

USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data): a session-based carrier menu service reached by dialling a short code.

USSD is the menu system reached by codes such as a short number with hashes. It opens a live session on the phone screen.

It can check an account balance or change a carrier setting. It is not a store-and-forward text mailbox.

USSD is a GSM supplementary service with a real-time session and short response strings. Unlike SMS, it does not rely on a message centre queue; like many carrier menus, it should not be treated as a secure channel for secrets.
