Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with U / Ultra-high definition (UHD)

Video formats with more picture samples than common HD formats.

A common UHD format has 3840 by 2160 pixels.

UHD resolution does not automatically indicate HDR, a particular color gamut or frame rate.
