Greenhouse

In the Tamlinux plant analogy, the greenhouse means the physical computer and its resources.

The botanical name for the physical computer and its resources.

The hardware envelope that supplies compute, memory, storage, displays and power.

A conceptual resource boundary, not a separate package or a guarantee that particular hardware has been validated.
