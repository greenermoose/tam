yuv420p

An FFmpeg pixel format with separate 8-bit luma and color planes.

It stores one Y sample per pixel and one Cb and Cr sample per two-by-two group.

Even-sized unpadded images use 12 bits per pixel before compression; the three planes are Y, Cb and Cr.

YUV; all glossary terms.
