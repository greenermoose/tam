YUYV

A packed 4:2:2 video pixel layout.

A packed 4:2:2 video pixel layout.

Pairs of pixels share a pair of color samples while keeping separate brightness samples.

The repeating 8-bit byte order is Y0, Cb, Y1, Cr; the format is also called YUY2 in some systems.
