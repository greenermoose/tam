Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with K / KWin

KWin is the window manager and compositor used by KDE Plasma.

KWin places and decorates windows and combines them for display. On a Wayland session it also serves as the display server.

Plasma Shell supplies panels, widgets and notifications, while KWin handles surfaces, focus and effects. KDE Plasma has historically supported both X11 and Wayland sessions, with KWin adapting its role to each.

KWin implements window management policy and the Wayland compositor role, including buffer presentation, input devices and output configuration. It also supports scripting and effects, which can change visible window behaviour without changing the applications themselves.
