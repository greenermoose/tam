Natural scrolling

A touchpad or mouse-wheel direction setting where moving fingers downward pushes page content downward, like a touchscreen.

Natural scrolling makes a laptop touchpad behave like a phone screen: sliding two fingers up pushes the page up so you see further down, instead of moving the scrollbar thumb.

Natural scrolling is a per-device libinput preference configured in Hyprland's input settings; some users prefer it on touchpads while keeping traditional scrolling on an external mouse wheel.

How does natural scrolling differ from traditional scrolling?

In natural scrolling, content moves in the same direction as your fingers; in traditional scrolling, the scrollbar moves in the direction of your fingers.

Controlled by libinput's natural_scroll boolean property, exposed separately for pointer and touchpad blocks in Hyprland's Lua input configuration.
