Night light

A display color-temperature filter that reduces blue light in the evening to make the screen warmer and easier on the eyes.

Night light gently shifts your screen toward warm amber colors at night without changing any actual files or screenshots.

On Tamlinux, night light is toggled with Meta + Ctrl + J or tam-toggle-nightlight and can be inspected read-only with tam-toggle-nightlight --status.

Does turning on night light alter the colors saved inside a screenshot or photo file?

No. Night light adjusts the display output color temperature on the way to the monitor; underlying image files remain unchanged.

Implemented on Wayland via gamma/color-temperature protocols (such as hyprsunset using zwlr_gamma_control_manager_v1), applying a LUT shift to the CRTC gamma ramp without mutating client buffers.
