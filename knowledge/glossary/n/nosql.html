Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with N / NoSQL

NoSQL is an umbrella term for databases that store data in forms other than fixed relational tables.

Where a relational database uses labelled tables, NoSQL may use documents, key-value pairs, wide columns or graphs.

NoSQL stores often trade joins and strict schemas for flexible records, horizontal scaling or particular access patterns.

The term covers very different systems; it does not mean "no SQL" in every case. Query languages, consistency models and index support differ widely, so compare stores by workload.
