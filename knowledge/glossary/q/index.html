Terms beginning with Q

Glossary definitions for technical terms and acronyms beginning with Q.

Explore

QAM (Quadrature Amplitude Modulation)QAM: Modulation encoding multiple bits per symbol by varying both carrier amplitude and phase.
QEMU (Quick Emulator)QEMU: Open-source machine emulator and virtualizer paired with KVM on Linux.
QFT (Quantum Fourier Transform)QFT: Quantum-circuit analogue of the discrete Fourier transform, core to Shor's algorithm.
QML (Qt Modeling Language)QML: Declarative UI language used by Qt 6 and Quickshell for Tamlinux desktop bars and plugins.
QNXQNX: Commercial POSIX-compliant real-time microkernel OS widely used in automotive and embedded systems.
QR code (Quick Response code)QR code: 2D matrix barcode with built-in Reed-Solomon error correction.
QuantumQuantum: Quantum describes the rule that some things in nature come in tiny fixed steps, not smooth continuous amounts.
QUICQUIC: Encrypted, multiplexed UDP transport protocol (RFC 9000) underlying HTTP/3.
QuickshellQuickshell: Qt6/QML Wayland shell toolkit used by Tamlinux for its top bar and desktop plugins.
QWERTYQWERTY: Standard Latin keyboard layout named after its top-left six letter keys.
