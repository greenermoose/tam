Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with Q / Quarantine

Quarantine holds a suspicious message for review instead of placing it straight in the inbox.

A quarantine is a holding area for mail that the system is not sure about. You can look there and decide whether a message is wanted, rather than having it mixed into ordinary mail or deleted outright.

A quarantine may be a separate folder, a digest of held messages or a service-side store. Users need a way to release wanted mail and report unwanted mail; administrators need retention limits and clear ownership.

Quarantines reduce false-positive harm but create privacy and storage obligations. Held messages may contain sensitive content, so access controls, short retention and audit trails matter. A quarantine is a filtering outcome, not a mail protocol.
