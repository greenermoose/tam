IETF (Internet Engineering Task Force)

Open international standards organization that develops and maintains core internet protocols.

The IETF is an open community of engineers, researchers, and network operators who write the shared technical rules—such as IP, TCP, DNS, TLS, and HTTP—that let computers everywhere talk to each other.

Anyone can join IETF mailing lists and working groups without membership fees. Proposals start as Internet-Drafts, undergo open technical review and rough consensus, and are published as RFCs. See The IETF and how RFCs work.

Standards-developing body organized into technical Areas overseen by the IESG under the IETF Administration LLC and IAB, publishing Standards Track, BCP, Informational, and Experimental documents in the RFC series (BCP 9 / RFC 2026 and RFC 9280). See The IETF and how RFCs work.
