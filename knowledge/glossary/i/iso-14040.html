Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with I / ISO 14040 (Life Cycle Assessment Principles and Framework)

ISO 14040 is the international standard that sets the principles and framework for life cycle assessment, usually shortened to LCA.

An LCA adds up environmental impacts from raw materials through use and disposal. ISO 14040 gives that study a common shape so results can be read and questioned in the same way.

With ISO 14044, the standard defines four phases: goal and scope, life cycle inventory, life cycle impact assessment and interpretation. The published amendment is ISO 14040 Amd 1:2020. ISO 14040 is methodology, not a label that a product can carry.

The goal-and-scope phase fixes the functional unit and system boundary, and those choices constrain every later phase. That is why conclusions from different LCAs can differ even for similar products. The framework underlies work such as a product carbon footprint, but it does not itself certify a result.
