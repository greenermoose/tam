Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with I / Instant messaging

Instant messaging: real-time text chat between people who are connected through a messaging service.

Instant messaging is chat that appears while people are using the service. It feels immediate, even though a provider may still store and forward each message.

Read receipts, typing indicators and presence are common extras. Different services implement them differently.

Instant messaging may be centralised, as with an app provider, or federated, as with XMPP and Matrix. Federation decides who runs servers; encryption decides who can read content.
