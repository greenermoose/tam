I420

A three-plane 8-bit 4:2:0 video layout.

A three-plane 8-bit 4:2:0 video layout.

I420 stores luma first, followed by separate Cb and Cr planes.

Plane stride and padding can differ from the visible image width; each chroma plane has half the luma width and height for even dimensions.
