Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with I / ISO 14044 (Life Cycle Assessment Requirements and Guidelines)

ISO 14044 is the international standard that sets requirements and guidelines for carrying out a life cycle assessment.

If an LCA is the study, this standard and ISO 14040 are the rulebook. They describe the steps a study should follow and how to report what it found.

Together the standards set out four phases: goal and scope, inventory, impact assessment and interpretation. ISO 14044 has two published amendments, Amd 1:2017 and Amd 2:2020. Like ISO 14040, it is methodology and not a product label.

The requirements govern how inventory data and impact results are handled and interpreted, which matters when an LCA supports a claim about a product carbon footprint or a comparison between products. Results depend on the chosen boundary and data quality, so the method is not a pass-or-fail certification and is separate from a management-system standard such as ISO 14001.
