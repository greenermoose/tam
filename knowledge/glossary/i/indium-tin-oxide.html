Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with I / Indium tin oxide (ITO transparent electrode)

Indium tin oxide, or ITO, is a transparent conducting film used where an electrode must let light through.

When you touch a screen and it responds, a see-through conductor is part of how the touch is detected. ITO is the common material for that thin film.

ITO is used as electrodes in LCD and OLED displays and in touchscreens. It accounts for most global indium consumption.

Because most indium goes into ITO, display material is central to indium supply and therefore to interest in recovering metals from discarded screens. That is a different question from the conflict minerals due-diligence list, which covers tin, tantalum, tungsten and gold rather than indium. ITO layers are part of the display stack described in boards, displays and assembly.
