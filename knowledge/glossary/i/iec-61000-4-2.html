Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with I / IEC 61000-4-2

IEC 61000-4-2 is a standard for testing equipment against electrostatic discharge.

A device can receive a static shock when a person touches it. This standard describes a repeatable way to check equipment against such shocks.

The test applies specified contact or air discharges to equipment. Results depend on the test level, setup, and performance criteria.

IEC 61000-4-2 addresses system-level electromagnetic compatibility immunity. Its test waveform and setup differ from device-level HBM and CDM tests, so their voltage ratings are not directly interchangeable.
