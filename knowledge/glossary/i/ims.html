Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with I / IMS (IP Multimedia Subsystem)

IMS (IP Multimedia Subsystem) is the carrier's internet-protocol service platform for sessions such as voice, video and RCS (Rich Communication Services).

IMS is the part of a mobile network that treats a call or a chat as an internet-style service. I stands for Internet, M for Multimedia and S for Subsystem. A carrier uses it to offer services beyond a plain short message.

RCS and IMS uses this platform for chat, group conversations, file transfer and richer calling. The phone registers with the IMS core, and the network applies its subscriber and policy rules before sessions start. An internet messaging app can look similar on screen but normally uses a public internet provider instead of the carrier's IMS core.

3GPP TS 23.228 defines the stage-two IMS architecture: a home subscriber server, call session control functions, media resources and service platforms connected through standard interfaces. SIP and SDP set up and describe sessions under 3GPP TS 24.229. IMS is a control and service framework, not a promise of end-to-end content encryption; GSMA RCS profiles define which features interoperate across operators.
