Terms beginning with a number

Glossary definitions for technical terms and acronyms beginning with a number.

Explore

8-bitA width of eight bits, giving 256 patterns and the basis of a byte.
32-bitA width of 32 bits, giving four billion unsigned values and a 4 GiB pointer address space.
64-bitA width of 64 bits, giving very large integers and the ABI used by most modern desktop programs.
