Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with E / E.164 (International Public Telecommunication Numbering Plan)

E.164 (International Public Telecommunication Numbering Plan): the international numbering plan used for public phone numbers.

E.164 is the plan behind international phone numbers. A number written with a plus sign and a country code follows this plan.

A service that stores contacts usually normalises numbers to E.164 form so that the same person is recognised from different local formats.

ITU-T E.164 defines the structure and a maximum of 15 digits for an international number. It assigns number space; it does not authenticate the holder of a number.
