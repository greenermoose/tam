Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with E / Electronic waste (discarded electrical and electronic equipment)

Electronic waste, often shortened to e-waste, is discarded electrical and electronic equipment.

Old computers, phones and appliances become e-waste when their owners throw them away. They contain both useful materials and hazardous parts, which is why collection and treatment are handled separately from ordinary rubbish.

The Global E-waste Monitor 2024 reported 62 million tonnes of e-waste in 2022, with only 22.3% documented as formally collected and recycled. The WEEE directive is the EU framework for producer duties on collection and treatment.

The 22.3% figure covers what is documented in formal collection and recycling; the remainder is not accounted for in those channels, which is different from saying every untracked device is dumped. Once collected, depollution removes hazardous parts before metals go to hydrometallurgical or pyrometallurgical recovery, and recyclers may be certified under R2v3 or e-Stewards.
