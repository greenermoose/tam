Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with E / e-Stewards (Certified Electronics Recycler Standard)

e-Stewards is a certification standard for electronics recyclers run by the Basel Action Network.

A certified recycler is checked on where hazardous e-waste goes and on how it protects the data on devices it receives. The certification applies to recycling facilities, not to products.

Version 4.1 applies the Basel Annex VII ban on hazardous e-waste exports as if it had been ratified everywhere, and it defines the start and end of the chain of custody. NAID AAA data security has been required since 1 July 2022.

Treating the Annex VII ban as if ratified everywhere closes a route for hazardous waste to leave a country through a place without the same rule. The defined custody endpoints make the standard's tracking test explicit. R2v3 also certifies recyclers and tracks downstream material, but its chain-of-custody and export provisions are not the same as these e-Stewards requirements.
