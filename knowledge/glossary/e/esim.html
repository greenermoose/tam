Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with E / eSIM (embedded Subscriber Identity Module)

eSIM (embedded Subscriber Identity Module): a subscriber identity module built into a device and provisioned remotely instead of swapped as a card.

An eSIM is a subscriber identity built into a phone. It can be loaded with a mobile plan without putting a plastic card into the device.

To change plans, a phone downloads a profile from the carrier. The subscription still depends on the same kind of secret and account record as a removable SIM.

GSMA SGP.21 defines the architecture and SGP.22 the consumer remote provisioning procedures; SGP.31 and SGP.32 cover IoT eSIM. Remote provisioning does not remove the need for strong proof of identity when a profile is transferred.
