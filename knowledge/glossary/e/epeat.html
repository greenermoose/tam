Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with E / EPEAT (Electronic Product Environmental Assessment Tool)

EPEAT (Electronic Product Environmental Assessment Tool) is a global ecolabel for electronics, run by the Global Electronics Council.

The label grades a registered product as Bronze, Silver or Gold. Bronze means it meets the required criteria; the higher tiers mean it also meets a share of optional criteria.

EPEAT is based on IEEE 1680.1-2018 and its amendment IEEE 1680.1a-2020, and it is an ISO 14024 Type 1 ecolabel. Bronze requires all mandatory criteria, Silver adds at least 50% of the optional criteria, and Gold adds at least 75%. A rating is product and country specific: the same model is not automatically registered at the same tier in every market.

Tier membership therefore needs both the exact product registration and the country, not just a model name. Because the criteria are the IEEE 1680.1 family, EPEAT is a multi-criteria product label rather than an energy specification such as ENERGY STAR or a management-system standard such as ISO 14001. EU Ecodesign similarly addresses product design but through regulation.
