Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with E / EU Ecodesign (Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation)

EU Ecodesign is the European framework for setting environmental design requirements on products sold in the EU, including computers, servers and displays.

Instead of asking buyers to judge a product, the rules set minimum design and energy behaviour that covered products must meet. Related energy labels give shoppers a visible comparison, and repair requirements are another part of the same policy area.

The framework was Directive 2009/125/EC and is now the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR), Regulation (EU) 2024/1781. Electronic displays are covered by Regulation (EU) 2019/2021, which includes the Energy Efficiency Index, standby and network-standby rules, and repairability requirements; the display energy label is Regulation (EU) 2019/2013. Computers are covered by Regulation (EU) No 617/2013, and servers and data storage by Regulation (EU) 2019/424.

The product-specific regulations matter more than the framework title when checking a device. Regulation (EU) 2019/2022 is about dishwashers, not computers, even though its number looks close to the display rules. Repairability requirements connect to the right to repair, while end-of-life duties sit under WEEE. Voluntary labels such as ENERGY STAR and EPEAT are separate schemes.
