Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with W / WOFF2 (Web Open Font Format 2)

WOFF2 is a compressed web font format that uses Brotli and specialized transforms for font tables.

WOFF2 makes font downloads even smaller than WOFF. It knows how fonts are built, so it can store repeated glyph outlines and table data more cleverly before applying strong compression. A browser unpacks it just as it unpacks WOFF.

WOFF2 typically yields files around a quarter smaller than WOFF 1.0. It supports TrueType, CFF and variable fonts, and modern browsers prefer it, often with WOFF as a fallback for older ones. The extra compression matters most for large CJK fonts and emoji sets.

The format uses Brotli compression plus a transform directory: glyf and loca may be recoded with split, null or high-order-byte transforms; hmtx may use a transformed table; and known tables such as cmap, name and post can be reconstructed from predefined formats. A WOFF2 decoder reverses these transforms to rebuild a valid SFNT. Metadata and table checksums are carried in the header.
