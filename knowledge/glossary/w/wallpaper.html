Wallpaper

The background image or solid color displayed behind open windows on a graphical desktop.

Wallpaper is the picture or calm color shown behind your windows on the desktop.

On Tamlinux, backgrounds are coordinated with desktop themes and can be switched from the Start menu Settings section or with tam-theme-bg-next and tam-theme-bg-switcher.

How do you change or cycle your desktop wallpaper on Tamlinux?

Open the Start menu's Background picker under Settings, or run tam-theme-bg-next / tam-theme-bg-switcher.

Wayland wallpaper daemons bind the zwlr_layer_shell_v1 background layer per output, rendering scaled buffers beneath tiled and floating compositor windows.
