Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with W / Window

A window is a rectangular area of the screen that shows one program's content and receives input for it.

Windows can overlap. You move one forward, resize it or put it aside while other windows stay open. The frame and title bar are not the whole window: the content inside belongs to the application.

On X11, a window is a server resource that clients draw into and a window manager places. On Wayland, an application's surface gains a window role through xdg-shell. Many toolkits draw their own title bars, a practice called client-side decoration.

A toplevel window participates in focus, stacking and event delivery. Its visible frame may come from a window manager or from the application's toolkit. Popups, tooltips and child surfaces use related but distinct roles, especially in Wayland's xdg-shell protocol.
