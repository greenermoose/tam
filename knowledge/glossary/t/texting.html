Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with T / Texting

Texting: sending short written messages between phones or messaging apps, usually through a store-and-forward service.

Texting is writing a short message and sending it to someone else. The receiver can read it later, because a service keeps it in between.

A text may use a carrier short message service, a multimedia service, rich chat or an internet app. The conversation view can look the same for all of them.

The term names a user activity, not one protocol. Each service has its own addressing, store, receipt semantics and privacy properties.
