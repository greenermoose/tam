Tap-to-click

A touchpad setting that treats a light finger tap as a mouse click without physically pressing the pad down.

Tap-to-click lets you click buttons by lightly tapping a laptop touchpad with your fingertip instead of pressing the pad until it clicks mechanically.

Multi-finger taps often map to right-click (two fingers) and middle-click (three fingers). If accidental taps happen while typing, you can disable tap-to-click or disable the touchpad while typing.

What is the trade-off of enabling tap-to-click on a laptop?

It makes clicking quieter and lighter, but increases the chance of accidental clicks if your palm brushes the touchpad.

Configured via libinput's tap-to-click option (LIBINPUT_CONFIG_TAP_ENABLED), with configurable button mapping (LRM vs LMR) and disable-while-typing (DWT) pairing.
