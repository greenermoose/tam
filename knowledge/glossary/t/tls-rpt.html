Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with T / TLS-RPT (SMTP TLS Reporting)

TLS-RPT tells sending servers where to report failures when they cannot make a valid TLS connection.

The letters stand for SMTP TLS Reporting. It is a way for a domain to ask other mail servers to send a summary when encrypted delivery runs into trouble.

The DNS TXT record lives at _smtp._tls.domain and begins v=TLSRPTv1. Its rua tag names the report destination. Reports often cover failures related to MTA-STS or DANE.

RFC 8460 defines SMTP TLS reporting. Reports are operational telemetry, not authentication of message content, and they can reveal infrastructure details. Report destinations outside the domain need authorization records, and retention should be short.
