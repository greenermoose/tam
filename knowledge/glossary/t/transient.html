Transient

An electrical transient is a short-lived change in voltage or current.

A sudden static shock produces a brief electrical disturbance. That disturbance is a transient.

Switching circuits, lightning, and electrostatic discharge can produce transients. Their duration and strength affect the protection needed.

A transient is described by its waveform, peak, rise time, and duration. Protection circuits must limit the voltage and handle the associated current and energy.
