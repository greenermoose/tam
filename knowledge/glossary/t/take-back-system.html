Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with T / Take-back system (producer collection of used products)

A take-back system is an arrangement in which a producer or retailer collects used products from customers.

Instead of putting an old device in the bin, you return it to the shop or the maker. That return route is the take-back system.

Take-back is often funded through extended producer responsibility, where the producer carries the cost of what happens after use. The UNEP take-back manual covers collection, transport and financing together; WEEE sets the EU producer duties in this area.

Collection alone does not close the loop: material must also be transported and financed onward, then handled through depollution and recovery with a documented chain of custody. Recyclers may be certified under R2v3 or e-Stewards.
