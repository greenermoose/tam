Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with T / TCO Certified (IT product certification)

TCO Certified is a product certification from TCO Development for IT products.

The mark covers several kinds of environmental and working-condition criteria, so one certification says more than a power figure alone. It applies to products, not to a piece of software.

The active criteria generation is generation 10. Its criteria cover climate, substances, circularity, responsible minerals, supply-chain social conditions and ergonomics. Generation 11 is a draft, not the current certification basis.

The responsible-minerals criteria sit alongside disclosure regimes such as conflict minerals reporting, while substance and circularity concerns overlap with RoHS and EPEAT. Because TCO Certified is a product certification, it is not a software standard and does not replace an environmental management system such as ISO 14001.
