H.264 (AVC)

A widely used video compression standard.

A widely used video compression standard.

An encoder makes a compressed H.264 stream; a decoder reconstructs pictures from it.

H.264 is also called AVC. Profiles and levels constrain supported coding features; pixel layout is a separate choice.
