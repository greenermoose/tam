Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with H / HSS (Home Subscriber Server)

HSS (Home Subscriber Server): the LTE and IMS database that holds subscriber profiles and authentication data.

An HSS is the subscriber database used by 4G LTE and IMS networks. It holds the account information needed when a phone joins the network or starts an IMS service such as RCS.

When a phone registers for rich chat, the IMS side uses the HSS to check which services that subscriber may use.

HSS combines the earlier HLR role with IMS subscription data and authentication vectors. In 5G the equivalent subscriber data function is UDM; 3GPP TS 23.501 describes the 5G architecture.
