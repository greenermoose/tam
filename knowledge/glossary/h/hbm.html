Human Body Model (HBM)

A standard test model for electrostatic discharge from a charged person to a device.

A person can collect static charge and transfer it to a chip on contact. HBM represents that event in a repeatable laboratory test.

The test applies a specified discharge between device terminals; its voltage rating describes performance under this model, not immunity to every real discharge.

The conventional HBM network uses a 100 pF capacitor and 1.5 kΩ series resistance. JEDEC and ANSI/ESDA standards specify test procedures and classifications.
