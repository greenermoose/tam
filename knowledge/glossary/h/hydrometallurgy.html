Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with H / Hydrometallurgy (metal recovery with solutions)

Hydrometallurgy recovers metals using aqueous solutions rather than furnaces.

Think of dissolving a metal out of a mixture and then collecting it again. That is the basic idea behind recovering valuable metal from discarded electronics.

Leaching moves metal into solution, and reductive recovery brings it back out as metal. The route is used for gold, silver, platinum and other metals recovered from e-waste.

The solution-based route is the contrast with pyrometallurgy, which uses heat and smelting, energy-intensive furnaces and off-gas controls. Either route starts with material that has been through depollution, and the material's origin is traced through chain of custody.
