Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with H / HLR (Home Location Register)

HLR (Home Location Register): the home network database that holds a subscriber's permanent record and current serving information.

An HLR is the main subscriber record kept by the home network. It says which account a subscription belongs to and where the phone is currently registered.

When a person uses another carrier’s network, that carrier asks the HLR for permission and records the phone in its visitor register.

3GPP TS 23.003 and TS 29.002 describe the HLR and its MAP interfaces. HSS in LTE/IMS and UDM in 5G evolved from this subscriber-record role.
