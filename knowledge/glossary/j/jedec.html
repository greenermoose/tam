Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with J / JEDEC

JEDEC is an organization that develops standards for semiconductor devices.

Manufacturers use common specifications so electronic parts can be designed and tested consistently. JEDEC publishes many such specifications.

Its standards cover semiconductor memory, device packages, and reliability tests, including electrostatic discharge tests.

JEDEC publishes device-level ESD test methods and classifications alongside other semiconductor standards. A device’s rating must be interpreted with its named test method.
