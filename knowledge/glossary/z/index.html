Terms beginning with Z

Glossary definitions for technical terms and acronyms beginning with Z.

Explore

Z80 (Zilog Z80)Z80: Classic 1976 8-bit microprocessor widely used in early personal computers, CP/M, and embedded systems.
ZFS (Zettabyte File System)ZFS: Copy-on-write transactional filesystem and volume manager with end-to-end data checksums.
ZRAM (Z in UNIX means compressed)ZRAM: A compressed RAM disk used as swap or temporary storage.
