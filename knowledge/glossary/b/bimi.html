Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with B / BIMI (Brand Indicators for Message Identification)

BIMI lets a mailbox provider show a brand's logo next to messages that meet its authentication rules.

The letters stand for Brand Indicators for Message Identification. A company publishes a logo and proves it owns the brand; supporting mail apps may then display that logo beside messages from the company.

The DNS TXT record lives at default._bimi.domain and begins v=BIMI1. It points to an SVG logo and often to a verified mark certificate. BIMI guidance expects aligned SPF, DKIM and DMARC at enforcement.

BIMI is a BIMI Group industry specification, not an IETF standard. Display is always mailbox-provider policy: a passing record does not force a client to show the logo. The value is a visual trust cue, and it should not be read as proof that a particular message is safe.
