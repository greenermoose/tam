Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with B / Blue Angel (Blauer Engel)

Blue Angel, or Blauer Engel in German, is Germany's federal ecolabel for products and services that meet published environmental criteria.

The label is a mark you can look up rather than a general claim by a seller. It covers both physical products and, in a narrow case, a defined kind of desktop operating software.

The label is owned by the German federal environment ministry, BMUV; criteria are drawn up by the UBA, decisions are made by the Environmental Label Jury, and the award is made by RAL. DE-UZ 215 covers defined desktop operating software in version 6 of June 2024. DE-UZ 78 covers computers, keyboards and mice in July 2024, and DE-UZ 213 covers servers and data storage in 2025.

The software criteria are limited to defined desktop operating software; they do not cover arbitrary apps. That makes the exact product scope part of any claim, just as with ENERGY STAR, where software alone cannot be certified even though a custom image can be part of a computer certification. Blue Angel is an ecolabel, unlike the management-system standard ISO 14001.
