Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with B / Bluetooth

A short-range wireless standard for connecting nearby devices such as headphones, keyboards, mice, and phones.

Bluetooth lets devices talk to each other over radio waves without a cable. You usually pair devices once, then they can reconnect when nearby.

Bluetooth includes Classic Bluetooth for continuous streams like audio and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) for low-power sensors and peripherals. Pairing creates shared keys so devices can recognize and connect securely.

Bluetooth operates in the 2.4 GHz ISM band with adaptive frequency hopping. Profiles define application behavior (A2DP, HID, GATT, etc.), while security modes and pairing methods (including LE Secure Connections) determine authentication and encryption properties. Debugging often involves controller state, rfkill, BlueZ, and profile/codec negotiation.
