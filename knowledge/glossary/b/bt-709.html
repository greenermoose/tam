BT.709

An ITU recommendation defining parameters for high-definition television.

An ITU recommendation defining parameters for high-definition television.

It specifies characteristics used to represent HDTV pictures and color.

Primaries, transfer characteristics and matrix coefficients are distinct signal parameters, even when grouped under one recommendation.

YUV; all glossary terms.
