Blue light

The short-wavelength, cool-toned part of visible white light emitted strongly by modern LED-backlit screens.

Blue light is the cool, bright-blue part of white light from a monitor. Reducing it in the evening makes the screen look warmer and less glaring in a dark room.

Night-light filters reduce the blue channel intensity of the display output (lowering color temperature in kelvin) without changing file data.

What happens to white light on screen when a filter reduces blue light?

The display shifts toward warmer yellow-amber tones (a lower kelvin color temperature).

Short-wavelength visible light (~450–490 nm) peaks in standard white-LED backlights; software color-temperature filters attenuate the blue gamma LUT curve to approximate a lower blackbody color temperature.
