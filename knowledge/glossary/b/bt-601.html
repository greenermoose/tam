BT.601

An ITU recommendation for digital standard-definition television.

An ITU recommendation for digital standard-definition television.

It defines parameters for digital television signals and their sampling.

Its matrix coefficients differ from those commonly associated with BT.709; signal metadata must identify the actual encoding.
