BT.2020

An ITU recommendation for ultra-high-definition television parameters.

An ITU recommendation for ultra-high-definition television parameters.

It defines a wide set of color primaries and other UHD television parameters.

BT.2020 primaries alone do not mean a signal is HDR; transfer function and other metadata matter.
