Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with P / PipeWire

The modern Linux multimedia server that routes audio and video streams between applications and hardware.

PipeWire is the background service that manages sound on Tamlinux—mixing audio from your browser, music player, and video calls and sending it to your chosen speakers or headphones.

PipeWire replaces older PulseAudio and JACK servers while remaining compatible with both. With WirePlumber as its session manager, tools like wpctl and tam-audio-* inspect and switch default audio sinks and sources.

What role does PipeWire play when two apps play sound at the same time?

PipeWire acts as the central audio graph, mixing both streams with low latency and routing them to the active output hardware.

PipeWire implements a lock-free multimedia processing graph over shared-memory buffers and memfds, providing native ALSA, PulseAudio (pipewire-pulse), and JACK compatibility plus Wayland screencast video streams via xdg-desktop-portal.
