Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with P / P2P (Person-to-Person)

P2P (Person-to-Person): a conversation between people, as distinct from automated business or application traffic.

P2P messaging means a chat between people rather than a message from a company. It is the ordinary case of one person writing to another.

When a service says encryption covers person-to-person conversations, that wording may exclude business bots and automated messages.

In RCS encryption specifications, P2P conversations are the scope of end-to-end protection; chatbot conversations are treated separately. In messaging generally, P2P describes a trust relationship, not a particular transport.
