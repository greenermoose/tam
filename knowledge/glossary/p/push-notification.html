Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with P / Push notification

Push notification: a small operating-system alert that wakes an app and tells it to check for new data.

A push notification is a small alert sent to a phone. It tells an app that something may be waiting.

The alert may show a preview, but the app usually fetches the real conversation afterwards. A lock-screen preview can appear even when the stored message is encrypted.

APNs and FCM are device push transports. Their success responses describe transport acceptance, not end-to-end message delivery or reading.
