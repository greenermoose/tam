Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with P / Pyrometallurgy (metal recovery with heat)

Pyrometallurgy recovers metals using heat, smelting and furnaces.

Where hydrometallurgy uses solutions, this route melts material in a furnace to separate metals. It is a long-established way of getting metal back from discarded equipment.

The process is energy intensive and needs off-gas controls to manage fumes and dust from the furnace.

Off-gas treatment is part of the process rather than an optional extra, because the material fed to a furnace can contain hazardous components. Depollution removes batteries, mercury lamps and brominated flame retardants before shredding and smelting, limiting what the furnace and its gas handling must deal with.
