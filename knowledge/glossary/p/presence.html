Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with P / Presence

Presence: information about whether a contact is available, busy or offline in a messaging service.

Presence tells you whether someone appears available in a chat service. It is a status hint, not proof that they will answer.

Presence is different from a delivery receipt. A person can be online while a message is delayed, or offline while a device has already accepted one.

XMPP and RCS define presence information with privacy rules. Capability discovery is related but different: it says what features a client supports, not whether a person is present.
