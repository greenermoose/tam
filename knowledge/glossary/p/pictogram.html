Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with P / Pictogram

A pictogram is a sign whose shape looks like the thing it represents.

A small drawing of a bus on a sign means 'bus stop' almost without words. That is a pictogram: a picture that carries a message quickly across language barriers. Signs often combine pictograms with a short label.

Early writing systems such as Egyptian hieroglyphs and cuneiform began with pictographic signs, which later became abstract and could stand for sounds. Modern pictograms appear on road signs, interfaces and emoji. They work well across languages but can be ambiguous without context.

Pictograms sit on a continuum between icon and writing sign: once a drawing is conventionally linked to a word or sound it functions as a logogram or phonogram. Unicode encodes many pictorial signs with standardized names, and presentation selectors choose text or emoji style. A font may implement one sign as monochrome outlines or layered color glyphs.
