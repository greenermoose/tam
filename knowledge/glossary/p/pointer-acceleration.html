Pointer acceleration

A setting that scales how far the on-screen cursor travels based on how fast you move your mouse or finger.

Pointer acceleration lets a slow, careful hand movement nudge the cursor a few pixels for precision, while a quick flick sends the cursor all the way across a wide screen.

libinput supports adaptive acceleration (speed-sensitive) and flat acceleration (constant ratio regardless of hand speed). Tune it in your desktop input config if the cursor feels too twitchy or sluggish.

When might someone choose a flat pointer acceleration profile instead of adaptive?

When they want the same physical hand distance to always move the cursor the exact same distance on screen regardless of speed.

libinput maps raw device deltas through a velocity-dependent gain curve (LIBINPUT_CONFIG_ACCEL_PROFILE_ADAPTIVE) or constant multiplier (LIBINPUT_CONFIG_ACCEL_PROFILE_FLAT) normalized to 1000 DPI.
