Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with P / Product carbon footprint (PCF)

A product carbon footprint, or PCF, is the greenhouse gas emissions attributed to one product.

A footprint turns many small emissions into one comparable figure, usually written as CO2-equivalent for a stated amount of product. It is a calculation, not a label on the box.

A PCF covers emissions across a chosen life-cycle boundary and is often calculated using life cycle assessment methods. It is expressed as CO2-equivalent per functional unit. It is not the same as an ecolabel.

The boundary can be cradle-to-grave or cradle-to-gate, and results depend on that choice and on data quality, so two numbers need matching functional units to be comparable. ISO 14040 and ISO 14044 provide the LCA framework; ISO/IEC 21031 is a separate, disclosure-only method for software carbon intensity. A management-system certificate under ISO 14001 is not a PCF.
