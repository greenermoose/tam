Provenance

The documented origin and authorship history of a change, build, or AI-assisted contribution.

Provenance is the paper trail showing where a change came from—who asked for it, what tools helped build it, and what checks verified it.

In public Tamlinux repositories, AI-assisted commits carry structured trailers (Co-authored-by, AI-Tool, AI-Model) and plain-text work records under work/ while excluding private session IDs and local paths.

What must never appear in a public Tamlinux provenance record?

Private conversation or session IDs, transcript file paths, personal home-directory paths, and names of private repositories.

Tamlinux provenance pairs Git commit trailers with per-session work/W<n>-<name> narratives and repository consistency checks (check-repo.py) to keep public history auditable without leaking private environment metadata.
