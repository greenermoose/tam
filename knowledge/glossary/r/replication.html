Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with R / Replication

Replication copies data to more than one place so a system can survive a failure or serve more readers.

It is keeping a second set of books in another library: readers can use either copy, but the two must be kept in step.

Replication can improve availability and read throughput. Copies may be updated synchronously or asynchronously.

Leader-follower and leaderless designs make different consistency and failover choices. Replication lag means a follower may briefly answer with older data.
