Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with R / RAN (Radio Access Network)

RAN (Radio Access Network): the part of a mobile network that talks to phones over radio and manages handover.

The RAN is the radio side of a mobile network. It is the cells and base stations a phone talks to, not the core that routes messages.

When you walk between cells, the radio network hands your connection from one base station to another. The message service does not become a different service because you moved.

LTE RAN is E-UTRAN with eNodeB; 5G RAN is NG-RAN with gNodeB. TS 36.300 and TS 38.300 describe scheduling, bearer handling and mobility.
