Retrieval practice

A learning technique where you actively recall an answer from memory before checking the solution.

Retrieval practice means pausing to answer a question from memory before looking at the answer, rather than just rereading the page.

Every Learner section in the tam Guide ends with folded Check yourself questions designed for retrieval practice, explaining not just what is right but why.

Why is trying to answer a folded question more effective than reading the answer right away?

Actively pulling an idea out of memory strengthens retention and immediately exposes gaps in understanding.

Grounded in cognitive psychology (the testing and generation effects), retrieval practice is built into the corpus pedagogy contract via details.q folds with formative feedback.
