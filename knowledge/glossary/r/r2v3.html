Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with R / R2v3 (Responsible Recycling Standard)

R2v3 is a certification standard for electronics recyclers published by SERI, the Sustainable Electronics Recycling International organisation.

A certified facility is checked on how it handles used electronics, including reuse, recycling and the security of data stored on old devices. The certificate belongs to a facility, not to a laptop or a component.

R2v3 was released on 1 July 2020 and has been the only active version since the R2:2013 transition ended on 30 June 2023. It is an ANSI-accredited facility certification covering reuse, downstream recycling, data security and environmental controls, with downstream tracking to final disposition or registration with SERI.

Downstream tracking is what connects a facility's certificate to the eventual fate of the material: it is followed to final disposition or to a downstream registered with SERI. That makes R2v3 a chain-of-custody question as well as an on-site one. e-Stewards is a separate recycler certification with different chain-of-custody and export rules; neither is a product ecolabel.
