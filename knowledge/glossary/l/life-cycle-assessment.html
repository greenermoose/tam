Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with L / Life cycle assessment (LCA method)

Life cycle assessment, or LCA, is a method for adding up the environmental impacts of a product across its life.

A cradle-to-grave study follows raw materials, manufacturing, use and disposal. A cradle-to-gate study stops at the factory gate, so its result does not include what happens later.

The method is defined by ISO 14040 and ISO 14044 in four phases: goal and scope, inventory, impact assessment and interpretation. It is used for product carbon footprints and for comparisons between products.

Results depend on the chosen boundary, functional unit and data quality, so a comparison is only as sound as those choices. LCA is a study method, not a label or a certificate; it also differs from a management system such as ISO 14001, which describes how an organisation operates.
