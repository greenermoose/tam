Lens

The reader's chosen difficulty level (All, Novice, Learner, or Expert) that filters what the tam pager displays.

A lens is the reading setting you choose inside the tam Guide. Picking Novice shows only plain-language introductions; Learner shows hands-on practice; Expert shows technical reference; All shows everything together.

Pressing 0, 1, 2, or 3 in the tam pager switches your lens immediately and keeps that preference as you follow links across pages.

What happens when you switch from the Novice lens (1) to the Learner lens (2)?

The pager hides Novice-only sections and displays Learner sections with goals, worked examples, Try it exercises, and Check yourself quizzes.

Implemented in src/depth.c, the active lens filters tagged data-level sections after fold numbering so fold identifiers remain stable across lens switches.
