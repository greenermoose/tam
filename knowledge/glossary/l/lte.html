Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with L / LTE (Long-Term Evolution)

LTE (Long-Term Evolution): the 4G cellular radio and packet-core system that replaced earlier 3G radio access.

LTE is the 4G mobile system many phones use for data. It can carry web traffic, chat traffic and voice through separate services.

A text can be carried by LTE signalling or by an app over an LTE data connection. The same signal bars can therefore support different message services.

3GPP TS 36.300 describes E-UTRAN and its radio procedures. LTE uses OFDMA on the downlink and SC-FDMA on the uplink, with an EPC core; 5G NR is its successor.
