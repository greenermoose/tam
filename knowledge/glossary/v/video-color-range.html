Guide / Glossary A–Z / Terms beginning with V / Video color range

The numeric range used for image component values.

A mismatch between a video signal and its display interpretation can make blacks look gray or hide shadow detail.

At 8 bits, conventional limited-range luma uses nominal values 16–235 and chroma 16–240; full range and other bit depths differ.
