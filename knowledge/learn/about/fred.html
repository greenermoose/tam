Fred Horch

This page introduces Fred Horch, the developer of Tamlinux.

Fred Horch is the developer of Tamlinux. He earned a degree in computer science from Swarthmore College, where he was a sysadmin for the department's Sun workstation lab from 1989 to 1991. After graduation, he worked in the San Francisco Bay Area as a network admin for Sybex, then attended UC Berkeley Law School and worked as general counsel for TriNet Services, an Internet consulting firm in the Research Triangle (Raleigh–Durham area) of North Carolina.

He is developing Tam so he can work more effectively with AI agents to get work done on the computers he and his family own.
