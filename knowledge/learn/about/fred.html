About Fred Horch, the developer of Tamlinux

Fred earned a degree in computer science from Swarthmore College, where he was a sysadmin for the department's Sun workstation lab from 1989 to 1991. After graduation, he worked in the San Francisco Bay Area as a network admin for Sybex, then earned a Juris Doctor from UC Berkeley Law School and worked as general counsel for TriNet Services, an Internet consulting firm in the Research Triangle (Raleigh–Durham area) of North Carolina.

Fred is developing Tam software in his spare time and on his own nickel to make his family's computers work better, and sharing the code freely to give back to the free software and open source communities. He hopes Tamlinux helps you do good things with your computers.
