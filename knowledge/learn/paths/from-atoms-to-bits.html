Why must a circuit be closed for steady current?

Charge needs a complete path. An open loop stops steady flow in ordinary wiring.

Stage 3: Digital logic

Digital logic: see how high and low voltages become bits and gates.

Check yourself

What does a bit represent at this level?

One of two stable states, often encoded as two voltage ranges, that logic gates combine.

Capstone

Explain in four sentences how a Meta-key press eventually depends on electrons, a bandgap switch, a circuit, and a logic decision. Do not change the system.

The key switch closes a circuit so electrons can flow as a detectable change. That change is sensed by semiconductor devices whose channels rely on bandgap physics. Digital logic treats the levels as bits. The compositor later maps the resulting key event to an action such as opening a terminal.
