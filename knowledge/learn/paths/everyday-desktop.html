Everyday desktop tasks

These step-by-step guides cover the desktop tasks you need most days without touching a terminal first.

Stage 1: Capture and share

Take screenshots, inspect clipboard history, and review desktop notifications safely.

Check yourself

Does copying text to the clipboard save it permanently to a file?

No. The clipboard holds a temporary selection until replaced or cleared; save important text in a real file.

Stage 2: Devices and screen comfort

Connect audio and Bluetooth devices, scale displays cleanly, and configure keyboard, touchpad, screen locking, and night light.

Check yourself

Why adjust display scaling or night light from a reversible setting before editing dotfiles?

Reversible controls let you confirm legibility and undo immediately without breaking session startup files.
