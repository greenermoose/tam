A Graduate Course in Applied Cryptography

This page points to a free graduate-level textbook covering symmetric ciphers, hashes, key exchange, signatures, and protocol security.

This reference is for learner readers and experts.

A Graduate Course in Applied Cryptography, by Dan Boneh and Victor Shoup. Covers stream and block ciphers, AEAD, hash functions, key exchange, public-key encryption, elliptic curves, zero-knowledge, and formal game-playing proofs.

Online PDF book. Free. Copyright Dan Boneh and Victor Shoup.
