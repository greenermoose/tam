IEEE 802.3an: 10GBASE-T Ethernet Physical Layer

This page points to the IEEE 802.3an amendment specifying 10 Gigabit Ethernet over unshielded or shielded twisted-pair copper cabling.

This reference is for expert readers.

IEEE Std 802.3an-2006 (folded into IEEE 802.3 Clause 55). Specifies 10GBASE-T over Category 6/6A copper using PAM-16 Tomlinson-Harashima precoding and (2048, 1723) LDPC forward error correction.

Published IEEE standard (available through the IEEE GET Program).
