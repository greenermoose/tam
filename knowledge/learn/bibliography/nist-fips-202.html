NIST FIPS 202 (SHA-3 Standard)

This page points to the standard defining the SHA-3 family of sponge-based hash and extendable-output functions.

This reference is for expert readers.

NIST FIPS PUB 202: SHA-3 Standard: Permutation-Based Hash and Extendable-Output Functions. Specifies the Keccak-f[1600] permutation, sponge construction, SHA3-224/256/384/512, and SHAKE128/SHAKE256.

Online PDF specification. Free. Public domain (U.S. Government work).
