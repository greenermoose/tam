NIST FIPS 180-4 (Secure Hash Standard)

This page points to the United States federal specification defining the SHA-2 family of cryptographic hash functions.

This reference is for expert readers.

NIST FIPS PUB 180-4: Secure Hash Standard (SHS). Defines message padding, parsing, initial hash values, round constants, and the compression functions for SHA-224, SHA-256, SHA-384, SHA-512, SHA-512/224, and SHA-512/256.

Online PDF specification. Free. Public domain (U.S. Government work).
