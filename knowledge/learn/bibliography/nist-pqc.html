NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography Standards (FIPS 203, 204, 205)

This page points to the United States federal standards for quantum-resistant key encapsulation and digital signatures.

This reference is for expert readers.

NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography Standardization: FIPS 203 (ML-KEM / Module-Lattice Key-Encapsulation Mechanism), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA / Module-Lattice Digital Signature Algorithm), and FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA / Stateless Hash-Based Digital Signature Algorithm), published 2024.

Online PDF specifications. Free. Public domain (U.S. Government work).
