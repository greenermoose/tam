HDMI Specification

This page points to the official industry specification for High-Definition Multimedia Interface systems.

This reference is for learner readers and experts.

HDMI Specification. HDMI Forum and HDMI Licensing Administrator. Technical specifications defining Transition-Minimized Differential Signaling (TMDS), Fixed Rate Link (FRL), Consumer Electronics Control (CEC), Enhanced Audio Return Channel (eARC), and display bandwidth profiles through HDMI 2.1.

Online specifications and summaries available from HDMI.org. Standards body copyright HDMI Forum.
