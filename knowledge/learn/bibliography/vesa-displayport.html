VESA DisplayPort Standard

This page points to the official industry specification for DisplayPort interfaces.

This reference is for learner readers and experts.

VESA DisplayPort Standard. Video Electronics Standards Association. Technical specifications covering the micro-packet Main Link architecture, 128b/132b channel encoding, AUX channel protocol, Multi-Stream Transport (MST), and DisplayPort Alternate Mode over USB Type-C.

Online specifications available via VESA membership and public standard summaries. Standards body copyright VESA.
