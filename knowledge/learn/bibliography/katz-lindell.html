Introduction to Modern Cryptography

This page points to a standard university textbook on formal cryptographic definitions, security proofs, and reductions.

This reference is for learner readers and experts.

Jonathan Katz and Yehuda Lindell, Introduction to Modern Cryptography (3rd edition). Covers Shannon secrecy, computational security, PRFs/PRPs, AEAD, hash functions, number theory, RSA, Diffie-Hellman, elliptic curves, and digital signatures with full security reductions.

Print and digital textbook. Commercial purchase. CRC Press.
