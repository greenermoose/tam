CTA-861: A DTV Profile for Uncompressed High Speed Digital Interfaces

This page points to the Consumer Technology Association standard defining digital video timing formats and EDID extension blocks.

This reference is for expert readers.

ANSI/CTA-861 (Consumer Technology Association). Defines standard Video Identification Codes (VICs), colorimetry and HDR InfoFrames, and CEA/CTA EDID extension blocks used by HDMI and DisplayPort sinks.
