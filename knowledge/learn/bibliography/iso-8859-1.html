ISO/IEC 8859-1: 8-bit Single-Byte Coded Graphic Character Sets (Latin-1)

This page points to the ISO standard defining the 8-bit Latin-1 character set.

This reference is for expert readers.

ISO/IEC 8859-1:1998, Information technology — 8-bit single-byte coded graphic character sets — Part 1: Latin alphabet No. 1. Assigns bytes 0xA0–0xFF to Western European characters, leaving 0x80–0x9F reserved for C1 control characters in ISO/IEC 2022 and ECMA-48 environments.

Published ISO standard. Paid specification; widely summarized in iso_8859-1(7).
