RC4 (Rivest Cipher 4)

Obsolete stream cipher with severe statistical biases, banned from TLS and WEP.

Novice definition

RC4 is an old, broken encryption algorithm that is no longer allowed in modern web or Wi-Fi security.

Learner definition

RFC 7465 prohibited RC4 in TLS due to keystream biases; WEP's misuse of RC4 made Wi-Fi crackable in minutes.

Expert definition

1987 byte-oriented stream cipher with 256-byte state permutation; vulnerable to Fluhrer-Mantin-Shamir and keystream-bias attacks.
