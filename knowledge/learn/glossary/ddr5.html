DDR5 (Double Data Rate 5 SDRAM)

Current-generation system RAM adding on-die ECC, dual 32-bit subchannels, and on-DIMM PMIC.

Novice definition

DDR5 is the current generation of computer RAM, offering higher speeds and built-in chip-level error checking.

Learner definition

Every DDR5 chip includes on-die ECC for internal cell reliability, though full bus protection still requires sideband ECC.

Expert definition

JEDEC JESD79-5 at 1.1 V, dual 32-bit (+8-bit ECC) subchannels per DIMM, 16n prefetch, and on-DIMM PMIC.
