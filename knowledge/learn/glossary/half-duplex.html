Half Duplex

Half duplex is a bidirectional channel where only one party can transmit at any given moment.

Novice definition

A two-way communication line where devices must take turns talking, like people using walkie-talkies. Both sides can send and receive, but if both try to speak at the exact same second, their signals collide and garble.

Learner definition

A mode of operation where transmission occurs in both directions over a shared physical medium, but sequentially rather than concurrently (such as 2-wire RS-485 or legacy coax Ethernet). Protocols require line-turnaround delays or Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA) to avoid collisions.

Expert definition

Time-Division Duplexing (TDD) or shared-medium operation where transceivers switch between transmit and receive states. In half-duplex links, the turnaround time (t_turnaround) and driver enable latency directly bound net channel utilization.
