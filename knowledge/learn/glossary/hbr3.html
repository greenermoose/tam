HBR3 (High Bit Rate 3)

DisplayPort 1.3/1.4 8.1 Gbps per-lane link rate supporting 4K 120 Hz or 8K with DSC.

Novice definition

HBR3 pushes DisplayPort to 8.1 Gbps per lane for high-refresh 4K and multi-monitor setups.

Learner definition

Part of DisplayPort link rates, HBR3 pairs with DSC compression for 5K/8K displays.

Expert definition

DisplayPort 1.3/1.4 8.10 Gbps/lane (8b/10b, 25.92 Gbps payload across 4 lanes) using TPS4 training.
