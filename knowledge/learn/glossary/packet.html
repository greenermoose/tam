Packet

A small unit of network data.

Novice definition

A packet is one chunk of data sent across a network, with addressing information attached.

Learner definition

A packet is a network-layer protocol data unit with headers and payload, routed independently or as part of a flow.

Expert definition

A packet is a formatted PDU containing control information and payload at a given layer, subject to MTU, fragmentation, and checksum rules.
