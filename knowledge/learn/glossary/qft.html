QFT (Quantum Fourier Transform)

Quantum-circuit analogue of the discrete Fourier transform, core to Shor's algorithm.

Novice definition

QFT is the key quantum-computing step that lets Shor's algorithm find hidden periods in numbers.

Learner definition

Shor's algorithm uses QFT to extract periodicity from modular exponentiation, breaking RSA and ECC on large quantum computers.

Expert definition

Unitary transformation on n qubits in O(n^2) gates enabling quantum phase estimation and period finding.
