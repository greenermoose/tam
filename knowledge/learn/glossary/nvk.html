NVK (NVIDIA Open-Source Vulkan Mesa Driver)

Open-source userspace Vulkan driver for NVIDIA GPUs in Mesa.

Novice definition

NVK is the newer open-source Vulkan driver in Mesa for NVIDIA graphics cards.

Learner definition

Working over the upstream nouveau/nova kernel DRM drivers, NVK aims to bring first-class open Vulkan to NVIDIA.

Expert definition

Mesa Vulkan ICD for Turing+ NVIDIA GPUs using the NAK compiler and nouveau/nova DRM kernel UAPI.
