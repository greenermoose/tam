HPD (Hot Plug Detect)

Physical signal pin on HDMI/DisplayPort alerting the GPU when a monitor connects, disconnects, or requests attention.

Novice definition

Hot Plug Detect (HPD) is the wire in a display cable that tells your computer 'a monitor was just plugged in or unplugged'.

Learner definition

Flickering monitors or wl_output churn often trace to noisy HPD pulses (short IRQ_HPD vs. full disconnect).

Expert definition

Dedicated pin (HDMI pin 19, DP pin 18); DisplayPort uses short 0.5-1 ms low pulses as AUX-channel IRQ_HPD interrupts.
