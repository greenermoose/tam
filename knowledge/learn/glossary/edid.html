EDID

EDID (Extended Display Identification Data) is a standardized data format used by monitors to describe their capabilities to a computer.

Novice definition

A digital identity card sent by your monitor through the display cable that tells the computer its brand, model, screen size, and supported resolutions.

Learner definition

A 128-byte (or 256-byte extended) binary metadata structure standardized by VESA. The operating system kernel reads EDID during display hotplugging to automatically select the monitor's native resolution and refresh rate without manual user configuration.

Expert definition

A VESA-defined binary descriptor transmitted over the I2C-based Display Data Channel (DDC2B) or DisplayPort AUX channel. EDID encodes manufacturer IDs, physical panel dimensions in millimeters, gamma response curves, colorimetry coordinates (CIE chromaticity), standardized VESA timing descriptors, and CTA-861 extensions for HDR and audio formats.
