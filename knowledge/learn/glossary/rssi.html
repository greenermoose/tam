RSSI (Received Signal Strength Indicator)

Measurement of incoming radio signal power (typically in dBm for Wi-Fi).

Novice definition

RSSI tells you how strong a Wi-Fi or Bluetooth signal is when it reaches your computer.

Learner definition

In Linux iw/nmcli output, Wi-Fi RSSI around -50 dBm is strong while -80 dBm is weak and prone to packet loss.

Expert definition

Receiver-measured RF power indicator (usually dBm); signal quality depends on SNR (RSSI minus noise floor), not RSSI alone.
