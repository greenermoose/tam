AES-128

Advanced Encryption Standard using a 128-bit key and 10 rounds.

Novice definition

AES-128 is a fast version of the AES cipher that uses a 128-bit secret key.

Learner definition

Part of AES variants, AES-128 offers 128-bit classical security and is widely used in TLS 1.3.

Expert definition

AES with 128-bit key and 10 SPN rounds; provides ~64-bit post-quantum brute-force security under Grover's algorithm (motivating AES-256 for quantum safety).
