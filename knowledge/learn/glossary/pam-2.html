PAM-2 (NRZ / 2-level Pulse-Amplitude Modulation)

Binary baseband signaling using two voltage levels (1 bit per symbol).

Novice definition

PAM-2 is classic binary signaling with just two voltage levels: high for 1 and low for 0.

Learner definition

Part of PAM modulation, PAM-2 (NRZ) is used up through PCIe 5.0 and USB 3.x.

Expert definition

2-level baseband modulation (1 bit/UI) with maximum eye height before channels hit ~30+ GHz Nyquist loss.
