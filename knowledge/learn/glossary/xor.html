XOR (Exclusive OR)

Logic operation outputting 1 when inputs differ; foundational in digital adders, parity, and ciphers.

Novice definition

XOR is a basic logic rule that outputs true when two inputs are different, and false when they are the same.

Learner definition

XOR is reversible (A XOR B XOR B = A), making it central to stream ciphers, one-time pads, and RAID parity.

Expert definition

Boolean addition modulo 2 (A ^ B); forms half-adders, LFSRs, CRC/ECC parity, and keystream mixing.
