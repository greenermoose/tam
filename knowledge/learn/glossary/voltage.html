Voltage

Voltage is electrical pressure that pushes electric charges through a conductor.

Novice definition

The electrical pressure created by a power supply or battery, measured in volts. Just as water pressure pushes water through a hose, voltage pushes electric current through wires from a point of higher voltage to a point of lower voltage.

Learner definition

The electric potential difference between two distinct points in an electrical circuit. Voltage determines how much energy is transferred per unit of electric charge (one volt equals one joule of energy per coulomb of charge).

Expert definition

The line integral of the electric field between two spatial coordinates, representing the work done against an electrostatic field to move a unit test charge between them. In computer digital signaling, distinct voltage thresholds define binary logic states and noise margins.
