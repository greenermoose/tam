VT102 (DEC Video Terminal)

1982 DEC hardware terminal whose escape sequences shaped the Linux console.

Novice definition

The VT102 was an early 1980s computer terminal whose control codes are still imitated by Linux text consoles.

Learner definition

The Linux kernel virtual console implements a subset of VT102 and ECMA-48 control sequences for cursor movement and screen clearing.

Expert definition

Digital Equipment Corporation VT102 (1982), a cost-reduced VT100 with Advanced Video Option and printer port; its ANSI/ECMA-48 subset is documented in console_codes(4).
