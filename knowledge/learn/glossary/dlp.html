DLP (Discrete Logarithm Problem)

Math problem of finding x given g^x mod p, broken by Shor's quantum algorithm.

Novice definition

The Discrete Logarithm Problem is a one-way math puzzle: easy to multiply powers forward, hard to reverse without the secret.

Learner definition

Classical Diffie-Hellman and DSA rely on DLP; quantum computers running Shor's algorithm can solve it efficiently.

Expert definition

Given generator g and h = g^x in finite cyclic group G, recover x; subexponential classically (NFS) and polynomial on quantum computers.
