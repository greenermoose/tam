RBR (Reduced Bit Rate)

DisplayPort 1.62 Gbps per-lane link rate.

Novice definition

RBR is the slowest fallback speed for a DisplayPort monitor cable (1.62 Gbps per lane).

Learner definition

Part of DisplayPort link rates, RBR is used for low-resolution displays or when link training falls back on a weak cable.

Expert definition

DisplayPort 1.0+ 1.62 Gbps/lane (8b/10b, 1.296 Gbps payload) across 1, 2, or 4 lanes.
