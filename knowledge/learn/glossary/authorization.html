Authorization

Deciding what an authenticated identity is allowed to read, change, or execute.

Novice definition

Showing your ID at the front desk proves who you are (authentication); checking which doors your badge is allowed to unlock is authorization.

Learner definition

After authentication establishes a principal (such as a Linux user ID or a signed token), authorization evaluates a policy—file permissions, capabilities, ACLs, or RBAC/ABAC rules—before granting access to a resource.

Expert definition

The reference monitor's policy-decision and policy-enforcement step mapping a subject, object, and requested operation (plus optional environmental attributes) to allow or deny according to an access control model.
