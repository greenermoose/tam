ACK (Acknowledge, 0x06)

ASCII 0x06 control character and general protocol acknowledgment signal.

Novice definition

ACK is a short signal meaning 'message received okay', both as ASCII byte 0x06 and in network protocols.

Learner definition

Part of the C0 control codes, ACK (0x06) confirms valid receipt in serial and ARQ protocols.

Expert definition

ASCII 0x06 (ACK, Ctrl+F). Also denotes positive acknowledgment packets in TCP, I2C, and Stop-and-Wait ARQ protocols.
