RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)

Access-control model assigning permissions to named roles rather than individual users.

Novice definition

RBAC organizes permissions by job role—such as 'admin', 'wheel', or 'audio'—instead of setting rules per person.

Learner definition

Unix supplementary groups (wheel, video, audio) are a simple form of RBAC.

Expert definition

NIST RBAC model mapping users -> roles -> permissions, contrasted with fine-grained ABAC.
