Gamma

A curve that relates stored values to displayed brightness.

Novice definition

Gamma is the brightness curve that makes images look right on a screen.

Learner definition

Gamma correction compensates for nonlinear display response, often with a power-law curve.

Expert definition

Gamma is the exponent in a transfer function, generalized by sRGB, PQ, and HLG curves in color-managed pipelines.
