HDR - High Dynamic Range

A display mode with a wider range of brightness and color.

Novice definition

HDR makes bright highlights and deep shadows look more realistic on a compatible screen.

Learner definition

High dynamic range carries more luminance and color precision than standard dynamic range, with metadata or PQ/HLG encoding.

Expert definition

HDR is a transfer-function, metadata, and display-capability system using PQ or HLG, wide color primaries, and tone mapping.
