FF (Form Feed, 0x0C)

ASCII 0x0C control character (Ctrl+L) used to clear the screen or advance a printer page.

Novice definition

FF is the Form Feed byte (0x0C); on printers it starts a new page, and in terminals Ctrl+L clears the screen.

Learner definition

Part of the C0 control codes, FF (0x0C) is treated like a line feed by the Linux console and bound to clear-screen in shells.

Expert definition

ASCII 0x0C (FF, '\f', Ctrl+L). Page-eject control in ECMA-48; Readline binds 0x0C to clear-screen.
