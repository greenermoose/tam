Teletype

An old machine that typed and printed messages over a wire.

Novice definition

A teletype is an early device that sent typed text over a wire and printed the reply.

Learner definition

A teletypewriter, or TTY, combined a keyboard, printer, and serial link for remote text communication.

Expert definition

A teletype is an electromechanical serial terminal using a keyboard, typebox or printer, and line signaling; its name survives in the Unix tty abstraction.
