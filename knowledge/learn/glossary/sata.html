SATA - Serial ATA

A common connector and protocol for storage devices.

Novice definition

SATA is the cable and language used by many hard drives and older SSDs.

Learner definition

Serial ATA carries storage commands and data over a point-to-point serial link.

Expert definition

SATA is a serial storage transport with FIS-based command and data framing, AHCI or IDE emulation, and hot-plug support.
