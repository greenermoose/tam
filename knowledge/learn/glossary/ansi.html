ANSI (American National Standards Institute)

US standards body whose X3.64 terminal standard popularized escape sequences.

Novice definition

ANSI is a standards organization; in Linux terminals, 'ANSI codes' usually means the escape sequences that color text and move the cursor.

Learner definition

ANSI X3.64 adopted ECMA-48 control sequences for video terminals, which is why ECMA-48 color and cursor codes are commonly called ANSI escape codes.

Expert definition

American National Standards Institute. ANSI X3.64 (withdrawn in favor of ECMA-48 / ISO/IEC 6429) and ANSI X3.4 (ASCII) shaped Unix terminal protocols and C89 (ANSI C).
