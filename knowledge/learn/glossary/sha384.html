SHA-384

Truncated 384-bit variant of SHA-512 in the SHA-2 family.

Novice definition

SHA-384 is a 384-bit version of SHA-2 often used in high-security TLS connections.

Learner definition

Part of SHA algorithms, SHA-384 runs 64-bit SHA-512 operations and truncates the output to 384 bits.

Expert definition

FIPS 180-4 SHA-384: SHA-512 with distinct IVs truncated to 384 bits, immune to length-extension attacks.
