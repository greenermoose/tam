Raster graphics

Images made of a grid of pixels.

Novice definition

Raster graphics are pictures built from pixels, like a photo or screenshot.

Learner definition

Raster graphics store a pixel grid; enlarging them reveals individual pixels unless resampled.

Expert definition

Raster graphics are sampled images with a fixed pixel matrix, channels, and color space, subject to quantization and resampling artifacts.
