Patch

A small change applied to software.

Novice definition

A patch is a focused edit that fixes or changes part of a program.

Learner definition

A patch is a diff or changeset applied to source, often carried by a distro until upstream accepts it.

Expert definition

A patch is a textual or binary delta with context, applied by patch or Git, possibly rebased and carried with provenance.
