ARQ (Automatic Repeat Request)

Error-control protocol that detects corrupt frames and requests retransmission.

Novice definition

ARQ is the simple rule 'if a packet arrives damaged or goes missing, ask the sender to send it again'.

Learner definition

TCP, Wi-Fi MAC frames, and many serial links use ARQ (Stop-and-Wait, Go-Back-N, or Selective Repeat) with ACK/NAK.

Expert definition

Link/transport reliability mechanism pairing CRC/checksum detection with ACK/NAK and timeouts for lossless delivery.
