Downstream

A project or distribution that builds on upstream software.

Novice definition

Downstream is the distro or product that takes upstream code and packages or modifies it.

Learner definition

Downstream consumers integrate upstream releases, carry patches, and distribute binaries or derived products.

Expert definition

Downstream is a derivative distribution or integration layer with its own packaging, patching, and release cadence relative to upstream.
