DPMS - Display Power Management Signaling

A display power-saving standard.

Novice definition

DPMS is the old standard that tells a monitor to dim or sleep when the computer is idle.

Learner definition

Display Power Management Signaling defines standby, suspend, and off states sent over display interfaces.

Expert definition

DPMS is a VESA signaling scheme for monitor power states, now largely folded into compositor idle and connector power management.
