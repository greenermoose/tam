I2C - Inter-Integrated Circuit

A two-wire bus for small chips and sensors.

Novice definition

I2C is a simple two-wire connection used by sensors and small chips to talk to a computer.

Learner definition

Inter-Integrated Circuit uses clock and data lines with addressed devices and acknowledge bits.

Expert definition

I2C is a synchronous multi-master serial bus with 7- or 10-bit addresses, open-drain signaling, and clock stretching.
