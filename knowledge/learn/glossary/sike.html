SIKE (Supersingular Isogeny Key Encapsulation)

Broken post-quantum KEM built on SIDH.

Novice definition

SIKE was a post-quantum encryption candidate based on SIDH that was broken in 2022.

Learner definition

NIST eliminated SIKE in Round 4 after classical attacks broke its underlying SIDH assumption in minutes on a single PC.

Expert definition

CCA2 KEM transform of SIDH; rendered insecure by the 2022 Castryck-Decru / Maino-Martindale / Robert classical key-recovery attacks.
