TTY (Teletype)

Linux terminal device abstraction (/dev/tty*) descended from physical teletypes.

Novice definition

A TTY is Linux's name for a text terminal—either a full-screen virtual console or a terminal window's connection.

Learner definition

The tty command prints your current terminal device (e.g., /dev/pts/0 or /dev/tty1); see also PTY.

Expert definition

Kernel character-device subsystem (drivers/tty/) binding serial UARTs, VTs, and PTYs to line disciplines and job control.
