UHBR (Ultra High Bit Rate)

DisplayPort 2.0/2.1 link rates (10, 13.5, and 20 Gbps per lane) using 128b/132b encoding.

Novice definition

UHBR is the newest ultra-fast DisplayPort speed tier (up to 80 Gbps total) for 4K 240 Hz and dual 8K displays.

Learner definition

Part of DisplayPort link rates, UHBR switches from 8b/10b to efficient 128b/132b channel coding.

Expert definition

DisplayPort 2.0/2.1 UHBR10/13.5/20 (10/13.5/20 Gbps/lane) with 128b/132b encoding and FEC.
