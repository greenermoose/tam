GHASH

Polynomial universal hash function over GF(2^128) used for authentication in AES-GCM.

Novice definition

GHASH is the fast authentication step inside AES-GCM that detects any tampering with encrypted data.

Learner definition

Accelerated by CPU carryless-multiply instructions (PCLMULQDQ), GHASH computes a tag over AAD and ciphertext.

Expert definition

Horner-rule evaluation over GF(2^128) with hash subkey H = AES_K(0^128) in AES-GCM; nonce reuse leaks H.
