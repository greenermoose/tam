RSA-OAEP (Optimal Asymmetric Encryption Padding)

Randomized, IND-CCA2-secure padding scheme for RSA encryption (RFC 8017).

Novice definition

RSA-OAEP is the safe padding method required when encrypting data with RSA so attackers cannot exploit math patterns.

Learner definition

Unlike legacy PKCS#1 v1.5 encryption (vulnerable to Bleichenbacher/ROBOT attacks), RSA-OAEP achieves IND-CCA2 security.

Expert definition

Bellare-Rogaway Feistel-based randomized padding (PKCS#1 v2.2 / RFC 8017) using MGF1 over SHA-256.
