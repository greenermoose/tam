HQC (Hamming Quasi-Cyclic)

Code-based post-quantum key encapsulation mechanism selected by NIST as a backup to ML-KEM.

Novice definition

HQC is a post-quantum encryption algorithm based on error-correcting codes rather than lattices, giving a backup in case lattice math breaks.

Learner definition

Selected by NIST in 2025 as a code-based KEM alongside ML-KEM, HQC provides diversity in mathematical assumptions.

Expert definition

NIST PQC Round 4 selected KEM based on quasi-cyclic syndrome decoding with concatenated BCH + Reed-Muller codes.
