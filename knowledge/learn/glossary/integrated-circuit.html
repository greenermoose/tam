Integrated circuit

A complete circuit built on a single piece of semiconductor.

Novice definition

An integrated circuit is a chip that contains many tiny electronic parts made together on one piece of silicon.

Learner definition

An integrated circuit combines transistors, diodes, and wiring on one die to perform a function.

Expert definition

An integrated circuit is a monolithic or hybrid semiconductor device containing interconnected components fabricated by lithography and diffusion.
