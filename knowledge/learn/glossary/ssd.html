SSD - Solid-State Drive

Storage with no moving parts.

Novice definition

An SSD is a fast storage device that keeps data in flash chips instead of spinning disks.

Learner definition

A solid-state drive uses NAND flash and a controller to provide persistent storage with low latency.

Expert definition

An SSD is a flash-based block device with FTL mapping, wear leveling, and NVMe or SATA transport.
