CTA-861 (DTV Video Timings)

Standard defining digital video timings, InfoFrames, and EDID extensions.

Novice definition

CTA-861 is the standard that helps monitors and TVs tell your computer which resolutions and HDR modes they support.

Learner definition

Used over HDMI and DisplayPort, CTA-861 defines standard Video Identification Codes (VICs) and EDID extension blocks.

Expert definition

ANSI/CTA-861 (formerly CEA-861) specifies uncompressed digital video/audio formats, AVI/DRM InfoFrames, and CEA Extension Version 3 blocks in EDID.
