DC

Electric current that flows in one direction.

Novice definition

DC is steady electricity that flows one way, like the power from a battery.

Learner definition

Direct current has constant polarity and is used by chips and many power supplies.

Expert definition

Direct current is unidirectional current with zero or slowly varying frequency, characterized by voltage, current, and ripple.
