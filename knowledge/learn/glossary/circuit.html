Circuit

A circuit is a continuous closed loop that allows electric current to circulate.

Beginner definition

A complete circular electrical path. Current leaves a power source, travels through wires and computer chips doing work, and must return to the power source. If the path is cut or disconnected, the circuit becomes open and current stops immediately.

Intermediate definition

An interconnected network of electrical conductors and components providing at least one closed trajectory for charge return. Power sources within the circuit force electrons up to a higher potential, sustaining continuous flow across connected loads.

Expert definition

A closed conductive network governed by Kirchhoff's circuit laws: the algebraic sum of currents entering any junction is zero, and the directed sum of potential differences around any closed loop is zero.
