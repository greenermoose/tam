Twisted pair

Two wires twisted together to carry signals.

Novice definition

Twisted pair is the kind of wire inside most network cables, with pairs twisted to reduce interference.

Learner definition

Twisted-pair cabling twists two conductors to cancel magnetic pickup and carries differential Ethernet signals.

Expert definition

Twisted pair is a balanced transmission line with characteristic impedance, twist rate, shielding categories, and crosstalk specifications.
