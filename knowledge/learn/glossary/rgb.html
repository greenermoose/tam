RGB - Red, Green, Blue

A color model built from red, green, and blue.

Novice definition

RGB is the way screens make colors by mixing red, green, and blue light.

Learner definition

RGB represents color as additive red, green, and blue components, commonly 8 bits each.

Expert definition

RGB is an additive color space with defined primaries, white point, transfer function, and matrix or LUT conversions.
