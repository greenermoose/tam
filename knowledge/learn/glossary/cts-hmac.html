CTS-HMAC (Ciphertext Stealing with HMAC)

Kerberos encryption type combining AES-CBC with ciphertext stealing and HMAC-SHA1/SHA2.

Novice definition

CTS-HMAC is the encryption format Kerberos uses to protect network login tickets without adding extra padding bytes.

Learner definition

Kerberos aes256-cts-hmac-sha1-96 and aes256-cts-hmac-sha384-192 pair CBC ciphertext stealing with HMAC integrity.

Expert definition

RFC 3962 / RFC 8009 Kerberos 5 enctype combining CBC-CS3 (ciphertext stealing) with truncated HMAC-SHA1 or HMAC-SHA384.
