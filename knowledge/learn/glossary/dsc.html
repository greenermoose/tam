DSC (Display Stream Compression)

VESA visually lossless, low-latency compression used over DisplayPort and HDMI for high-res/high-Hz modes.

Novice definition

DSC is an invisible, real-time video compression inside modern display cables that fits 4K 144 Hz or 8K over one cable.

Learner definition

DSC 1.2 achieves roughly 3:1 visually lossless compression with sub-scanline latency so high-refresh monitors work without artifacts.

Expert definition

VESA Display Stream Compression 1.2a: slice-based DPCM/ICH/entropy coding over DisplayPort 1.4+ and HDMI 2.1.
