ESD

A sudden static-electric discharge that can damage electronics.

Novice definition

ESD is the static shock you can feel when touching a doorknob, and it can destroy computer chips.

Learner definition

Electrostatic discharge transfers charge between objects and can damage semiconductor junctions.

Expert definition

ESD is a transient discharge event modeled by human-body or machine models, mitigated by grounding, packaging, and protection diodes.
