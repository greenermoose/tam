SIGTSTP (Terminal Stop signal)

Catchable signal (20) sent by Ctrl+Z to suspend a foreground job.

Novice definition

SIGTSTP is the 'pause' signal sent when you press Ctrl+Z, returning you to the shell prompt so you can resume the job later with fg.

Learner definition

Part of Unix signals, SIGTSTP can be caught by editors like micro or ncurses apps to restore the terminal before pausing.

Expert definition

POSIX terminal stop signal (20 on x86_64 Linux), emitted by N_TTY on VSUSP (0x1A) when ISIG is set.
