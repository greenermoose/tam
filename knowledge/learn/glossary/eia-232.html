EIA-232 (RS-232)

Formal Electronic Industries Alliance name for the RS-232 serial standard.

Novice definition

EIA-232 is the official name for the classic RS-232 serial port standard.

Learner definition

See RS-232 for voltage levels and serial console usage.

Expert definition

EIA/TIA-232-F standard defining +/-3 V to +/-15 V single-ended asynchronous serial signaling.
