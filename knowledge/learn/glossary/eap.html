EAP (Extensible Authentication Protocol)

Authentication framework used in enterprise Wi-Fi (802.1X / WPA-Enterprise) and VPNs.

Novice definition

EAP is the login framework used by company and university Wi-Fi networks when you sign in with a username, password, or certificate.

Learner definition

Unlike home Wi-Fi PSK, WPA2/WPA3-Enterprise uses EAP (EAP-TLS, PEAP, EAP-TTLS) over IEEE 802.1X.

Expert definition

RFC 3748 extensible authentication framing carrying TLS/credential methods inside RADIUS/EAPOL.
