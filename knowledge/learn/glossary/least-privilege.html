Least privilege

Giving every user, program, or process only the minimum rights needed for its job.

Novice definition

A hotel housekeeper's key opens guest rooms on their floor, not the hotel's bank vault—so a lost key does minimal damage.

Learner definition

Running daily work as an unprivileged user, splitting root powers into Linux capabilities, and isolating apps with seccomp or namespaces all limit what a bug or attacker can reach.

Expert definition

Saltzer and Schroeder's design principle requiring every program and user to operate with the minimal set of privileges necessary to complete the task, bounding blast radius and eliminating ambient authority.
