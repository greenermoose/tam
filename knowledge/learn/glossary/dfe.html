DFE (Decision Feedback Equalizer)

Receiver equalizer subtracting post-cursor inter-symbol interference from previous bits.

Novice definition

DFE is a smart filter in fast cables that cancels out the 'echo' of the previous bit before reading the next bit.

Learner definition

Paired with CTLE in PCIe, USB4, and DisplayPort, DFE cleans up reflections without amplifying high-frequency noise.

Expert definition

Non-linear tapped delay-line equalizer subtracting weighted prior symbol decisions h_k * d_{n-k} to cancel post-cursor ISI.
