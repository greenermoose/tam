BER

The rate at which bits are received incorrectly.

Novice definition

BER is a measure of how many bits get damaged during transmission.

Learner definition

Bit error rate is the number of incorrect bits divided by the total bits received.

Expert definition

BER is the probability or measured ratio of bit errors over a channel, often quoted before and after forward error correction.
