DDC - Display Data Channel

A channel for controlling a monitor.

Novice definition

DDC is the hidden data link that lets a computer read a monitor's capabilities and change its settings.

Learner definition

Display Data Channel carries EDID and control commands, often over I2C, including DDC/CI brightness and input switching.

Expert definition

DDC is a VESA interface using I2C or CI over auxiliary channels for EDID, MCCS controls, and display management.
