Electromagnetic Wave

An electromagnetic wave is a propagating oscillation of electric and magnetic fields that travels through space.

Novice definition

Invisible waves of energy traveling through the air at the speed of light, carrying information without any physical wires. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile phones, and radio all communicate by sending and receiving electromagnetic radio waves through antennas.

Learner definition

Self-propagating coupled waves of oscillating electric and magnetic fields. In computer networking, high-frequency alternating current fed into an antenna accelerates electrons, radiating radio waves into space. The waves induce an alternating electrical current in distant receiving antennas.

Expert definition

Electrodynamic radiation governed by Maxwell's equations traveling at velocity c = 1 / sqrt(mu_0 * epsilon_0). Digital communications modulate radio frequency (RF) carrier waves using techniques like Quadrature Amplitude Modulation (QAM) and Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiplexing (OFDM) in Wi-Fi and 5G cellular systems.
