AAD (Additional Authenticated Data)

Plaintext metadata (such as packet headers) integrity-protected by an AEAD cipher without being encrypted.

Novice definition

AAD is extra header information—like an envelope address—that stays readable in transit but is tamper-proofed by encryption.

Learner definition

In AEAD ciphers like AES-GCM or ChaCha20-Poly1305, AAD is authenticated by the MAC tag alongside the ciphertext.

Expert definition

Additional Authenticated Data fed into an AEAD authentication tag (e.g., GHASH in GCM or Poly1305) so tampering with headers causes decryption failure.
