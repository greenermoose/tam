Latency

The delay before data arrives.

Novice definition

Latency is the wait between sending something and getting a response.

Learner definition

Latency measures delay, often round-trip time, and is distinct from bandwidth.

Expert definition

Latency is the time for a signal or packet to traverse a path, including propagation, serialization, queuing, and processing delay.
