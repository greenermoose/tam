Workspace

A workspace is a group of windows you can switch between.

Beginner definition

A group of windows you can switch between.

Intermediate definition

A compositor-managed group of windows; viewing another workspace is different from moving a window into it.

Expert definition

A grouping and visibility abstraction whose display mapping and movement semantics depend on the compositor. Distinguish focus changes, window moves and whole-workspace output moves.
