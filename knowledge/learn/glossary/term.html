TERM

The environment variable that names a terminal type.

Novice definition

TERM is the setting that tells programs what kind of terminal they are talking to.

Learner definition

TERM names a terminfo entry describing cursor, color, and control capabilities.

Expert definition

TERM is an environment variable selecting a terminfo database entry that programs use to emit correct control sequences.
