USB - Universal Serial Bus

A standard for connecting devices to a computer.

Novice definition

USB is the common plug and protocol used for keyboards, mice, drives, and many other devices.

Learner definition

Universal Serial Bus provides power and data over a tiered serial network with hot-plug and device classes.

Expert definition

USB is a host-centered serial bus with enumeration, endpoints, transfer types, power delivery, and evolving link speeds.
