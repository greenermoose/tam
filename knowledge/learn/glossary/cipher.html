Cipher

A mathematical recipe for scrambling and unscrambling data using a key.

Novice definition

A cipher is the rulebook that turns readable text into scrambled text (ciphertext) and turns it back when you have the secret key.

Learner definition

A cipher defines encryption and decryption algorithms parameterized by a key, operating either byte-by-byte (stream cipher) or on fixed chunks (block cipher).

Expert definition

A pair of efficiently computable algorithms (Enc, Dec) over key, message, and ciphertext spaces satisfying correctness and security definitions such as IND-CPA or PRP indistinguishability.
