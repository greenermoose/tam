HAIFA (HAsh Iterative FrAmework)

Cryptographic hash construction adding salt and bit-counter inputs to prevent length-extension and multi-target attacks.

Novice definition

HAIFA is an improved hash-function layout that fixes structural weaknesses found in older SHA-1 and SHA-2 designs.

Learner definition

Used by BLAKE and BLAKE2, HAIFA mixes a salt and message-length counter into every compression block.

Expert definition

Biham-Dunkelman iterative hash mode (C_i = F(C_{i-1}, M_i, bits, salt)) eliminating length-extension and herding flaws.
