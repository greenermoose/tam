QNX

Commercial POSIX-compliant real-time microkernel OS widely used in automotive and embedded systems.

Novice definition

QNX is a reliable real-time microkernel operating system commonly used inside cars, medical devices, and factory controllers.

Learner definition

QNX demonstrates that message-passing microkernels can meet strict real-time deadlines.

Expert definition

Neutrino message-passing RTOS microkernel with deterministic priority-inheritance IPC and isolated userspace drivers.
