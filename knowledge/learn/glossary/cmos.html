CMOS

A low-power transistor circuit family.

Novice definition

CMOS is the way most modern chips are built, using pairs of complementary transistors that draw little power.

Learner definition

CMOS logic uses complementary n-channel and p-channel MOSFETs so one transistor is off when the other is on.

Expert definition

CMOS is complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor logic, valued for low static power and high noise immunity.
