Photon

A photon is a tiny packet of light energy.

Novice definition

Light and other electromagnetic waves travel in packets called photons. Photons have no rest mass. Their energy depends on color or wavelength: blue light photons carry more energy than red light photons. Screens emit photons; fiber cables guide them.

Learner definition

A photon is the quantum of the electromagnetic field. Absorption of a photon can promote an electron across a semiconductor bandgap if the photon energy is large enough. Emission of photons is how LEDs and displays produce light.

Expert definition

The quantum of electromagnetic radiation with energy E = hf; absorption and emission of photons couple optical signals to electronic carrier transitions.
