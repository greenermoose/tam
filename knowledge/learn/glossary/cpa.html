CPA (Chosen-Plaintext Attack)

Threat model where an attacker can obtain ciphertexts for plaintexts of their choice.

Novice definition

CPA is an attack scenario where the attacker can feed known messages into an encryption system and inspect the output.

Learner definition

Any modern cipher must remain secure under CPA (see IND-CPA).

Expert definition

Cryptanalytic model granting the adversary access to an encryption oracle E_K(.).
