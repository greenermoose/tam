ARM - Advanced RISC Machines

A processor family common in phones and small computers.

Novice definition

ARM is the processor design used in most phones, tablets, and many small Linux computers.

Learner definition

ARM is a RISC instruction set family licensed as cores or architecture, widely used in mobile and embedded systems. Originally created in 1983 as the Acorn RISC Machine by Acorn Computers, it was renamed Advanced RISC Machines when ARM Ltd was formed in 1990, and is today officially stylized simply as Arm.

Expert definition

Arm is a RISC architecture with profiles, exception levels, and optional extensions, implemented by many vendors under license.
