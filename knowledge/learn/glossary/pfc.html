PFC (Power Factor Correction)

Power-supply stage shaping AC input current into a smooth sine wave in phase with voltage.

Novice definition

PFC is a circuit in modern PC power supplies that draws wall electricity smoothly instead of in wasteful spikes.

Learner definition

Active PFC achieves >0.95 power factor and supports universal 100-240 V AC input.

Expert definition

Boost converter stage preceding the main SMPS isolation transformer to maintain sinusoidal input current and low THD.
