SHA-2

Family of 224/256/384/512-bit cryptographic hash functions (FIPS 180-4).

Novice definition

SHA-2 is the standard family of secure digital fingerprint functions used across Linux packages, TLS, and Git.

Learner definition

Part of SHA algorithms, SHA-2 includes SHA-256 and SHA-384.

Expert definition

NIST FIPS 180-4 Merkle-Damgard hash family (SHA-224, SHA-256, SHA-384, SHA-512) using Davies-Meyer compression.
