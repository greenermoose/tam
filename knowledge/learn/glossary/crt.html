CRT

An old display technology using an electron beam.

Novice definition

A CRT is the bulky glass tube screen used by older monitors and televisions.

Learner definition

A cathode-ray tube scans an electron beam across a phosphor screen to draw an image.

Expert definition

A CRT is a vacuum tube display with electron guns, deflection coils, shadow mask or aperture grille, and analog scan timing.
