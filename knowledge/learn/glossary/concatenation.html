Concatenation

Joining things end to end.

Novice definition

Concatenation means sticking strings or files together in order to make one longer result.

Learner definition

Concatenation appends one sequence to another; in shells it often means adjacent strings or files joined without a separator.

Expert definition

Concatenation is the associative operation that forms a sequence from operands, with language-specific rules for strings, lists, and files.
