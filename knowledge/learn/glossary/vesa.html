VESA

The standards body behind many display interfaces.

Novice definition

VESA is the group that sets common display standards such as VESA mounts and DisplayPort.

Learner definition

The Video Electronics Standards Association publishes DisplayPort, EDID, DDC, and mounting standards.

Expert definition

VESA is a standards organization producing display, monitor-arm, and video-interface specifications, including DisplayPort and eDP.
