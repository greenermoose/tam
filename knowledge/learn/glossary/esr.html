ESR (Equivalent Series Resistance)

Internal resistive loss inside a capacitor that limits ripple filtering and causes heating.

Novice definition

ESR is the tiny hidden resistance inside a capacitor; as old capacitors wear out, rising ESR can make power supplies unstable.

Learner definition

Low-ESR polymer and ceramic capacitors are critical in switch-mode power supplies (SMPS) and CPU VRMs.

Expert definition

Lumped series resistance of a real capacitor (Z = ESR + j(omega*L - 1/(omega*C))), governing ripple voltage and I^2*R dissipation.
