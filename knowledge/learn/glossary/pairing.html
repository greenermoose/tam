Pairing

Setting up two devices to trust each other.

Novice definition

Pairing is the one-time setup that lets your phone and headphones or keyboard talk securely.

Learner definition

Pairing exchanges keys and bonds Bluetooth devices so they reconnect without repeating setup.

Expert definition

Pairing is a Bluetooth authentication and key-exchange procedure producing a bond, with SSP, passkey, or numeric comparison variants.
