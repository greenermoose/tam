Threat model

A structured description of what you are protecting, who might attack it, and how.

Novice definition

Before buying locks, you decide whether you are guarding against a curious roommate, a burglar, or a flood.

Learner definition

A threat model lists assets, trust boundaries, attacker capabilities, and mitigations—for example, deciding whether local disk encryption or network TLS addresses a stolen-laptop scenario.

Expert definition

A formal specification of assets, security assumptions, adversary goals and capabilities (e.g., Dolev–Yao network, PPT, or physical side-channel), and trust boundaries analyzed via frameworks such as STRIDE or attack trees.
