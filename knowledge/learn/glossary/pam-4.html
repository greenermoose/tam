PAM-4 (4-level Pulse-Amplitude Modulation)

Signaling using four voltage levels to carry 2 bits per symbol (00, 01, 10, 11).

Novice definition

PAM-4 doubles wire speed without doubling clock frequency by using four voltage levels instead of two.

Learner definition

Part of PAM modulation; PCIe 6.0 and 400G Ethernet use PAM-4 with FEC to handle its tighter voltage margins.

Expert definition

4-level baseband modulation (2 bits/symbol, 9.5 dB SNR penalty vs. NRZ) requiring Gray coding, DFE, and FEC.
