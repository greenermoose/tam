Service

A background program managed by the system.

Novice definition

A service is a program that runs in the background to provide a function such as networking or printing.

Learner definition

A service is a long-running unit managed by an init system, with start, stop, enable, and dependency behavior.

Expert definition

A service is a systemd unit type describing a process or process group, its execution context, dependencies, and restart policy.
