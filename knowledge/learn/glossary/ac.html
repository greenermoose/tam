AC - Alternating Current

Electric current that reverses direction.

Novice definition

AC is electricity that alternates direction, like power from a wall outlet.

Learner definition

Alternating current reverses polarity periodically and is used for mains distribution and transformers.

Expert definition

Alternating current is a periodic bidirectional current described by frequency, amplitude, phase, and waveform.
