RSA

A classic public-key cryptosystem based on the difficulty of factoring large integers.

Novice definition

RSA relies on the fact that multiplying two huge prime numbers together is easy, while splitting the result back into those two primes is astronomically hard for ordinary computers.

Learner definition

RSA supports digital signatures and key transport, though modern systems prefer Ed25519 and X25519 for smaller keys, faster speed, and resistance to padding pitfalls.

Expert definition

Trapdoor permutation f(x) = x^e mod N where N = p*q and e*d = 1 mod phi(N), requiring OAEP or PSS padding for IND-CCA2/EUF-CMA security; broken in polynomial time by Shor's quantum algorithm.
