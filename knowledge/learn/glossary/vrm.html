VRM (Voltage Regulator Module)

Multi-phase DC-DC buck converter on a motherboard stepping 12 V/20 V down to ~1 V for the CPU/GPU.

Novice definition

The VRM is the row of power components next to your CPU that steps charger/PSU voltage down to the ~1 volt the processor needs.

Learner definition

Multi-phase VRMs respond rapidly to CPU load spikes and work with DVFS to adjust voltage per P-state.

Expert definition

Multi-phase synchronous buck converter controlled via SVID/SVI3 telemetry to supply high-current sub-volt rails.
