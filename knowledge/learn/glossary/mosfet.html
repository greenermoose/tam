MOSFET - Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor

A transistor controlled by an electric field.

Novice definition

A MOSFET is a tiny switch used in chips; a voltage on its gate controls whether current flows.

Learner definition

A MOSFET has source, drain, gate, and body terminals; gate voltage modulates the channel between source and drain.

Expert definition

A MOSFET is an insulated-gate field-effect transistor whose channel conductivity is controlled by gate charge; it is the building block of CMOS.
