Scaling

Resizing an image or interface to fit a display.

Novice definition

Scaling makes text and images bigger or smaller so they fit the screen comfortably.

Learner definition

Scaling maps logical coordinates or image samples to physical pixels, using integer or fractional factors.

Expert definition

Scaling is resampling or coordinate transformation between source and destination grids, with filters for raster images and compositor transforms for UI.
