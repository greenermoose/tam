Backlight

The light source behind an LCD panel.

Novice definition

The backlight is the lamp that lights up an LCD screen.

Learner definition

A backlight illuminates an LCD from behind or edge; brightness is controlled by PWM or constant-current dimming.

Expert definition

Backlight control uses LED strings, boost converters, and PWM or DC dimming, exposed through sysfs or a display interface.
