SIDH (Supersingular Isogeny Diffie-Hellman)

Broken isogeny-based key exchange protocol (broken classically in 2022).

Novice definition

SIDH was an experimental post-quantum key exchange method that was broken by new math attacks in 2022.

Learner definition

SIDH's collapse (via Castryck-Decru) illustrates why post-quantum algorithms undergo years of public cryptanalysis before standardization.

Expert definition

Isogeny-based key exchange over supersingular elliptic curves; broken in polynomial time classically using Kani's theorem and torsion-point images.
