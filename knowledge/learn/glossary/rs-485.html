RS-485 (TIA-485)

Differential multi-drop serial bus supporting long cables (up to 1200 m) and noisy environments.

Novice definition

RS-485 is a rugged serial cable standard that uses two twisted wires to carry signals cleanly across buildings or factories.

Learner definition

Because RS-485 uses differential signaling over twisted pair with 120-ohm termination, it resists electrical noise far better than RS-232.

Expert definition

TIA/EIA-485 balanced half/full-duplex multipoint differential bus (A/B lines, common-mode -7 V to +12 V, 120-ohm termination).
