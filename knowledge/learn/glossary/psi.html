PSI (Pressure Stall Information)

Linux kernel metrics (/proc/pressure/*) measuring time tasks spend waiting for CPU, memory, or I/O.

Novice definition

PSI is Linux's built-in slowdown meter that shows whether your computer is actually bogged down waiting for RAM, CPU, or disk.

Learner definition

Read /proc/pressure/memory to see 'some' and 'full' stall percentages; systemd-oomd watches PSI to catch thrashing early.

Expert definition

Kernel accounting (kernel/sched/psi.c) exposing 10s/60s/300s averages of task stall time across cpu, memory, and io.
