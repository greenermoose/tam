CDH (Computational Diffie-Hellman)

Hardness assumption that computing g^(ab) from g^a and g^b is infeasible.

Novice definition

CDH is the math puzzle behind Diffie-Hellman key exchange: knowing two public shares does not let you compute the shared secret.

Learner definition

CDH is weaker (easier to break, harder to assume) than DDH and is implied by the Discrete Logarithm Problem (DLP).

Expert definition

Assumption in a cyclic group G that given (g, g^a, g^b), computing g^(ab) is hard for PPT adversaries.
