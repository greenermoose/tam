Bit (binary digit)

The smallest unit of digital information.

Novice definition

A bit is a single yes-or-no value, written as 1 or 0.

Learner definition

A bit is one binary digit; eight bits make a byte, and bits are grouped to encode numbers, text, and instructions.

Expert definition

A bit is a base-2 digit with two states, implemented physically by voltage, charge, magnetization, or optical intensity and interpreted by digital logic.
