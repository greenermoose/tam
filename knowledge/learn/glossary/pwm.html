PWM

Rapid switching used to control average power.

Novice definition

PWM is a way of dimming lights or controlling motors by switching power on and off very quickly.

Learner definition

Pulse-width modulation varies the duty cycle of a square wave to control average voltage or power.

Expert definition

PWM is a modulation technique encoding a value in the duty cycle of a fixed-frequency pulse train, filtered by inertia or capacitance.
