Journal

A log of events or pending changes.

Novice definition

A journal is a record that the system keeps of what happened or what is about to change.

Learner definition

A journal can record system events or filesystem transactions so recovery and diagnosis are possible.

Expert definition

A journal is an append-only ordered record used for logging, crash recovery, or transactional metadata, depending on the subsystem.
