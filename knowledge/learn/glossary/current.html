Current

Current is the rate at which electric charge flows through a conductive path.

Novice definition

The stream of tiny electric charges (electrons) moving through a wire, measured in amperes or amps. Current can only flow when there is a complete, unbroken circuit loop for the charges to travel around.

Learner definition

The quantity of electrical charge passing a given cross-section of a conductor per second (one ampere equals one coulomb per second). Under Ohm's law, current equals voltage divided by resistance.

Expert definition

The macroscopic drift of mobile charge carriers through a conducting medium under an applied electric field, governed by charge density and carrier mobility. Current flowing through resistance dissipates thermal energy proportional to the square of current multiplied by resistance.
