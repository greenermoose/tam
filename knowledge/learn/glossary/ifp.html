IFP (Integer Factorization Problem)

Math problem of factoring a large composite number N = pq into its prime factors, underlying RSA.

Novice definition

Integer factorization is the puzzle of splitting a huge number back into the two prime numbers multiplied to make it.

Learner definition

RSA security relies on IFP being hard classically (requiring 2048-3072+ bit keys), though Shor's algorithm solves it on a quantum computer.

Expert definition

Given N = pq for large primes p, q, recover p and q; classical complexity is subexponential via GNFS.
