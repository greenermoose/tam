QAM (Quadrature Amplitude Modulation)

Modulation encoding multiple bits per symbol by varying both carrier amplitude and phase.

Novice definition

QAM packs more bits into each radio wave pulse by changing both its height (amplitude) and timing shift (phase).

Learner definition

Wi-Fi scales from 16-QAM up to 1024-QAM or 4096-QAM when signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) is high.

Expert definition

Two-dimensional I/Q constellation modulation (M-QAM carrying log2(M) bits/symbol); higher M requires exponentially higher SNR.
