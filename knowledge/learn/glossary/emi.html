EMI (Electromagnetic Interference)

Unwanted electrical noise radiated or conducted between circuits and cables.

Novice definition

EMI is stray electrical noise from nearby wires, motors, or power supplies that can scramble data on unshielded cables.

Learner definition

Twisted-pair differential cables, ferrite beads, and metal shielding reduce EMI pickup and emissions.

Expert definition

Conducted or radiated electromagnetic coupling degrading signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and causing bit errors or HPD glitches.
