RF - Radio Frequency

Radio-frequency signals used for wireless communication.

Novice definition

RF means radio waves, the invisible signals used by Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and phones.

Learner definition

Radio frequency refers to alternating signals in the radio spectrum, roughly 3 kHz to 300 GHz, used for wireless links.

Expert definition

RF is electromagnetic signaling in the radio band, characterized by frequency, wavelength, power, modulation, and impedance matching.
