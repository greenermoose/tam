Mandatory Access Control (MAC)

System-wide security rules enforced by the kernel that even resource owners cannot override.

Novice definition

Even if a file belongs to you, a building-wide rule can still forbid taking top-secret pages out of the vault room.

Learner definition

Linux Security Modules such as SELinux and AppArmor enforce MAC policies that restrict what a process can touch, even if that process runs with normal file-owner permissions.

Expert definition

Centralized policy enforcement over security labels (classifications, categories, or types) where subjects cannot alter policy or propagate permissions; formalizes Bell–LaPadula confidentiality, Biba integrity, and Type Enforcement.
