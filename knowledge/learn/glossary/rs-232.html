RS-232 (Recommended Standard 232)

Classic single-ended serial port standard (+/-3 V to +/-15 V) used for consoles and modems.

Novice definition

RS-232 is the classic computer serial port standard used for consoles, industrial gear, and older modems.

Learner definition

Unlike 3.3 V TTL UART pins, RS-232 uses inverted +/-12 V signals, so connecting them directly without a transceiver chip damages UARTs.

Expert definition

EIA/TIA-232 point-to-point asynchronous serial interface (mark = -3..-15 V, space = +3..+15 V) over DB-9 / DE-9.
