Clipboard

A temporary place for copied data.

Novice definition

The clipboard is where copied text or images wait until you paste them.

Learner definition

A clipboard stores copied data for pasting, often as multiple MIME types negotiated between applications.

Expert definition

A clipboard is a selection or data-transfer protocol with ownership, MIME offers, and persistence managed by the compositor or toolkit.
