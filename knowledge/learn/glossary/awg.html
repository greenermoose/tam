AWG (American Wire Gauge)

Standardized wire-diameter numbering system where smaller numbers mean thicker copper.

Novice definition

AWG measures how thick a wire is; counterintuitively, a smaller AWG number (like 20 AWG) is thicker than 28 AWG.

Learner definition

Thicker wires (lower AWG) have lower electrical resistance and carry higher USB-PD current with less voltage drop.

Expert definition

Logarithmic diameter standard where every 3 AWG step halves/doubles cross-sectional area and DC resistance per unit length.
