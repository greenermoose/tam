Decode

Turning an encoded instruction or signal into meaning.

Novice definition

To decode is to work out what a coded message or instruction means.

Learner definition

Decoding interprets an instruction's opcode and operands, or converts an encoded signal back into data.

Expert definition

Decode is the pipeline stage that parses instruction fields, resolves operands, and drives execution controls; in media it reverses an encoding.
