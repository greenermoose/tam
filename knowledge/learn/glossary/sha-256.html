SHA-256

256-bit member of the SHA-2 cryptographic hash family.

Novice definition

SHA-256 creates a unique 256-bit (64-hex-character) fingerprint for any file or message.

Learner definition

Part of SHA algorithms, SHA-256 verifies Arch packages, TLS certificates, and Tamlinux build digests.

Expert definition

256-bit SHA-2 digest (FIPS 180-4): 64 rounds over 512-bit blocks, offering 128-bit collision and 256-bit preimage resistance.
