Parallel Communication

Parallel communication is transmitting multiple bits simultaneously across separate physical wires.

Novice definition

Sending information like multiple cars driving side-by-side on an eight-lane highway: an entire byte (8 bits) travels at the exact same moment across eight separate wires. Older printers and disk drives used wide ribbon cables for parallel communication.

Learner definition

A transmission architecture where multiple bits travel simultaneously across a bus of parallel electrical conductors synchronized to a common clock. At high speeds, physical variations in wire length cause clock skew, where bits arrive at slightly different times, setting an upper frequency limit.

Expert definition

Wide-bus synchronous architectures (such as legacy PCI, ATA/IDE, and modern DDR4/DDR5 DRAM interfaces). While retired from external cabling due to skew and electromagnetic crosstalk, parallel buses remain essential for short-distance, tightly length-matched on-die and memory-channel routing.
