JWT (JSON Web Token)

Compact, URL-safe token format carrying signed identity claims between services (RFC 7519).

Novice definition

A JWT is a signed digital pass—written in JSON—that proves who you are and what you are allowed to do.

Learner definition

Used in OIDC and web APIs, a JWT carries claims (subject, issuer, expiration) protected by a JWS signature.

Expert definition

IETF RFC 7519 claims object encoded in JWS/JWE; verifiers must validate alg, iss, aud, exp, and signature.
