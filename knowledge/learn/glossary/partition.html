Partition

A defined section of a storage device.

Novice definition

A partition is a slice of a disk that the operating system treats as a separate area.

Learner definition

A partition is a contiguous block range described by a partition table, holding a filesystem or other volume.

Expert definition

A partition is a table entry mapping logical block addresses and attributes; GPT and MBR are common partition-table formats.
