NIST P-256 (secp256r1)

Widely used 256-bit elliptic curve for digital signatures and key exchange.

Novice definition

NIST P-256 is a standard mathematical curve used to create compact, fast encryption keys and digital signatures.

Learner definition

Commonly used in TLS certificates, SSH keys, and FIDO2 passkeys, P-256 provides roughly 128 bits of classical security.

Expert definition

NIST-standardized prime-field elliptic curve (also secp256r1 / prime256v1) over F_p with p = 2^256 - 2^224 + 2^192 + 2^96 - 1, used in ECDSA and ECDH.
