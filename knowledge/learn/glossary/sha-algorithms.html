SHA algorithms (SHA-1, SHA-2, SHA-256, SHA-3, SHA3-256, SHA384)

NIST-standardized family of cryptographic hash functions.

Novice definition

SHA is a family of standard mathematical 'fingerprinting' functions used to verify downloads, passwords, and certificates.

Learner definition

The SHA family includes deprecated SHA-1, the widely used SHA-2 (SHA-256, SHA-384), and sponge-based SHA-3 (SHA3-256).

Expert definition

NIST Secure Hash Algorithm family: SHA-1 (FIPS 180-4, broken), SHA-2 (Merkle-Damgard, FIPS 180-4), and SHA-3 (Keccak sponge, FIPS 202).
