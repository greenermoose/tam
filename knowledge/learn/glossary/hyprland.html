Hyprland

Hyprland is the tiling Wayland compositor Tamlinux uses today for its graphical desktop.

Beginner definition

Hyprland arranges windows in tiles, reads Meta-key shortcuts, and talks to Wayland clients. Tamlinux packages Hyprland with its own binding files. A planned future target is Sway; until that lands, Hyprland is the live compositor.

Intermediate definition

Hyprland is an independent Wayland compositor with its own configuration language and plugin ABI. Tamlinux bindings live under the product session tree and must be checked against those files, not against stock Hyprland defaults or another distribution's chords.

Expert definition

The current Tamlinux Wayland compositor; configuration and bindings are product-specific overlays on upstream Hyprland.
