IND-CPA (Indistinguishability under Chosen-Plaintext Attack)

Baseline semantic security property against passive eavesdroppers who can choose plaintexts.

Novice definition

IND-CPA means an attacker cannot tell which of two messages was encrypted, even if they get to see other messages encrypted.

Learner definition

Randomized encryption (using a fresh IV or nonce) is required for IND-CPA; deterministic modes like ECB fail it.

Expert definition

Left-or-right indistinguishability game with an encryption oracle only; weaker than IND-CCA2 and INT-CTXT.
