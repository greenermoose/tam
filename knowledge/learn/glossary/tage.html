TAGE (TAgged GEometric history length)

State-of-the-art CPU branch-predictor algorithm using multiple geometric history tables.

Novice definition

TAGE is the clever pattern-matching design modern CPUs use to guess conditional branches with over 99% accuracy.

Learner definition

By combining short and very long branch-history tables with tag matching, TAGE captures complex loop and call patterns.

Expert definition

Seznec & Michaud branch predictor indexing tagged tables by exponentially increasing global branch-history lengths.
