INT-CTXT (Integrity of Ciphertext)

Security property ensuring an attacker cannot forge a valid new ciphertext.

Novice definition

INT-CTXT means an attacker cannot craft a fake encrypted message that decrypts without being caught.

Learner definition

Combining IND-CPA encryption with INT-CTXT authentication (as AEAD does) yields IND-CCA2 security.

Expert definition

Cryptographic notion requiring negligible probability of producing a fresh ciphertext that verifies under decryption.
