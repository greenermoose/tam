SIGSTOP (Stop signal)

Uncatchable signal (19) that pauses a process until SIGCONT is sent.

Novice definition

SIGSTOP immediately freezes a running program without closing it, until you resume it later.

Learner definition

Part of Unix signals, SIGSTOP cannot be caught or ignored; unlike Ctrl+Z (SIGTSTP), the program cannot intercept it.

Expert definition

POSIX signal (19 on x86_64 Linux) that suspends process execution in the kernel until SIGCONT.
