UART - Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter

A simple serial port circuit.

Novice definition

A UART is the part that turns bytes into a serial stream of bits and back again.

Learner definition

A universal asynchronous receiver-transmitter frames bytes with start, data, parity, and stop bits at an agreed rate.

Expert definition

A UART is an asynchronous serial controller with shift registers, baud divisor, FIFO, and line-status detection.
