EUF-CMA (Existential Unforgeability under Chosen-Message Attack)

Standard security definition for digital signatures and MACs.

Novice definition

EUF-CMA means even if an attacker sees signatures on many messages they choose, they cannot forge a signature on any new message.

Learner definition

Ed25519, RSA-PSS, ECDSA, and ML-DSA are all designed to satisfy EUF-CMA.

Expert definition

Standard signature/MAC security game: no PPT adversary with a signing oracle can output a valid (m*, sigma*) for any m* not previously queried.
