Screenshot

A capture of what is on screen.

Novice definition

A screenshot is a picture of your screen at one moment.

Learner definition

A screenshot captures framebuffer or compositor output, optionally a window or region, into an image.

Expert definition

A screenshot is a compositor or display-server capture using shared memory, DMA-BUF, or screencopy protocols, with color and cursor handling.
