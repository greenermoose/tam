APIC

An interrupt controller used in modern PCs.

Novice definition

An APIC is the chip or block that routes interrupts to the right processor core.

Learner definition

The advanced programmable interrupt controller delivers interrupts in multiprocessor systems, often with per-core local APICs.

Expert definition

An APIC is a programmable interrupt controller architecture with local and I/O units, vectors, priorities, and inter-processor interrupts.
