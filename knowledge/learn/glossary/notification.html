Notification

A short message from an application.

Novice definition

A notification is a pop-up or banner that tells you something happened.

Learner definition

A desktop notification is an asynchronous message shown by a notification daemon, often with actions and urgency.

Expert definition

A notification is a freedesktop.org D-Bus message with app name, summary, body, hints, actions, and expiration, rendered by a daemon.
