DWDM (Dense Wavelength-Division Multiplexing)

Fiber-optic technique sending dozens of closely spaced laser wavelengths down one glass strand.

Novice definition

DWDM sends many different colors of laser light down a single optical fiber at the same time to multiply capacity.

Learner definition

Each wavelength channel in DWDM carries independent 10G/100G/400G data streams over long-haul single-mode fiber.

Expert definition

ITU-T G.694.1 50/100 GHz C-band optical multiplexing packing 40-96+ channels onto SMF.
