LLL (Lenstra-Lenstra-Lovasz)

Polynomial-time lattice basis-reduction algorithm.

Novice definition

LLL is a classic math algorithm for finding short vectors in a grid, used to test cryptographic schemes.

Learner definition

LLL runs in polynomial time and is the foundation for stronger lattice attacks like BKZ.

Expert definition

1982 lattice basis-reduction algorithm finding a delta-reduced basis in polynomial time with approximation factor 2^((n-1)/2).
