AIEE (American Institute of Electrical Engineers)

Historical US electrical-engineering society that merged with IRE in 1963 to form IEEE.

Novice definition

AIEE was the early engineering group that merged in 1963 to become IEEE, the organization behind Ethernet and Wi-Fi standards.

Learner definition

Understanding AIEE's 1963 merger with the IRE explains why IEEE standards span both power and radio/digital communications.

Expert definition

American Institute of Electrical Engineers (1884-1963); merged with Institute of Radio Engineers (IRE) to form IEEE.
