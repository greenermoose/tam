Touchpad

A flat pointing surface on a laptop.

Novice definition

A touchpad is the flat pad below a laptop keyboard that moves the pointer.

Learner definition

A touchpad is a capacitive pointing device reporting absolute contacts, pressure, and gestures.

Expert definition

A touchpad is an input device using capacitive sensing and HID multitouch reports, with palm rejection and gesture processing.
