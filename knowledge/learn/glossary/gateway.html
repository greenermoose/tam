Gateway

The router that connects a local network to other networks.

Novice definition

A gateway is the device a computer sends traffic to when the destination is outside its own network.

Learner definition

A gateway is a next-hop router used for destinations not covered by local routes.

Expert definition

A gateway is a routing next hop, often the default route, that forwards packets between networks and may perform NAT or filtering.
