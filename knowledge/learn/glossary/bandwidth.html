Bandwidth

The maximum rate data can flow.

Novice definition

Bandwidth is how much data a connection can carry per second.

Learner definition

Bandwidth is the capacity of a link or channel, often measured in bits per second.

Expert definition

Bandwidth is the maximum information rate of a channel under its physical and protocol constraints, distinct from achieved throughput.
