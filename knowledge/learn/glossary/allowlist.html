Allowlist

A list of things that are explicitly permitted.

Novice definition

An allowlist names the programs or addresses that are allowed, and everything else is blocked.

Learner definition

An allowlist implements default-deny policy by enumerating permitted items.

Expert definition

An allowlist is a positive security policy with explicit entries and default denial, contrasted with a denylist.
