Byte

A group of eight bits treated as one unit.

Novice definition

A byte is eight bits, enough to hold one small number or one character of text.

Learner definition

A byte is the usual addressable unit of memory and storage; sizes such as KiB and MiB count bytes.

Expert definition

A byte is eight bits on modern systems, although historical machines used other widths; C guarantees at least eight bits per byte.
