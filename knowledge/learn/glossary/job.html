Shell job

Beginner definition

A command or pipeline managed by your shell.

Intermediate definition

A shell job can contain several processes and can run in foreground or background.

Expert definition

A shell bookkeeping and terminal-control unit, commonly organized with a process group. Job numbers belong to that shell, whereas process IDs have different identity and lifetime rules.
