Glyph

The drawn shape of a character.

Novice definition

A glyph is the actual shape of a letter or symbol as it appears on screen or paper.

Learner definition

A glyph is a visual representation of a character or character sequence in a font.

Expert definition

A glyph is a font outline or bitmap indexed by glyph ID, mapped from characters through cmap and shaping rules.
