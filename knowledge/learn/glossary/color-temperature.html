Color temperature

A measure of how warm or cool white light looks.

Novice definition

Color temperature describes whether white light looks reddish and warm or bluish and cool, measured in kelvin.

Learner definition

Color temperature is the blackbody temperature whose hue approximates a white point, such as 6500 K for daylight.

Expert definition

Color temperature is a correlated color temperature in kelvin, used with white-point chromaticity and display calibration.
