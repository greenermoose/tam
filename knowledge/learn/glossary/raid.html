RAID - Redundant Array of Independent Disks

Combining disks for speed, capacity, or redundancy.

Novice definition

RAID is a way of combining several disks so they are faster, larger, or survive a disk failure.

Learner definition

RAID distributes data across disks with mirroring, striping, or parity to trade capacity, performance, and fault tolerance.

Expert definition

RAID is a storage virtualization scheme using striping, mirroring, and parity across member devices, implemented by hardware or mdraid.
