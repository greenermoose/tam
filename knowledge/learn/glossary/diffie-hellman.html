Diffie-Hellman

A method for two parties to agree on a shared secret over a public channel.

Novice definition

Diffie-Hellman lets two people mix their own secret colors with a shared paint color in public so both end up with the same secret shade that onlookers cannot reconstruct.

Learner definition

Diffie-Hellman key exchange (including elliptic-curve ECDH such as X25519) lets a client and server derive a shared session key without ever transmitting it.

Expert definition

Key-agreement protocol in a cyclic group G of prime order q relying on the Computational and Decisional Diffie-Hellman assumptions; ephemeral DH provides forward secrecy when authenticated.
