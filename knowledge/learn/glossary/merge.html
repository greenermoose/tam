Merge

Combining two lines of development.

Novice definition

To merge is to bring changes from one branch into another.

Learner definition

A merge integrates histories, creating a merge commit when the branches diverged.

Expert definition

A merge computes a common ancestor and combines trees, with conflict resolution and possibly a multi-parent commit.
