RLL (Run-Length Limited)

Line coding bounding the minimum and maximum number of identical bits between transitions.

Novice definition

RLL coding guarantees a wire or disk signal flips often enough for the reader to stay in sync, without flipping too fast.

Learner definition

8b/10b and 64b/66b scrambling are modern forms of run-length limiting for serial links.

Expert definition

(d, k) constrained channel coding bounding consecutive zeros between 1-transitions to prevent clock drift and ISI.
