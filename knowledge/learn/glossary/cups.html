CUPS (Common Unix Printing System)

Standard modular printing service on Linux handling print queues, IPP, and printer drivers.

Novice definition

CUPS is the Linux printing system that sends documents from your apps to local or network printers.

Learner definition

Managed via cups.service and http://localhost:631, CUPS supports driverless IPP Everywhere printers.

Expert definition

OpenPrinting CUPS daemon (cupsd) scheduling IPP jobs through filter chains (PDF -> PWG/URF raster) to backends.
