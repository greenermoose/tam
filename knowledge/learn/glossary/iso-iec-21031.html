ISO/IEC 21031 (Software Carbon Intensity)

International standard for measuring software carbon intensity (SCI).

Novice definition

ISO/IEC 21031 is a standard for estimating how much carbon pollution a piece of software causes per unit of work.

Learner definition

Originating from the Green Software Foundation, ISO/IEC 21031 defines the SCI score by combining energy use, grid carbon intensity, and embodied hardware emissions.

Expert definition

ISO/IEC 21031:2024 specifies SCI = ((E * I) + M) / R, where E is energy consumed, I is location-based marginal emissions, M is amortized embodied emissions, and R is the functional unit.
