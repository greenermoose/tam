OVP (Over-Voltage Protection)

Circuit safeguard that shuts down or clamps power when voltage exceeds safe limits.

Novice definition

OVP is a safety circuit in chargers and motherboards that cuts power if voltage spikes too high.

Learner definition

USB-PD controllers and PSUs include OVP alongside over-current and ESD protection.

Expert definition

Hardware comparator/crowbar/MOSFET-disconnect stage tripping above V_ovp threshold to protect downstream silicon.
