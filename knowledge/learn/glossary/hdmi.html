HDMI

HDMI (High-Definition Multimedia Interface) is a proprietary standard for transmitting uncompressed digital video and multi-channel audio over a single cable.

Novice definition

A trapezoid-shaped cable connector commonly used to connect computers, game consoles, and media players to televisions and computer monitors.

Learner definition

A digital audiovisual standard developed jointly by consumer electronics manufacturers. It integrates uncompressed video, digital audio, Consumer Electronics Control (CEC) commands, and content protection (HDCP) into one cable connection.

Expert definition

A digital interface transmitting serialized data over Transition-Minimized Differential Signaling (TMDS) across three data channels and one clock line (in HDMI 1.0 through 2.0), or Fixed Rate Link (FRL) packetized signaling across up to four differential lanes in HDMI 2.1 (delivering up to 48 Gbps aggregate throughput). HDMI includes sidebands for DDC (EDID), CEC, and eARC (Enhanced Audio Return Channel).
