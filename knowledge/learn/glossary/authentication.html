Authentication

Proving who you are.

Novice definition

Authentication is how a computer checks that you are really you, usually with a password or key.

Learner definition

Authentication verifies an identity using something you know, have, or are.

Expert definition

Authentication is the verifiable establishment of a principal identity through credentials, challenges, certificates, or tokens.
