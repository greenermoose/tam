KEX (Key Exchange)

Cryptographic handshake step where two parties establish a shared secret over an untrusted network.

Novice definition

Key exchange (KEX) is how two computers agree on a shared secret key over the internet without an eavesdropper learning it.

Learner definition

SSH and TLS run a KEX step (such as X25519 or hybrid ML-KEM+X25519) before encrypting data.

Expert definition

Diffie-Hellman or KEM-based key-establishment protocol (e.g., SSH KEXINIT / RFC 4253, TLS 1.3 KeyShare).
