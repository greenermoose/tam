YUV

A color model that separates brightness from color.

Novice definition

YUV stores brightness separately from color, which saves bandwidth in video.

Learner definition

YUV represents luma and two chroma components, often subsampled for video compression.

Expert definition

YUV is a family of luma-chroma color representations with BT.601/709/2020 matrices, chroma subsampling, and limited or full range.
