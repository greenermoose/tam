Regular expression

A compact pattern for matching text.

Novice definition

A regular expression is a pattern that describes what text to find, such as a date or an email address.

Learner definition

A regular expression is a formal pattern language with literals, classes, anchors, and repetition, used by grep and many tools.

Expert definition

A regular expression is an expression in a regular language with quantifiers, alternation, and grouping; POSIX BRE, ERE, and PCRE differ in syntax.
