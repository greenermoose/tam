BSD (Berkeley Software Distribution)

Family of open-source Unix operating systems (FreeBSD, OpenBSD, NetBSD, DragonFly BSD) and permissive licenses.

Novice definition

BSD is a family of free, open-source Unix-like operating systems developed from UC Berkeley's Unix work.

Learner definition

Unlike Linux (which is just a kernel), FreeBSD and OpenBSD develop their kernel and userland tools together in one repository.

Expert definition

Descendants of 4.4BSD-Lite (FreeBSD, OpenBSD, NetBSD, DragonFly BSD) and the permissive 2-clause/3-clause BSD licenses.
