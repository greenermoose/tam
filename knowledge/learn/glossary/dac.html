Discretionary Access Control (DAC)

An access model where the owner of a resource decides who may access it.

Novice definition

If you own a notebook, you decide which friends are allowed to read it or write in it.

Learner definition

Standard Unix file permissions (chmod) and POSIX ACLs are DAC: a file's owner can grant or revoke read, write, and execute rights for other users and groups.

Expert definition

An access-matrix model in which subjects possessing ownership or control rights over an object may propagate access rights to other subjects at their discretion—leaving DAC vulnerable to Trojan-horse information flow (motivating MAC).
