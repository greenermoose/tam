BEAST (Browser Exploit Against SSL/TLS)

2011 chosen-plaintext attack against predictable IVs in TLS 1.0 CBC mode.

Novice definition

BEAST was a 2011 attack on old TLS 1.0 encryption that showed why predictable initialization vectors are unsafe.

Learner definition

BEAST exploited chained CBC IVs in TLS 1.0; modern TLS 1.2/1.3 use AEAD ciphers like AES-GCM instead.

Expert definition

Chosen-plaintext blockwise adaptive attack on TLS 1.0 CBC chained IVs, mitigated by TLS 1.1+ explicit IVs and AEAD.
