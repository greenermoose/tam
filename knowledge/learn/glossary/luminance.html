Luminance

The brightness of light emitted or reflected.

Novice definition

Luminance is how bright a surface or display appears.

Learner definition

Luminance measures luminous intensity per unit area, in candelas per square meter.

Expert definition

Luminance is photometric radiance weighted by the human luminosity function, measured in cd/m² for displays.
