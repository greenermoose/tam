ISI (Inter-Symbol Interference)

Signal distortion where one transmitted pulse smears into neighboring bit slots.

Novice definition

ISI happens when fast electrical pulses blur together on a cable so one bit bleeds into the next.

Learner definition

Cable loss and reflections cause ISI, which closes the 'eye diagram' and is countered by CTLE and DFE.

Expert definition

Channel-dispersion distortion where pulse-response tails overlap adjacent symbol intervals, raising BER.
