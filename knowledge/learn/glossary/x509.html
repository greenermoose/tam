X.509

The standard digital certificate format used by TLS, HTTPS, and public-key infrastructure.

Novice definition

X.509 is the standard layout for a website's digital passport, listing the site's name, its public key, expiration dates, and the issuer's signature.

Learner definition

An X.509 certificate binds a subject identity (such as a DNS hostname in Subject Alternative Name) to a public key, encoded in binary DER or text PEM format.

Expert definition

ITU-T / RFC 5280 ASN.1 DER certificate profile containing tbsCertificate (version, serial, issuer, validity, subject, SPKI, extensions like KeyUsage and SAN) and signatureValue.
