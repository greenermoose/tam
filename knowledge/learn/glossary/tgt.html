TGT (Ticket-Granting Ticket)

Initial Kerberos credential allowing a user to request service tickets without re-entering a password.

Novice definition

A TGT is a master login pass in Kerberos that lets your computer fetch access tickets for individual services automatically.

Learner definition

After you authenticate with kinit, the KDC issues a time-limited TGT cached locally for single sign-on.

Expert definition

Kerberos V5 credential issued by the AS-REQ/AS-REP exchange and presented to the TGS to obtain service tickets.
