DPCD (DisplayPort Configuration Data)

Register space inside a DisplayPort monitor read/written over the AUX channel during link training.

Novice definition

DPCD is a small table of settings inside a DisplayPort monitor that your GPU reads to negotiate cable speed.

Learner definition

Linux DRM drivers read DPCD registers over the DisplayPort AUX channel to check lane count, link rate, and DSC support.

Expert definition

VESA DisplayPort AUX-channel address space (0x00000+) storing receiver capabilities, link-training status, and PSR/DSC controls.
