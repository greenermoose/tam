Unix signals (SIGINT, SIGTERM, SIGKILL, SIGSTOP, SIGQUIT, SIGTSTP, SIGPIPE)

Asynchronous kernel notifications sent to processes to interrupt, pause, or terminate them.

Novice definition

Signals are short messages the Linux kernel sends to running programs—such as telling a program to stop, pause, or exit cleanly.

Learner definition

Common signals include SIGINT, SIGTERM, SIGKILL, SIGSTOP, SIGQUIT, SIGTSTP, and SIGPIPE.

Expert definition

POSIX asynchronous/synchronous process notifications delivered by the kernel; processes may catch or ignore most signals via sigaction(2), except SIGKILL and SIGSTOP.
