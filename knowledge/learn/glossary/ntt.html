NTT (Number Theoretic Transform)

Fast Fourier Transform over finite fields used to multiply polynomials in lattice cryptography.

Novice definition

NTT is a fast math trick that makes post-quantum lattice encryption (like ML-KEM) run in microseconds.

Learner definition

By transforming ring polynomials into point-value form mod q, NTT reduces multiplication from O(n^2) to O(n log n).

Expert definition

Discrete Fourier transform over Z_q[X]/(X^n + 1) using primitive roots of unity; core performance engine of ML-KEM and ML-DSA.
