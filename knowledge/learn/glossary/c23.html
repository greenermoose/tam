C23 (ISO C23)

2024 C language revision adding nullptr, constexpr, #embed, and bit utilities.

Novice definition

C23 is the 2024 update to the C language, adding modern conveniences like nullptr and binary #embed.

Learner definition

Part of the C standards family; Tamlinux avoids requiring C23 yet because older rescue toolchains may not support it.

Expert definition

ISO/IEC 9899:2024; introduces nullptr, typeof, constexpr, [[...]] attributes, <stdbit.h>, and removes K&R function definitions.
