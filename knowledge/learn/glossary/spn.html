SPN (Substitution-Permutation Network)

Block-cipher architecture alternating non-linear byte substitutions (S-boxes) and bit permutations.

Novice definition

An SPN is the layered 'mix and scramble' structure used inside modern ciphers like AES.

Learner definition

Each SPN round applies AddRoundKey, SubBytes (confusion), and ShiftRows/MixColumns (diffusion).

Expert definition

Shannon confusion-diffusion iterated block-cipher structure (AES, PRESENT), contrasted with Feistel networks (DES).
