HBR2 (High Bit Rate 2)

DisplayPort 1.2 5.4 Gbps per-lane link rate supporting 4K at 60 Hz.

Novice definition

HBR2 doubles DisplayPort speed to 5.4 Gbps per lane, enabling 4K monitors at 60 Hz.

Learner definition

Part of DisplayPort link rates, HBR2 provides 17.28 Gbps usable bandwidth over 4 lanes.

Expert definition

DisplayPort 1.2 5.40 Gbps/lane (8b/10b encoding) with TPS3 link-training equalization.
