VLSI (Very-Large-Scale Integration)

Fabrication of microchips packing millions to billions of transistors onto one silicon die.

Novice definition

VLSI is the engineering behind cramming billions of microscopic transistors onto a single CPU or GPU chip.

Learner definition

Modern CMOS VLSI design uses automated synthesis and place-and-route tools to build complex processors.

Expert definition

IC design methodology integrating 10^5 to 10^11+ MOSFETs on monolithic silicon dies.
