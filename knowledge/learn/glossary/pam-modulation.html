PAM modulation (PAM-2, PAM-4, PAM-5)

Pulse-Amplitude Modulation encoding 1 or more bits per electrical voltage level.

Novice definition

Pulse-Amplitude Modulation (PAM) sends data over wires by stepping voltage between 2, 4, or 5 distinct levels.

Learner definition

High-speed buses step up from PAM-2 (binary) to PAM-4 (PCIe 6.0, DisplayPort/Ethernet) and PAM-5 (Gigabit Ethernet).

Expert definition

Multi-level baseband signaling (PAM-2, PAM-4, PAM-5) trading SNR margin for doubled spectral efficiency.
