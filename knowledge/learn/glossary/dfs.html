DFS (Dynamic Frequency Selection)

Wi-Fi 5 GHz mechanism that detects weather/aviation radar and vacates shared channels.

Novice definition

DFS lets Wi-Fi routers use extra 5 GHz channels as long as they listen and move away if nearby radar appears.

Learner definition

If a 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection drops briefly for channel scanning, DFS radar avoidance may be active.

Expert definition

IEEE 802.11h / regulatory 5 GHz UNII-2 mechanism requiring Channel Availability Check (CAC) and In-Service Monitoring.
