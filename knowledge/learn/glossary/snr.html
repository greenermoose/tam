SNR - Signal-to-Noise Ratio

The strength of a signal compared with background noise.

Novice definition

SNR compares how loud the useful signal is with the noise around it.

Learner definition

Signal-to-noise ratio is signal power divided by noise power, usually expressed in decibels.

Expert definition

SNR is the ratio of signal power to noise power at a point in a channel, determining the achievable error rate and capacity.
