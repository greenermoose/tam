SMPS (Switched-Mode Power Supply)

High-efficiency power converter using fast-switching transistors and inductors instead of linear heat dissipation.

Novice definition

An SMPS is the efficient modern power supply inside laptop chargers and PCs that switches power on and off thousands of times a second.

Learner definition

Unlike hot linear regulators, SMPS converters achieve 85-95%+ efficiency, though they require output filtering to limit switching ripple.

Expert definition

High-frequency (kHz-MHz) MOSFET/GaN converter (buck, boost, flyback, LLC) regulating voltage via PWM duty cycle.
