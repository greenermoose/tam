Post-quantum cryptography

Classical algorithms designed to resist attacks by both ordinary and quantum computers.

Novice definition

Post-quantum (or quantum-safe) cryptography runs on your normal laptop today, using new math puzzles that even future quantum computers cannot solve quickly.

Learner definition

Post-quantum algorithms such as ML-KEM and ML-DSA replace RSA and elliptic-curve key exchange/signatures, often paired in hybrid modes like X25519MLKEM768.

Expert definition

Cryptographic primitives running on classical hardware whose hardness assumptions (e.g., Module-LWE, stateless hash-based Merkle trees, syndrome decoding) have no known polynomial-time quantum algorithm.
