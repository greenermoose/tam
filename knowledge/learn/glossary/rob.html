ROB (Reorder Buffer)

CPU hardware queue that lets instructions execute out of order while committing results in program order.

Novice definition

The Reorder Buffer is a CPU waiting room that lets fast instructions finish early while still putting final results in the exact right order.

Learner definition

Larger ROBs allow CPUs to look further ahead for parallel work, though speculative results are squashed on branch misprediction.

Expert definition

Circular microarchitectural buffer tracking in-flight micro-ops for precise exceptions and in-order architectural retirement.
