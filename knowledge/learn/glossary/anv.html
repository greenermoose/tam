ANV (Intel Vulkan Mesa Driver)

Open-source userspace Vulkan graphics driver for Intel GPUs in Mesa.

Novice definition

ANV is the open-source Linux graphics driver that runs modern Vulkan games and apps on Intel GPUs.

Learner definition

Part of Mesa alongside Iris (OpenGL), ANV talks to the kernel i915 or xe DRM driver.

Expert definition

Mesa Vulkan ICD for Intel Gen7+ / Xe GPUs interfacing with kernel DRM/KMS via GEM/execbuf ioctls.
