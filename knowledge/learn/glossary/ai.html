AI - Artificial Intelligence

Software that performs tasks associated with human intelligence.

Novice definition

AI is software that can recognize patterns, answer questions, or generate text and images.

Learner definition

Artificial intelligence covers machine-learning systems that infer patterns from data, including language and vision models.

Expert definition

AI is the field and set of systems performing inference or learning, from symbolic methods to neural networks, with accuracy, bias, and resource limits.
