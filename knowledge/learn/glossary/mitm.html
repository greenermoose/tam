MITM (Man-in-the-Middle)

Network attack where an adversary secretly intercepts and alters communication between two parties.

Novice definition

A man-in-the-middle attack happens when someone sits secretly between you and a server, reading or changing your messages.

Learner definition

TLS certificates and SSH host-key verification protect against MITM attackers impersonating the server.

Expert definition

Active adversary-in-the-middle intercepting/rewriting traffic; mitigated by authenticated key exchange (X.509 PKI, SSH host keys).
