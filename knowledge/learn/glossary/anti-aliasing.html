Anti-aliasing

Softening jagged edges by blending colors.

Novice definition

Anti-aliasing smooths the stair-step edges of text and shapes so they look less jagged.

Learner definition

Anti-aliasing samples coverage at pixel edges and blends foreground and background colors.

Expert definition

Anti-aliasing approximates partial pixel coverage using supersampling, grayscale, or subpixel rendering and coverage masks.
