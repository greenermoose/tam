Z80 (Zilog Z80)

Classic 1976 8-bit microprocessor widely used in early personal computers, CP/M, and embedded systems.

Novice definition

The Z80 is a famous 1976 8-bit processor that ran early home computers and still lives on inside embedded hardware.

Learner definition

Hardware from the 8-bit Z80 era shows how much software efficiency mattered when RAM was measured in kilobytes.

Expert definition

8-bit CISC microprocessor (binary-compatible superset of Intel 8080) with 16-bit address space and shadow register set.
