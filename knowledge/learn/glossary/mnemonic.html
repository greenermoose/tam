Mnemonic

A short name for an instruction or operation.

Novice definition

A mnemonic is a short word like add or mov that stands for one machine instruction.

Learner definition

A mnemonic is the human-readable spelling of an opcode in assembly language, such as mov, add, or jmp.

Expert definition

A mnemonic is an assembler token mapped to an opcode and operand form, often overloaded by instruction set and syntax mode.
