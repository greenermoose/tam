AES-NI (AES New Instructions)

x86_64 CPU instruction-set extension accelerating AES encryption in constant time.

Novice definition

AES-NI is a built-in CPU feature that makes AES encryption much faster and avoids timing leaks.

Learner definition

Part of AES variants, AES-NI executes AES rounds in dedicated silicon rather than lookup tables.

Expert definition

Intel/AMD x86_64 instruction extension (AESENC, AESDEC, PCLMULQDQ) providing constant-time, cache-timing-resistant AES and GCM.
