AES-256

Advanced Encryption Standard using a 256-bit key and 14 rounds.

Novice definition

AES-256 is the strongest standard version of AES, using a 256-bit secret key for extra margin against future computers.

Learner definition

Part of AES variants, AES-256 is used for LUKS2 disk encryption and post-quantum symmetric security.

Expert definition

AES with 256-bit key and 14 SPN rounds; retains 128-bit resistance even against Grover's quantum search.
