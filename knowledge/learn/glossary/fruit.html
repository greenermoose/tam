Fruit

In the Tamlinux plant analogy, fruit means the programs you use to get work done.

Beginner definition

Applications you use to get work done.

Intermediate definition

The application layer: for example, document editors and browsers.

Expert definition

The workload-facing application layer. Its requirements constrain the layers below, while its settings and documents may have separate recovery lifecycles.
