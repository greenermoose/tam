FREAK (Factoring RSA Export Keys)

2015 TLS downgrade attack forcing servers and clients onto weak 512-bit export RSA.

Novice definition

FREAK was a TLS flaw where attackers tricked browsers into using deliberately weakened 1990s 'export-grade' encryption.

Learner definition

Modern TLS 1.3 removes export ciphers and signs handshake transcripts to prevent downgrade attacks.

Expert definition

Man-in-the-middle downgrade to 512-bit RSA_EXPORT suites; fixed by removing export suites and transcript-signing in TLS 1.3.
