SVID (Serial Voltage Identification)

Intel high-speed serial bus between a CPU and its VRM to request dynamic voltage changes.

Novice definition

SVID is the fast control wire a CPU uses to tell the motherboard's power circuit what voltage it needs right now.

Learner definition

During DVFS P-state transitions and C10 sleep entry, the CPU commands VRM voltage steps over SVID.

Expert definition

Intel 3-wire (clock, data, alert) serial protocol between processor VR controller and VRM PWM controller.
