FTDI (Future Technology Devices International)

Widely used maker of USB-to-UART/serial bridge chips (such as FT232R).

Novice definition

FTDI is a well-known brand of USB-to-serial adapter chips used to connect a laptop to hardware debug ports.

Learner definition

On Linux, FTDI adapters appear as /dev/ttyUSB0 via the kernel ftdi_sio driver.

Expert definition

Vendor of USB-to-UART/FIFO/SPI bridge ICs (FT232R, FT2232H) supported natively by Linux ftdi_sio.
