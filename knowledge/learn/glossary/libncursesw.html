libncursesw

The wide-character version of ncurses.

Novice definition

libncursesw is the ncurses library built to handle wide characters and Unicode text.

Learner definition

libncursesw adds wide-character and multibyte support to ncurses for international text.

Expert definition

libncursesw is the ncurses ABI variant compiled with wide-character support, exposing wchar_t and cchar_t interfaces.
