MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication)

Requiring two or more independent proofs of identity (knowledge, possession, or biometrics) to log in.

Novice definition

MFA means proving who you are with more than just a password—for example, a password plus a USB security key.

Learner definition

Strong MFA combines something you know (passphrase) with something you have (FIDO2 key) or are (fingerprint).

Expert definition

Authentication requiring >=2 distinct factors (knowledge, possession, inherence); hardware FIDO2/WebAuthn resists phishing unlike SMS OTP.
