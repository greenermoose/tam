OLED

A display whose pixels emit their own light.

Novice definition

An OLED screen has pixels that light themselves, giving deep blacks and thin panels.

Learner definition

Organic light-emitting diode displays use emissive organic layers, avoiding a separate backlight.

Expert definition

An OLED is an emissive display with organic electroluminescent layers, per-pixel drive, and burn-in and lifetime trade-offs.
