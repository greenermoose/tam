DVI - Digital Visual Interface

A digital video connector that preceded HDMI.

Novice definition

DVI is the older digital video plug used before HDMI and DisplayPort.

Learner definition

Digital Visual Interface carries digital or analog video over a compact connector with TMDS lanes.

Expert definition

DVI is a TMDS-based interface with DVI-D, DVI-A, and DVI-I variants, single or dual link, and optional DDC.
