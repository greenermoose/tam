Lattice

A regular grid of points in many dimensions used to build quantum-safe cryptography.

Novice definition

A lattice is like a repeating 3D crystal grid, except extended to hundreds of dimensions—making it easy to step to a nearby point if you know the secret grid directions, and nearly impossible if you do not.

Learner definition

Structured lattice problems such as Learning With Errors (LWE) underpin NIST's primary post-quantum standards ML-KEM (key encapsulation) and ML-DSA (digital signatures).

Expert definition

A discrete additive subgroup of R^n generated by integer linear combinations of basis vectors; worst-case lattice problems (GapSVP, SIVP) reduce to average-case LWE and Module-LWE.
