CTR (Counter mode)

Block-cipher mode encrypting an incrementing counter to produce a stream cipher.

Novice definition

CTR mode turns a block cipher into a stream cipher by encrypting a sequence of counting numbers.

Learner definition

Used in AES-CTR and AES-GCM, CTR mode allows parallel encryption and random-access decryption, provided nonces never repeat.

Expert definition

NIST SP 800-38A Counter mode: O_i = E_K(IV || i), C_i = P_i XOR O_i.
