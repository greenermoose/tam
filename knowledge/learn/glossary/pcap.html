PCAP (Projected Capacitive Touch)

Multi-touch capacitive screen technology sensing finger capacitance through glass.

Novice definition

PCAP (projected capacitive) is the touch technology behind modern smartphone and laptop touchscreens.

Learner definition

Note: in networking, 'pcap' also refers to packet-capture files; in display history it means Projected Capacitive touch.

Expert definition

Mutual-capacitance X/Y ITO electrode grid detecting finger-induced electric-field coupling through cover glass.
