Ethernet

The standard wired local-network technology.

Novice definition

Ethernet is the common wired networking standard used by cables and network ports.

Learner definition

Ethernet defines frames, MAC addressing, and physical links at 10 Mbps through 400 Gbps and beyond.

Expert definition

Ethernet is an IEEE 802.3 family covering framing, CSMA/CD history, full-duplex switched links, VLAN tags, and PHY specifications.
