Font

A collection of character shapes and metrics.

Novice definition

A font is the design of letters and symbols used to display text.

Learner definition

A font contains glyph outlines, metrics, and mappings used to render text at various sizes.

Expert definition

A font is a digital typeface resource with outlines, hinting, metrics, cmap, and shaping tables, loaded by fontconfig and a renderer.
