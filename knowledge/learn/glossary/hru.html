HRU (Harrison-Ruzzo-Ullman model)

Classic formal access-control model proving that general safety verification is undecidable.

Novice definition

The HRU model is a foundational computer-security theorem showing why unrestricted permission systems are hard to verify automatically.

Learner definition

In access-control theory, HRU models subjects, objects, and an access matrix with primitive create/enter/delete commands.

Expert definition

1976 access-matrix safety model proving undecidability of general leakage unless restricted to mono-operational systems.
