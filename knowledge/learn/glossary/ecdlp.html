ECDLP (Elliptic Curve Discrete Logarithm Problem)

Discrete logarithm problem over elliptic-curve point groups, enabling compact keys.

Novice definition

ECDLP is the elliptic-curve version of the discrete logarithm puzzle, allowing much shorter keys than RSA for the same security.

Learner definition

P-256 and Curve25519 rely on ECDLP; like DLP, it is vulnerable to large-scale quantum computers via Shor's algorithm.

Expert definition

Given points P and Q = kP on an elliptic curve over a finite field, find scalar k; best classical attack is Pollard's rho in O(sqrt(n)).
