ASPM (Active State Power Management)

PCIe power-saving mechanism putting idle links into low-power L0s/L1 states.

Novice definition

ASPM automatically puts high-speed PCIe slots (like SSDs and Wi-Fi cards) into a light sleep when they are idle.

Learner definition

Linux power-tuning tools check PCIe ASPM policies (powersave vs. performance) to save laptop battery.

Expert definition

PCI Express link-layer power states (L0, L0s, L1, L1.1/L1.2) negotiated via /sys/module/pcie_aspm/parameters/policy.
