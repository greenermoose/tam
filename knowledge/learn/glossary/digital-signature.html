Digital signature

A cryptographic proof created with a private key and verified with a public key.

Novice definition

A digital signature proves who sent a file or message and shows that nobody changed it after it was signed.

Learner definition

Unlike a symmetric MAC, anyone with the signer's public key can verify a digital signature, while only the holder of the private key can produce one.

Expert definition

An asymmetric tuple (KeyGen, Sign, Verify) achieving EUF-CMA security; modern schemes include Ed25519, ECDSA, RSA-PSS, and post-quantum ML-DSA and SLH-DSA.
