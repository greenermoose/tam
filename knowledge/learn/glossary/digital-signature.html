Digital signature

A cryptographic proof that data came from a key holder and was not altered.

Novice definition

A digital signature is a mathematical stamp that proves who made a file and that it has not been changed.

Learner definition

A digital signature uses a private key to sign a digest and a public key to verify authenticity and integrity.

Expert definition

A digital signature is a public-key authentication scheme, often over a hash, providing integrity, authenticity, and non-repudiation.
