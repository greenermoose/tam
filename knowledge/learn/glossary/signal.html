Signal

A signal is a notification sent to a running program, such as a request to interrupt it.

Beginner definition

A notification sent to a process, such as an interruption request.

Intermediate definition

A numbered notification with a disposition; SIGTERM can be handled or ignored, so sending it does not prove cleanup completed.

Expert definition

A process/thread notification governed by delivery, disposition and masking rules. SIGKILL and SIGSTOP cannot be caught, blocked or ignored; use signal(7) for precise semantics.
