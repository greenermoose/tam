ATX (Advanced Technology eXtended)

Standard desktop motherboard and power-supply form factor and connector specification.

Novice definition

ATX is the standard size and power-plug layout used by most desktop PC motherboards and power supplies.

Learner definition

ATX defines +12V, +5V, +3.3V, and +5VSB standby rails plus the PS_ON# power-good signaling.

Expert definition

Intel ATX12V specification defining desktop chassis mounting, 24-pin main power rails, and Power Good timing.
