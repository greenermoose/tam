AVX2 (Advanced Vector Extensions 2)

256-bit integer SIMD and FMA extension on x86_64 (Haswell / Excavator and newer).

Novice definition

AVX2 upgrades AVX to handle 256-bit whole-number math at once, speeding up video, encryption, and compression.

Learner definition

Because pre-2013 CPUs lack AVX2, distributed packages target baseline x86_64 while local -march=native builds can use AVX2.

Expert definition

x86-64-v3 ISA level feature expanding most SSE/AVX integer ops to 256-bit YMM registers plus gather instructions.
