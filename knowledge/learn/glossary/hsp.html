HSP (Hidden Subgroup Problem)

Algebraic problem generalized by Shor's algorithm to break RSA, Diffie-Hellman, and ECC on quantum computers.

Novice definition

The Hidden Subgroup Problem is the general math puzzle that quantum computers solve easily for RSA and elliptic curves, but not for lattices.

Learner definition

Abelian HSP is solved in polynomial time by QFT/Shor's algorithm, whereas non-abelian/lattice problems resist known quantum speedups.

Expert definition

Group-theoretic problem of identifying a hidden subgroup H <= G from a function f constant on cosets.
