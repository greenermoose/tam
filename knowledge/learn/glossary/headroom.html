Headroom

Extra capacity kept in reserve.

Novice definition

Headroom is the spare space or capacity left so a system can handle growth or bursts.

Learner definition

Headroom is the margin between current use and a limit, such as free disk, memory, or bandwidth.

Expert definition

Headroom is an operational reserve measured against a hard or soft limit, used for burst absorption, snapshots, and recovery.
