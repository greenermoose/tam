VPN

A private tunnel over a public network.

Novice definition

A VPN is a secure tunnel that makes a remote network look local.

Learner definition

A virtual private network encrypts and encapsulates traffic between peers or a client and gateway.

Expert definition

A VPN is a tunnel using IPsec, WireGuard, OpenVPN, or similar, providing confidentiality, authentication, and virtual addressing.
