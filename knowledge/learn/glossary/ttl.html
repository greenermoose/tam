TTL (Transistor-Transistor Logic)

Classic 5 V (or loosely 3.3 V) single-ended digital logic voltage levels used on board-level UARTs.

Novice definition

In hardware tinkering, 'TTL serial' means low-voltage (3.3 V or 5 V) serial pins directly on a circuit board, unlike high-voltage RS-232.

Learner definition

USB-to-TTL UART adapters connect a PC to microcontroller or router debug headers—always match 3.3 V vs. 5 V levels.

Expert definition

Originally 5 V bipolar logic (V_IL <= 0.8 V, V_IH >= 2.0 V); colloquially denotes uninverted 3.3 V/5 V CMOS UART signaling.
