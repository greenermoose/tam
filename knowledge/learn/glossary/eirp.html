EIRP (Effective Isotropic Radiated Power)

Measure of total radiated radio power combining transmitter output and antenna gain.

Novice definition

EIRP measures how strongly a Wi-Fi or radio device broadcasts in its strongest direction, which regulators cap by law.

Learner definition

Calculated in dBm as (TX power - cable loss + antenna gain), EIRP limits vary by country and Wi-Fi band.

Expert definition

Equivalent power an isotropic antenna would need to match peak directional power flux density: P_EIRP(dBm) = P_TX - L_c + G_ant.
