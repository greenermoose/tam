PCIe - Peripheral Component Interconnect Express

The modern high-speed expansion link.

Novice definition

PCIe is the fast slot and link used by graphics cards, SSDs, and other high-speed devices.

Learner definition

PCI Express is a point-to-point serial fabric that carries PCI-style transactions over lanes.

Expert definition

PCIe is a layered packet-based interconnect with lanes, root complexes, switches, and transaction, data-link, and physical layers.
