BOM (Bill of Materials / Byte Order Mark)

Inventory of software/hardware components (SBOM) or Unicode U+FEFF Byte Order Mark.

Novice definition

A BOM (Bill of Materials) is a complete ingredients list of every library, package, or part inside a product.

Learner definition

In software supply chains, an SBOM lists every dependency and version; in text files, a UTF-8 BOM (EF BB BF) is usually avoided on Linux.

Expert definition

Software/Hardware Bill of Materials (SPDX/CycloneDX) for provenance, or Unicode U+FEFF encoding signature.
