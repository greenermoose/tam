Contrast ratio

The difference between the brightest and darkest parts of an image.

Novice definition

Contrast ratio says how bright white can get compared with black.

Learner definition

Contrast ratio compares maximum and minimum luminance, often measured simultaneously or sequentially.

Expert definition

Contrast ratio is a luminance ratio, with static, dynamic, and ANSI variants; OLED and local dimming complicate measurement.
