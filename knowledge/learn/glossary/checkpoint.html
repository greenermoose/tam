Checkpoint

Beginner definition

A recorded state of a system component captured before making a change, providing a specific rollback point if the change fails.

Intermediate definition

Checkpoints exist at distinct layers: filesystem subvolume snapshots, bootloader and EFI records, and mutable settings backups. A checkpoint at one layer does not protect or restore another.

Expert definition

A point-in-time state capture characterized by quiescent writers, boundary specification, and verified integrity. Captures occur only before actual mutations of that layer, while long-term fallback relies on an established recovery anchor.
