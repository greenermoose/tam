LCD - Liquid-Crystal Display

A flat display that controls light with liquid crystals.

Novice definition

An LCD is the common flat screen technology that uses liquid crystals and a backlight.

Learner definition

A liquid-crystal display modulates polarized light through pixel cells and color filters.

Expert definition

An LCD is a transmissive or reflective panel using liquid-crystal cells, polarizers, TFT addressing, and a backlight or frontlight.
