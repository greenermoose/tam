DVFS (Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling)

Power-management technique lowering CPU/GPU clock speed and voltage together when load is light.

Novice definition

DVFS automatically slows down a processor and lowers its voltage when you are just reading, saving battery and heat.

Learner definition

Because dynamic power scales with V^2 * f, lowering voltage alongside frequency yields cubic power savings.

Expert definition

Hardware/OS P-state scaling (amd-pstate, intel_pstate) adjusting PLL frequency and VRM voltage.
