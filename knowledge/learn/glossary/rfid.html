RFID (Radio-Frequency Identification)

Wireless identification using electromagnetic fields to power and read passive or active tags.

Novice definition

RFID uses tiny wireless chips—like transit cards or badge fobs—that answer a reader without needing a battery.

Learner definition

NFC is a specialized short-range 13.56 MHz subset of RFID used by security keys and payment cards.

Expert definition

Inductive/backscatter RF identification across LF (125 kHz), HF (13.56 MHz), and UHF (860-960 MHz) bands.
