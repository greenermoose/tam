IND-CCA2 (Indistinguishability under Adaptive Chosen-Ciphertext Attack)

Gold-standard security property for public-key encryption and KEMs.

Novice definition

IND-CCA2 is the standard benchmark proving an encryption scheme leaks nothing even against active tampering.

Learner definition

Modern schemes like RSA-OAEP and ML-KEM (via Fujisaki-Okamoto transform) achieve IND-CCA2 security.

Expert definition

Game-based security definition where a PPT adversary cannot distinguish encryptions of m0 vs. m1 even with adaptive decryption oracle queries (except on the challenge ciphertext).
