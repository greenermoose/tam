Runlevel

A traditional numbered system state.

Novice definition

A runlevel is an old way of describing which services should run, such as single-user or graphical mode.

Learner definition

Runlevels 0 through 6 defined shutdown, single-user, multi-user, and graphical states in SysV init.

Expert definition

A runlevel is a SysV init system state selected by /etc/inittab, mapped to systemd targets on modern Linux.
