Privilege

Special permission to do something restricted.

Novice definition

Privilege is the extra power to perform a protected action, such as changing system files.

Learner definition

Privilege is an authorization to bypass or perform operations beyond ordinary user permissions.

Expert definition

Privilege is a kernel-enforced capability, credential, or policy grant, often decomposed into capabilities rather than all-or-nothing root.
