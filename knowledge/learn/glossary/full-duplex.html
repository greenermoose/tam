Full Duplex

Full duplex is a communication channel that supports simultaneous transmission in both directions.

Novice definition

A two-way connection where both devices can talk and listen at the exact same time without waiting, like two people on a telephone call. Modern wired computer networks and serial cables operate in full duplex using separate wires for sending and receiving.

Learner definition

Bidirectional data transmission occurring concurrently across isolated physical pathways (such as 4-wire RS-485, RS-232 TX/RX lines, or twisted-pair switched Ethernet). Because transmit and receive lines are physically separate, collisions are completely eliminated.

Expert definition

True concurrent bidirectional signaling over dedicated independent channels or via frequency-division duplexing (FDD). Advanced transceivers (such as 1000BASE-T Ethernet) achieve full duplex over shared physical pairs using active hybrid hybrid-echo-cancellation DSP circuits.
