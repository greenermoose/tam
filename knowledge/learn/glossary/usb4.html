USB4

40-80 Gbps tunneled USB architecture based on Thunderbolt 3, carrying PCIe, DisplayPort, and USB 3.

Novice definition

USB4 is the fastest USB standard over USB-C, combining data, external monitors, and PCIe docks on one cable.

Learner definition

USB4 tunnels USB 3.2, DisplayPort, and PCIe packets dynamically over 40 Gbps (or 80 Gbps v2) dual-lane links.

Expert definition

USB4 Specification (Thunderbolt-derived connection manager) multiplexing Tunneled PCIe, DP, and USB3 over USB-C.
