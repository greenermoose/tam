tldr pages

Community-maintained concise command cheat sheets focused on practical examples.

Novice definition

tldr pages are short, practical example sheets for terminal commands when a full manual page is too long.

Learner definition

tam bundles curated offline tldr pages (tam tldr or from the Guide index).

Expert definition

Curated Markdown/indexed command-example corpus (tldr-pages) integrated into tam's offline reader.
