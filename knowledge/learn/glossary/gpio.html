GPIO - General-Purpose Input/Output

A general-purpose pin a computer can read or drive.

Novice definition

GPIO is a pin on a chip that software can use as a switch or sensor input.

Learner definition

General-purpose input/output pins expose digital levels and are configured for input, output, interrupts, or alternate functions.

Expert definition

GPIO is a programmable digital pin interface with direction, pull, drive strength, and interrupt configuration, often multiplexed with peripherals.
