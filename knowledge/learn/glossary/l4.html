L4 (L4 microkernel family)

Family of minimal, high-performance second-generation microkernels (including seL4).

Novice definition

L4 is a family of tiny, fast microkernel operating systems where drivers and filesystems run safely as normal programs.

Learner definition

Modern seL4 is mathematically proved secure in machine-checked proofs and informs microkernel research.

Expert definition

Jochen Liedtke's 2nd-generation microkernel lineage (L4Ka, Fiasco.OC, seL4) minimizing kernel TCB to IPC, threads, and address spaces.
