Adapter

A device that connects different interfaces or supplies power.

Novice definition

An adapter is a connector or power supply that lets one kind of plug or signal work with another.

Learner definition

An adapter converts power, video, or data between incompatible interfaces, sometimes actively.

Expert definition

An adapter is a physical or active conversion device, such as a USB-C dock, power brick, or video converter, with protocol and power limits.
