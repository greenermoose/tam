Execute

To carry out an instruction or program.

Novice definition

To execute is to actually perform the steps a program or instruction describes.

Learner definition

Execution takes decoded instructions and applies them to registers, memory, and devices.

Expert definition

Execute is the pipeline stage that performs arithmetic, memory access, or control transfer and produces results and condition flags.
