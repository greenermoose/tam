OCP (Over-Current Protection)

Power-supply safeguard that cuts or limits current before wires or chips overheat.

Novice definition

OCP is a built-in safety cutoff that shuts down a power supply or USB port if a device draws too much current.

Learner definition

On USB ports and PC PSUs, OCP prevents short circuits from melting cables or damaging traces.

Expert definition

Shunt/R_DS(on) current-sense comparator tripping hiccup or latched shutdown above I_ocp.
