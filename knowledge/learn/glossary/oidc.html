OIDC (OpenID Connect)

Identity layer on top of OAuth 2.0 providing signed ID tokens for user authentication.

Novice definition

OpenID Connect (OIDC) is the standard behind 'Sign in with ...' buttons that safely proves your identity to an app.

Learner definition

OIDC adds an ID Token (a signed JWT) and UserInfo endpoint on top of OAuth 2.0 authorization.

Expert definition

OpenID Connect 1.0 identity layer over OAuth 2.0 (RFC 6749), issuing JWS ID Tokens verified against JWKS keys.
