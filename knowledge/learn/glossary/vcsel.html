VCSEL (Vertical-Cavity Surface-Emitting Laser)

Low-cost semiconductor laser emitting vertically from the wafer surface, used in short-reach multimode fiber.

Novice definition

A VCSEL is a tiny, efficient laser chip used in short-range fiber-optic cables and some optical sensors.

Learner definition

850 nm VCSELs power OM3/OM4 multimode SFP+ transceivers over 100-400 meter datacenter links.

Expert definition

Surface-emitting laser diode with distributed Bragg reflector (DBR) mirrors; lower threshold current than edge-emitting DFB lasers.
