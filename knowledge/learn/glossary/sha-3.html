SHA-3

Sponge-based cryptographic hash family (Keccak) standardized in NIST FIPS 202.

Novice definition

SHA-3 is a newer family of secure hash functions built with a completely different design from SHA-2 as a backup standard.

Learner definition

Part of SHA algorithms, SHA-3 uses the Keccak sponge construction and resists length-extension attacks.

Expert definition

NIST FIPS 202 (2015) Keccak sponge family (SHA3-224/256/384/512 and SHAKE128/256 extendable-output functions).
