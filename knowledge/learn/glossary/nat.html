NAT

Rewriting network addresses as packets pass through.

Novice definition

NAT lets many devices share one public address by translating addresses and ports.

Learner definition

Network address translation rewrites source or destination addresses and often ports, maintaining a translation table.

Expert definition

NAT is a stateful header-rewriting function, including SNAT, DNAT, masquerading, and connection tracking, with ALG complications.
