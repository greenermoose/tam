Passphrase

A longer password made of words.

Novice definition

A passphrase is a password made from several words, easier to remember and often stronger.

Learner definition

A passphrase is a knowledge secret, often used to unlock key material or disk encryption.

Expert definition

A passphrase is a high-entropy human-memorable secret, commonly fed through a KDF to derive a key-encryption key.
