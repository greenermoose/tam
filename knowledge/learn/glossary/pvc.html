PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride)

Common flexible plastic insulation jacket used on standard electrical and network cables.

Novice definition

PVC is the standard plastic outer jacket on most everyday power and Ethernet cables.

Learner definition

In-wall or plenum runs require low-smoke plenum-rated jackets instead of standard PVC.

Expert definition

Thermoplastic polymer cable dielectric/jacket; higher dielectric loss than PTFE/FEP at multi-GHz frequencies.
