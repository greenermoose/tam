Refresh rate

How many times per second a display redraws.

Novice definition

Refresh rate is how often a screen updates its picture, measured in hertz.

Learner definition

Refresh rate is the number of full frames per second a display scans out, such as 60 Hz or 144 Hz.

Expert definition

Refresh rate is the display scan-out frequency, tied to pixel clock, blanking intervals, and variable-refresh capabilities.
