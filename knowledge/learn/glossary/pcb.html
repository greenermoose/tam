PCB - Printed Circuit Board

A board that holds and connects electronic components.

Novice definition

A PCB is the green or colored board that carries chips and wires between them.

Learner definition

A printed circuit board uses copper traces and planes on insulating layers to connect components.

Expert definition

A PCB is a laminated dielectric substrate with patterned copper conductors, vias, and pads supporting assembled components.
