LK201 (DEC Keyboard)

1983 DEC terminal keyboard whose layout and key codes shaped VT220 escape sequences.

Novice definition

The LK201 was the keyboard shipped with DEC VT220 terminals, featuring a row of 20 function keys.

Learner definition

Gaps in modern terminal function-key numbering (skipping 16 and 22) come from non-function keys in the LK201 top row.

Expert definition

DEC LK201 keyboard (1983) grouped F1-F20 into blocks; positions 16 and 22 corresponded to Break/Do or group separators, explaining why CSI 16 ~ and 22 ~ are absent.
