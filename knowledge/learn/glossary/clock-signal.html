Clock signal

A regular timing pulse that coordinates digital circuits.

Novice definition

A clock signal is the steady beat that keeps a computer's parts working in step.

Learner definition

A clock signal alternates high and low to sequence logic; its frequency is measured in hertz.

Expert definition

A clock signal is a periodic square wave distributed to synchronous circuits, defining setup and hold windows and clock domains.
