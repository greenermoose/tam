Rasterization

Rasterization is the process of converting mathematical shapes, curves, or 3D models into a two-dimensional grid of pixels for display on a screen.

Novice definition

Turning abstract geometric drawings, fonts, or 3D objects into dots of colored light on a monitor.

Learner definition

The stage in a graphics pipeline where vector primitives (points, lines, and triangles) are projected onto screen coordinates and evaluated to determine which individual pixel centers fall inside each geometric shape.

Expert definition

Fixed-function or compute-driven hardware evaluation of primitive edge equations across bounding boxes. For each covered pixel, the rasterizer interpolates vertex attributes (depth, normals, texture coordinates, color) across the triangle surface, generating discrete fragments passed to fragment shader pipelines.
