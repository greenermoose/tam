LR (Long Reach / Link Register)

10 km single-mode fiber optic standard (e.g., 10GBASE-LR), or ARM Link Register.

Novice definition

In fiber optics, LR ('Long Reach') means a 1310 nm laser transceiver built for cables up to 10 kilometers.

Learner definition

10GBASE-LR uses single-mode fiber (OS2), unlike short-reach 850 nm SR modules on multimode fiber.

Expert definition

IEEE 802.3 Clause 52 10GBASE-LR (1310 nm DFB laser over OS2 SMF, 10 km); in ARM assembly, R14/X30 Link Register.
