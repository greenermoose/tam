HBR (High Bit Rate)

DisplayPort 2.7 Gbps per-lane link rate.

Novice definition

HBR is the classic standard DisplayPort speed (2.7 Gbps per lane), enough for 1080p/1440p displays.

Learner definition

Part of DisplayPort link rates; 4 lanes of HBR provide 8.64 Gbps of usable video bandwidth.

Expert definition

DisplayPort 1.0+ 2.70 Gbps/lane (8b/10b encoding).
