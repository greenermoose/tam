Quantum

Quantum describes the rule that some things in nature come in tiny fixed steps, not smooth continuous amounts.

Beginner definition

At the scale of atoms and electrons, energy and light often change only in fixed steps. Those steps are called quanta. Computer chips work because electrons in semiconductors obey these stepped energy rules, which create the bandgap that transistors switch across.

Intermediate definition

Quantum mechanics describes systems whose observables take discrete eigenvalues. In solids, allowed electron energies form bands; transitions between bands involve discrete energy exchanges with photons or phonons. Everyday circuit models rest on that microscopic picture.

Expert definition

Pertaining to physical quantities that take discrete values; solid-state devices rely on quantized electronic energy bands and transitions.
