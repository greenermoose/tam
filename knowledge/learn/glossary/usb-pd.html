USB-PD (USB Power Delivery)

Protocol negotiating variable voltages (5 V to 48 V) and up to 240 W over USB-C CC pins.

Novice definition

USB-PD lets a USB-C charger and your laptop safely agree on higher voltage and wattage for fast charging.

Learner definition

Using biphase-mark coding over the USB-C CC wire, USB-PD negotiates 5 V, 9 V, 15 V, 20 V, or EPR 28-48 V profiles.

Expert definition

USB Power Delivery 3.1 protocol (BMC over CC1/CC2) supporting PDO negotiation up to 240 W (48 V / 5 A EPR).
