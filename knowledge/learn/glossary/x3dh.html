X3DH (Extended Triple Diffie-Hellman)

Asynchronous key-agreement protocol used by Signal and end-to-end encrypted messaging.

Novice definition

X3DH is the handshake that lets messaging apps start an encrypted chat even when the other person is offline.

Learner definition

By combining long-term identity keys, signed prekeys, and one-time prekeys, X3DH achieves forward secrecy and deniability.

Expert definition

Signal Protocol asynchronous key agreement executing 3-4 DH operations (X25519/X448) over uploaded prekey bundles.
