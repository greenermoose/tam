PCI-SIG (PCI Special Interest Group)

Industry consortium maintaining the PCI Express (PCIe) specification.

Novice definition

PCI-SIG is the industry group that defines the PCIe expansion slot standards used by GPUs and NVMe SSDs.

Learner definition

PCI-SIG publishes PCIe 4.0, 5.0, and 6.0 electrical, FLIT, and error-checking specifications.

Expert definition

Standards body owning PCI Express Base and CEM specifications.
