Electron

An electron is a tiny negatively charged particle that moves in atoms and in electric circuits.

Beginner definition

An electron is one of the particles that make up ordinary matter. In metals and semiconductors, some electrons can move and carry electric current. When people talk about electricity in wires and chips, they are usually talking about the motion of electrons.

Intermediate definition

In a conductor, loosely held electrons drift under an electric field and form a current. In a semiconductor, far fewer electrons occupy the conduction band unless energy or doping adds them. Device physics tracks electron and hole concentrations separately.

Expert definition

A negatively charged elementary particle whose motion and energy states determine electrical conduction in solids and the behavior of transistors.
