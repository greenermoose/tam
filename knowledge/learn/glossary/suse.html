SUSE / openSUSE

Long-standing German Linux family known for YaST, Zypper, and early Btrfs/Snapper adoption.

Novice definition

SUSE (and community openSUSE) is a major Linux distribution family that created the Snapper snapshot tool used by Tamlinux.

Learner definition

openSUSE Leap (fixed) and Tumbleweed (rolling) pioneered Btrfs root snapshots with Snapper and RPM/Zypper packaging.

Expert definition

RPM-based Linux family (SLE, openSUSE Tumbleweed/Leap) originating Snapper, YaST, and Open Build Service (OBS).
