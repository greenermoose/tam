Throughput

The rate data actually moves.

Novice definition

Throughput is how much data really gets through a connection in a given time.

Learner definition

Throughput is achieved goodput or transfer rate, usually lower than raw bandwidth because of overhead and loss.

Expert definition

Throughput is the measured rate of successful data delivery at a layer, affected by framing, congestion, errors, and protocol overhead.
