LSB (Least Significant Bit)

Lowest-value bit (2^0) in a binary number, or first bit transmitted in UART serial.

Novice definition

The LSB is the 'ones place' bit at the rightmost end of a binary number.

Learner definition

In standard UART serial communication, bytes are sent LSB-first right after the start bit.

Expert definition

Bit position 0 (weight 2^0) of an integer or byte; LSB-first transmission order in RS-232/UART and Ethernet.
