MAC address

A hardware address for a network interface.

Novice definition

A MAC address is the unique-looking address burned into a network card.

Learner definition

A MAC address is a 48-bit or 64-bit link-layer identifier used on Ethernet and Wi-Fi, often assigned by the vendor.

Expert definition

A MAC address is an EUI-48 or EUI-64 link-layer address with OUI and NIC parts, used for local delivery and neighbor discovery.
