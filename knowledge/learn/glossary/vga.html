VGA

An old analog video connector and mode.

Novice definition

VGA is the blue trapezoid connector and analog signal used by older monitors.

Learner definition

Video Graphics Array is an analog RGBHV interface and a family of 640x480-era display modes.

Expert definition

VGA is an analog video standard with separate red, green, blue, horizontal sync, and vertical sync signals, plus DDC support.
