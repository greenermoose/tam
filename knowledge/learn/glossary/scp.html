SCP (Short-Circuit Protection)

Fast power-supply cutoff triggered when output impedance drops near zero.

Novice definition

SCP immediately shuts off power if two wires accidentally touch and create a short circuit.

Learner definition

Unlike slow thermal fuses, electronic SCP in PSUs and USB-PD chargers trips in microseconds.

Expert definition

Rapid-response fault protection collapsing output rails upon near-zero load impedance.
