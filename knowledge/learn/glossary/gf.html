GF (Galois Field / Finite Field)

Finite mathematical field (such as GF(2^128)) used in AES, GCM, and error-correcting codes.

Novice definition

A Galois Field is a closed set of numbers with special arithmetic used inside encryption and error-checking math.

Learner definition

AES S-boxes use GF(2^8), while AES-GCM authentication (GHASH) and XTS tweaks operate in GF(2^128).

Expert definition

Finite field GF(p^n) of characteristic p; binary extension fields GF(2^n) map directly to bit-vectors with XOR addition and polynomial multiplication.
