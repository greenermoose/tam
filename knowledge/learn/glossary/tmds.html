TMDS

The signaling used by HDMI and DVI.

Novice definition

TMDS is the high-speed signaling method that carries digital video over HDMI and DVI cables.

Learner definition

Transition-minimized differential signaling encodes bytes over four differential pairs with minimal transitions for clock recovery.

Expert definition

TMDS is a serial differential link using 8b/10b-like encoding, a clock pair, and three data pairs for HDMI and DVI video.
