OFDM (Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiplexing)

Multi-carrier modulation splitting a wideband radio channel into many overlapping orthogonal subcarriers.

Novice definition

OFDM splits one wide Wi-Fi radio channel into hundreds of tiny parallel lanes so signals do not smear together.

Learner definition

Used in Wi-Fi (802.11a/g/n/ac/ax/be), 4G/5G, and DOCSIS 3.1, OFDM resists multipath indoor reflections.

Expert definition

IFFT/FFT-based multi-carrier modulation with cyclic prefix eliminating inter-symbol interference across frequency-selective channels.
