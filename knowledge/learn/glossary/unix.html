Unix

Influential 1970s Bell Labs operating system whose pipes, files, and POSIX design shaped Linux and BSD.

Novice definition

Unix is the foundational operating system created in the 1970s that introduced folders, pipes, and the 'everything is a file' idea.

Learner definition

Linux and BSD are Unix-like systems following POSIX/SUS standards inherited from Unix.

Expert definition

AT&T Bell Labs OS (Thompson & Ritchie) establishing hierarchical filesystems, fork/exec, pipes, TTYs, and C-based portability.
