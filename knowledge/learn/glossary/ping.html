Ping

A tool that tests whether a host responds.

Novice definition

Ping sends a small message to another computer and waits for a reply to check the connection.

Learner definition

Ping uses ICMP echo request and reply to measure reachability and round-trip time.

Expert definition

Ping is an ICMP or ICMPv6 echo diagnostic, reporting RTT and loss; it may be blocked or rate-limited independently of service availability.
