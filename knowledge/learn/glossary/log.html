Log

A record of events written by software.

Novice definition

A log is a file or stream where programs write messages about what they did or what went wrong.

Learner definition

A log records timestamped events from the kernel, services, and applications for diagnosis and audit.

Expert definition

A log is a structured or unstructured event record, often with priority, facility, timestamp, and provenance, collected by a logging system.
