AMD64

The 64-bit extension of x86.

Novice definition

AMD64 is the 64-bit version of the x86 processor family used by most PCs.

Learner definition

AMD64 extends x86 with 64-bit registers, addresses, and a new calling convention while retaining compatibility.

Expert definition

AMD64, also called x86-64, is the 64-bit ISA extension with long mode, additional registers, and RIP-relative addressing.
