DOCSIS (Data Over Cable Service Interface Specification)

Telecommunications standard for high-speed internet over coaxial cable TV lines.

Novice definition

DOCSIS is the standard that cable modems use to deliver internet over TV coaxial cables.

Learner definition

DOCSIS 3.1/4.0 uses OFDM and QAM modulation with LDPC error correction to reach multi-gigabit speeds over coax.

Expert definition

CableLabs MAC/PHY specification multiplexing upstream/downstream RF channels over hybrid fiber-coax (HFC) networks.
