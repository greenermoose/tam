DisplayPort link rates (RBR, HBR, HBR2, HBR3, UHBR)

Standardized per-lane bit rates from 1.62 Gbps (RBR) to 20 Gbps (UHBR20).

Novice definition

DisplayPort cables negotiate a speed grade—from basic RBR up to ultra-fast UHBR—depending on your monitor's resolution and cable quality.

Learner definition

DisplayPort link-training steps through RBR, HBR, HBR2, HBR3, and UHBR.

Expert definition

VESA DisplayPort per-lane raw bit rates: RBR (1.62G), HBR (2.7G), HBR2 (5.4G), HBR3 (8.1G), and UHBR 10/13.5/20G.
