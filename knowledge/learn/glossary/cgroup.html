Cgroup (control group)

A kernel feature that groups processes for resource control.

Novice definition

A cgroup is a group of processes that the system can limit, measure, or isolate together.

Learner definition

Control groups organize processes hierarchically and apply CPU, memory, I/O, and other resource controls.

Expert definition

A cgroup is a hierarchical kernel mechanism exposing controllers through a unified or legacy filesystem, with delegation and accounting semantics.
