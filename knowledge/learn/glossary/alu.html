ALU

The part of a processor that does arithmetic and logic.

Novice definition

The ALU is the calculator inside a CPU that adds numbers and makes simple logical decisions.

Learner definition

The arithmetic logic unit performs integer arithmetic and bitwise operations under the control of instruction decoding.

Expert definition

An arithmetic logic unit is a combinational circuit that computes selected integer and bitwise functions and sets condition flags.
