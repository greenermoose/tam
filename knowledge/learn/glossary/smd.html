SMD (Surface-Mount Device)

Electronic component soldered flat onto PCB pads rather than through drilled holes.

Novice definition

SMDs are the tiny flat components soldered directly onto the surface of modern circuit boards.

Learner definition

Resistors, capacitors, and SOIC-8 chips on USB-UART adapters are SMDs.

Expert definition

Surface-mount technology (SMT) packages (0402, 0603, SOIC, QFN) soldered via reflow.
