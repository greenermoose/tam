Certificate authority

A trusted organization that signs digital certificates to vouch for public keys.

Novice definition

A certificate authority acts like a passport office for websites: it checks who owns a domain and signs a digital passport so your browser trusts that site's key.

Learner definition

Operating systems and browsers ship a root trust store of certificate authority keys used to validate certificate chains for HTTPS and software updates.

Expert definition

A trust anchor in a hierarchical PKI (RFC 5280) issuing X.509 certificates, audited via Certificate Transparency logs and constrained by revocation (CRL/OCSP) and CAA records.
