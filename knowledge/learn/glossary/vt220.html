VT220 (DEC Video Terminal)

1983 DEC terminal that introduced 8-bit C1 controls and numeric function-key sequences.

Novice definition

The VT220 was a classic 1980s terminal whose function-key codes (like ESC [ 1 5 ~) are still used by modern terminals.

Learner definition

VT220 introduced the LK201 keyboard layout and CSI ~ sequences for F5-F20, skipping 16 and 22 for historical hardware keys.

Expert definition

DEC VT220 (1983) added 8-bit character sets, C1 controls (0x80-0x9F), soft-downloadable fonts, and CSI n ~ function-key encodings (11~-15~, 17~-21~, 23~-24~).
