USB-C (USB Type-C)

Reversible 24-pin physical connector supporting USB data, USB-PD power, and DisplayPort Alt Mode.

Novice definition

USB-C is the small, reversible oval plug used for charging, data, and external monitors.

Learner definition

USB-C is a physical connector shape, not a speed guarantee: a USB-C cable might carry USB 2.0, USB4, DisplayPort, or 240 W power.

Expert definition

USB Type-C 24-pin reversible connector with CC1/CC2 configuration-channel pins for PD negotiation and Alt-Mode muxing.
