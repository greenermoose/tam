Chipset

Support chips that connect the CPU to other components.

Novice definition

The chipset is the set of helper chips that connect the processor to storage, USB, and other devices.

Learner definition

The chipset provides I/O and interconnect functions not built into the CPU, such as additional PCIe lanes and legacy devices.

Expert definition

A chipset is a group of integrated controllers implementing platform I/O, fabric, and management functions around the processor.
