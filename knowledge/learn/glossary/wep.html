WEP (Wired Equivalent Privacy)

Obsolete, broken 1997 Wi-Fi encryption protocol superseded by WPA2 and WPA3.

Novice definition

WEP was the first Wi-Fi security system from the 1990s; it is completely broken and should never be used.

Learner definition

WEP reused 24-bit RC4 IVs and lacked proper integrity checks, allowing keys to be recovered in minutes.

Expert definition

IEEE 802.11-1997 WEP combining RC4 with a 24-bit IV and linear CRC-32 ICV; broken by FMS and PTW attacks.
