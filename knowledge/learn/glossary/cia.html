CIA (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability)

The three foundational security properties of information systems.

Novice definition

The CIA triad stands for Confidentiality (keeping secrets private), Integrity (preventing tampering), and Availability (keeping systems working).

Learner definition

Security designs balance all three: encryption protects confidentiality, hashes/MACs protect integrity, and redundancy protects availability.

Expert definition

Foundational security model: Confidentiality (Bell-LaPadula), Integrity (Biba/Clark-Wilson, AEAD), and Availability (DoS/fault tolerance).
