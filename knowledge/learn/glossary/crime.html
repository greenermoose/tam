CRIME (Compression Ratio Info-leak Made Easy)

2012 attack exploiting TLS-layer compression to recover session cookies.

Novice definition

CRIME was an attack that leaked secret web cookies by measuring the size of TLS-compressed packets.

Learner definition

Because compression shrinks repeated text, attackers could test cookie guesses by watching packet lengths; TLS 1.3 forbids TLS compression.

Expert definition

Chosen-plaintext oracle against DEFLATE compression at the TLS record layer, eliminated by disabling TLS compression in TLS 1.2/1.3.
