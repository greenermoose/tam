Resolution

The number of pixels in an image or display.

Novice definition

Resolution is how many pixels wide and tall a screen or image is, such as 1920 by 1080.

Learner definition

Resolution gives the pixel dimensions of a display or image; higher resolution shows more detail but needs more bandwidth.

Expert definition

Resolution is the raster grid size in pixels, distinct from pixel density and physical size, and constrained by mode and scaling.
