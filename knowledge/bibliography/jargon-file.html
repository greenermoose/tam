Guide / Sources and further reading / The Jargon File

This page points to the hacker dictionary that records the culture around Emacs, vi, and the editor argument.

This reference is for readers at any level.

The Jargon File, edited by Eric S. Raymond. The entries EMACS, vi, and holy wars record the spoof expansions, the mode-confusion complaint, and the observation that such arguments often dress personal preferences as technical verdicts.

Online at catb.org. Free to read. The file is a cultural and historical source, not a normative specification; treat its jokes as jokes.
