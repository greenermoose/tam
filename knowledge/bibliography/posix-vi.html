POSIX ex and vi

This page points to the standardized definitions of the ex line editor and the vi screen editor.

This reference is for expert readers.

POSIX.1-2017, ex — line editor and POSIX.1-2017, vi — screen editor, in The Open Group Base Specifications, Issue 7 (IEEE Std 1003.1-2017). Defines the ex command grammar and addresses, startup files and EXINIT, exit status and error consequences, and the vi open and visual modes, which refer to ex for the full command language.

Online specification from The Open Group. Free to read. Standard published by IEEE and The Open Group.
