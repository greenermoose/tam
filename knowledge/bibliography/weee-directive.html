Guide / Sources and further reading / WEEE Directive: Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment

This page points to the European rules for collecting and recovering waste electrical and electronic equipment.

This reference is for expert readers.

Waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE), published by the European Commission; Directive 2012/19/EU, published in the Official Journal of the European Union. Sets out obligations for the collection, recovery and producer responsibility of electronic waste. Type: directive and regulator guidance. Level: expert.
