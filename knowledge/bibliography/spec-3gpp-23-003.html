3GPP TS 23.003: Numbering, addressing and identification, published by 3GPP; ETSI. Defines the identifiers used in mobile networks, including the IMSI, MSISDN and equipment identities, and their relationship to subscriber records. Author: 3GPP; ETSI. Type: standard. Level: advanced. Subject: networking.
