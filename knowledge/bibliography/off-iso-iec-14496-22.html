This page points to the ISO/IEC Open Font Format standard behind OTF and TTF fonts.

This reference is for expert readers.

ISO/IEC 14496-22 Open Font Format (ISO/IEC). Supports the formal definition of the Open Font Format and its relation to OpenType and TrueType font files. Use it for the standards-level statement of what an Open Font font contains.
