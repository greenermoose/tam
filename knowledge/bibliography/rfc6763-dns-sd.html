Guide / Sources and further reading / RFC 6763: DNS-Based Service Discovery

This page points to the IETF specification for DNS-Based Service Discovery, the convention for listing and resolving named service instances with ordinary DNS records.

This reference is for expert readers.

RFC 6763, by S. Cheshire and M. Krochmal (IETF, February 2013). Defines DNS-SD: service instance naming, browsing with PTR records, resolution with SRV and TXT records, service type and subtype names, the required empty TXT record, and handling of unknown TXT keys. It is commonly used with Multicast DNS on a local link.

Online IETF specification. Free. Open standard. Companion name-resolution specification: RFC 6762.
