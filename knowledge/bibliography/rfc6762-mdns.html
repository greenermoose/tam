RFC 6762: Multicast DNS

This page points to the IETF specification for Multicast DNS, the protocol that resolves names on a local link without a configured DNS server.

This reference is for expert readers.

RFC 6762, by S. Cheshire and M. Krochmal (IETF, February 2013). Defines Multicast DNS: the use of the DNS wire format with link-local multicast on UDP port 5353, the .local suffix, probing and announcement of unique records, cache-flush semantics, known-suppression, goodbye packets, and negative answers with NSEC records.

Online IETF specification. Free. Open standard. Companion service-discovery specification: RFC 6763.
