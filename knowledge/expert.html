Expert path

Gain: Explain process, descriptor and recovery boundaries using evidence.

For you if: You already adapt shell commands and want precise semantics and competing explanations.

Levels: starts and finishes at Expert (3); allow about three reading sessions. Choose another level whenever needed.

Stage 1: Descriptors

  1. Pipelines: run the redirection/status experiment.

Check yourself

Decide before revealing the explanation.

Does reversing two redirections necessarily preserve their destinations?

No. Bash applies them left to right; a duplication records the destination at that point.

Stage 2: Identity

  1. Processes: compare existence with stable identity.
  2. Permissions: observe shared inode modes.

Check yourself

Decide before revealing the explanation.

Can a successful existence check prove process health?

No. It says neither that the original identity persists nor that the process is healthy.

Stage 3: Recovery

  1. Update mechanics: compare a name with an open inode.

Check yourself

Decide before revealing the explanation.

What does an immutable generation omit?

Mutable documents/settings and potentially other native/boot state; recovery must identify each affected boundary.

Capstone

Explain an old-running/new-on-disk symptom without assuming a failed package transaction. Reproduce name-versus-open-file behavior only in a scratch directory.

Use the updates page experiment: open active.txt on descriptor 3, rename a replacement over its name, then compare a new open with the saved descriptor. The captured output is new followed by old. Explain why that supports a lifetime distinction, then record what additional process/version evidence a real incident would require. Close the descriptor and remove the exercise file/directory.
