Taking a screenshot or recording the screen

This page shows how to save a picture of your screen, copy text out of a picture, and record a short video.

Capture what you can see

Goal: you will be able to save a screenshot to a file, copy it, and find it again.

Why it matters: a screenshot lets you show someone an error message or keep a copy of something that will change. You do not need a separate program or an account online.

Before you start: you need a graphical session. The screen will freeze for a moment while you choose an area. Nothing is sent anywhere; the picture stays on your computer.

Find the key marked Print, PrtSc, or Print Screen, usually near the top right of the keyboard. Press it once. The screen stops changing and the pointer becomes a crosshair. Drag a box around what you want, then let go. The picture is saved and copied. If you change your mind, press Print again while the crosshair is showing.

Worked example

  1. Open a window with something you want to keep, such as this guide.
  2. Press Print. The screen freezes and the pointer becomes a crosshair.
  3. Hold the left mouse button and drag a box around the part you want, then release.
  4. Look for a notification saying the screenshot was saved to the clipboard and to a file. Click the notification if you want to draw on the picture.
  5. Open your Pictures folder. The newest file is named like screenshot-2026-11-02_14-30-00-AbCdEf.png.

Captured from the packaged keybindings and the capture script on 2026-11-02. The file name and folder will differ on your computer.

What just happened

  1. Pressing Print started tam-capture-screenshot in its smart selection mode.
  2. The picker froze the screen so the picture would not change while you chose.
  3. The chosen area was saved as a PNG file in your Pictures folder.
  4. The same PNG was placed on the clipboard, so you can paste it into a message or document.
  5. A notification offered to open the picture in the annotator.

Try it

Take a second screenshot of a single window: press Print, move the pointer over the window, and press Return. Then paste into a text editor with Meta + V and confirm the picture appears.

If it goes wrong

The crosshair does not appear
Another capture may already be open. Press Print once to cancel it, then try again.
No new file in Pictures
Check whether you pressed Esc or pressed Print again, which cancels the capture. Also check the notification; it names the folder that was used.
Nothing seems to happen
The notification may have expired. Open your Pictures folder and look for the newest file; the capture is saved even when the notification is missed.

Check yourself

Decide your answer before you reveal it.

Where does a screenshot go after you finish dragging the box?

It is saved as a PNG file in your Pictures folder and copied to the clipboard. Both happen, so you can paste it immediately or open the file later.

How do you cancel a screenshot after the screen has frozen?

Press Print again, or press Esc. The screen returns to normal and no file is saved.

More capture jobs

  • Copy text out of a picture: press Meta + Ctrl + Print, then drag over the text. The words go to the clipboard.
  • Record the screen: press Alt + Print. If a recording is already running, this stops it; otherwise the capture menu opens so you can start one. Recordings are saved in your Videos folder.
  • Pick a colour: press Meta + Print, then click a colour on screen. Its value is copied.
  • See every capture choice: press Meta + Ctrl + C to open the capture menu.
