Reading and dismissing notifications

This page shows how pop-up messages work, how to find one you missed, and how to silence them for a while.

Read, dismiss, and silence a notification

Goal: you will be able to dismiss a pop-up, open an older one, and turn notifications off and on again.

Why it matters: notifications carry messages from your programs, but a pile of pop-ups gets in the way. You can clear them without reading, and still find them later.

Before you start: you need a graphical session. Notifications are stored for your account only. A notification that offers an action can be clicked while it is showing.

A notification appears at the edge of the screen and fades after a while. To dismiss the newest one, press Meta + N. To dismiss all of them, press Meta + Shift + N. To silence new notifications, press Meta + Ctrl + N; press the same keys again to turn them back on.

Worked example

  1. Open a terminal with Meta + Return.
  2. Type tam-notification-send "Tea is ready" "Take the kettle off the heat" and press Return.
  3. A notification appears. The terminal prints nothing, because the message is the result.
  4. Press Meta + N to dismiss it.
  5. Press Meta + Shift + H to open the notification history and find the same message again.

Captured from the packaged notification commands and keybindings on 2026-11-02. The appearance of the pop-up depends on your desktop theme.

What just happened

  1. tam-notification-send sent a headline and a description to the session's notification service.
  2. The service showed a pop-up and recorded the message in your history.
  3. Meta + N removed the newest pop-up.
  4. Meta + Shift + H opened the history, which persists across restarts.

Try it

Send a notification with an action that opens this guide when clicked. Run tam-notification-send "Guide" "Click to open the guide" --exec tam show use/desktop/notifications, then click the pop-up.

If it goes wrong

No pop-up appears
Notifications may be silenced. Press Meta + Ctrl + N and look for a message saying notifications are on, then send yours again. You can still find the message in history.
The terminal reports an error
tam-notification-send needs a graphical session and the tools busctl and jq. Check that you are in a desktop session, not a text-only console.
A notification disappears too quickly
Open the history with Meta + Shift + H. Pop-ups are temporary; the history is not.

Check yourself

Decide your answer before you reveal it.

Which keys dismiss one notification, and which dismiss all of them?

Meta + N dismisses the newest one. Meta + Shift + N dismisses all of them.

If notifications are silenced, is a message lost?

No. It is recorded in the history, and you can open the history with Meta + Shift + H.

New words

Notification
A short message a program shows without taking over the screen.
Silence
Stop pop-ups from appearing for a while. Also called do not disturb.
History
The list of messages already received.
