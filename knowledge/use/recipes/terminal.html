Open a terminal and return to the Guide

This recipe shows how to open a terminal and how to reopen this Guide.

Steps

  1. On the graphical desktop, press Meta + Return to open a terminal.
  2. To leave the Guide if it is open, press Esc.
  3. To reopen Welcome, type tam hi and press Enter.
  4. Follow First steps or another link with Tab or ], then Right Arrow.
