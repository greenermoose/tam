Dictate with your voice

This recipe shows packaged voice dictation chords.

Steps

  1. Focus a text field.
  2. Hold F9, speak, then release F9 to transcribe.
  3. Or press Meta + Ctrl + X once to start and again to stop.
  4. Details: Voice dictation.

Check yourself

Is F9 a tap-toggle?

No. F9 is push-to-talk: hold to record, release to stop.
