Using Tamlinux

This page helps you find instructions for everyday work on your computer.

Choose a subject below. Press 1, 2, or 3 to select the reading level that suits you.

  • Desktop orientation: open and recover an empty practice window.
  • Workspaces: distinguish switches, window moves and display moves.
  • Voice dictation: speak to enter text locally without cloud dependencies.
  • Shell voice commands: issue safe, validated commands at the shell prompt.
  • Pipelines: connect and diagnose streams.
  • Jobs and signals: start, wait for and signal only your own tasks.
  • Permissions: practice modes in a temporary directory.
