Guide / Desktop and applications / Typography

Typography is the craft and technology of written text: how people invented writing, how letters are drawn, and how a computer turns stored characters into shapes on a screen. This series starts with no background at all and ends with research-level questions and a practical diagnostic method.

Start here

From marks to meaning begins with the first writing systems and needs nothing beyond reading. Choose Novice for the story, Learner for a short practice task, or Expert for precise references.
