Workspaces and Multi-Display Layout

Workspaces provide separate virtual desktops so you can group related tasks together without visual clutter.

Navigating workspaces

KeybindingAction
SUPER + 1 .. 9, 0Switch directly to workspace 1 through 10
SUPER + Shift + 1 .. 9, 0Move the active window to workspace 1 through 10
SUPER + TABMove to the next workspace
SUPER + Shift + TABMove to the previous workspace
SUPER + Ctrl + TABReturn to the former workspace

Multiple displays

Workspaces are navigated individually. To move the current workspace to a neighboring monitor, use SUPER + Shift + Alt + Arrow in the direction of that display.

The scratchpad

The scratchpad is a hidden tray for temporary utility windows (like a quick scratchpad terminal, music player, or notes editor):

  • SUPER + Alt + S — send the active window to the scratchpad.
  • SUPER + S — toggle the scratchpad workspace.
