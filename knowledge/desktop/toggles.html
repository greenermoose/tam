Desktop toggles

This page explains on/off switches for idle behaviour, night light, notification silencing, and related helpers.

What is a toggle?

A toggle turns a desktop behaviour on or off without opening a long settings app: keep the session awake, warm the screen colours at night, or quiet notifications.

Why it matters

Quick toggles prevent the machine from sleeping mid-task or blasting alerts while you focus.

How to think about it

Use the Toggle menu (Meta + Ctrl + O) or the dedicated commands named below; job recipes cover idle and night light day to day.

New words

Idle
Time with no input after which the session may dim, lock, or sleep.
Night light
Warmer display colours for evening use.

Find the toggle menu

Goal: you will be able to open the Toggle menu and recognize idle versus night-light controls.

Learning path: Everyday desktop tasks — Start | ← Back | Next →

Worked example

  1. Press Meta + Ctrl + O to run tam-menu toggle toggle.
  2. Leave the menu without changing anything if you are unsure.
  3. Read Lock and idle before changing sleep behaviour, and Night light before changing colours.

Try it

Open and dismiss the Toggle menu once. Do not disable idle on a laptop you will leave unattended.

Check yourself

Which chord opens the Toggle menu in packaged bindings?

Meta + Ctrl + O.
