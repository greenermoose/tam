Everyday desktop tasks

Set up displays, audio, Bluetooth, clipboard, screen capture, notifications, and screen locking.

Explore

  • Arranging your screens and changing brightness — Change brightness and text size, arrange monitors, and turn the laptop screen off or mirror it
  • Changing the volume and choosing speakers or headphones — Raise, lower and mute sound, switch outputs, and choose a microphone
  • Connecting Bluetooth headphones or a mouse — Turn Bluetooth on, pair a device, connect it, and remove it again
  • Finding something you copied earlier — Copy, paste, and recover earlier clipboard items, including images
  • Locking the screen and keeping it awake — Lock the screen, unlock it, prevent idle locking, and suspend the computer
  • Making the screen warmer at night — Turn the blue-light filter on or off and check its state
  • Reading and dismissing notifications — Read a pop-up, find an older message, silence notifications, and send one from the terminal
  • Taking a screenshot or recording the screen — Capture part of the screen, copy text from it, or record a short video
  • Turning the touchpad on or off and finding keyboard shortcuts — Toggle the touchpad, adjust keyboard backlight, and look up any keybinding
