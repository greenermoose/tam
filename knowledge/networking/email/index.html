Guide / Networking / Email

Email is a store-and-forward service: a client writes a message, servers carry and deliver it, and a client later reads it from a mailbox. This series follows that whole journey.

Start here

Begin with What is email? to separate the mail client from the mail server. To build the subject in order, follow From first message to reliable delivery. Each page offers Novice for the idea, Learner for practice, and Expert for detail.
