Guide / Systems and administration / Mobile communications / Texting and instant messaging

Texting looks simple: a person writes words, presses send and another person sees them. Behind that small act are radios, subscriber identities, message stores, routing systems and encryption choices. This series starts with no phone knowledge and ends with the questions researchers still study.

Start here

Begin with What is texting? to separate the phone in your hand from the networks and services behind it. To build the subject in order, follow From first text to research-level messaging.
