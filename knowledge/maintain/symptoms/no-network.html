No network or DNS only

This page helps when the network fails in whole or in part.

Checks

  1. Link up?
  2. Address assigned?
  3. Names resolve?

Follow Networking and the network recipe. Do not reorder DNS before proving link.

Check yourself

What should you record before changing the system?

The symptom, time, and what you have not yet measured.
