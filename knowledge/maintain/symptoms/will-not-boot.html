Will not boot

This page helps when the computer does not reach a usable login.

Checks

Evidence first: last visible logo or menu; whether Limine appears; whether unlock prompts appear.

  1. Name the boot phase with boot phases.
  2. Inspect artifacts with boot when the system is readable.
  3. Choose a route with boot recovery.

Check yourself

What should you record before changing the system?

The symptom, time, and what you have not yet measured.
