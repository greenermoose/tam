Login works but no desktop

This page helps when text login works but the graphical desktop does not appear.

Checks

On console-first Tamlinux, run tam-desktop after login when you want graphics.

  1. Confirm you are not waiting for a greeter that is not packaged.
  2. Check compositor and shell services from a VT.
  3. Read login routes and Session and desktop.

Check yourself

What should you record before changing the system?

The symptom, time, and what you have not yet measured.
