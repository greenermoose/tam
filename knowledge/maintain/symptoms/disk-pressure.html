Disk full or snapshot pressure

This page helps when the disk is full or snapshots grow without bound.

Checks

  1. Measure free space with documented read-only tools.
  2. Identify Btrfs snapshot pressure via Snapper.
  3. Plan cleanup with a recorded rollback point; do not delete unknown subvolumes casually.
  4. See filesystems.

Check yourself

What should you record before changing the system?

The symptom, time, and what you have not yet measured.
