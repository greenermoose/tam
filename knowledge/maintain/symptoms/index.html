Symptom index

This page starts from what you see and points to checks and teaching pages.

  • Blank screen after login
  • Will not boot
  • Login works but no desktop
  • No network or DNS only
  • Wrong binary after an update
  • Keyboard or mouse dead in the GUI
  • Disk full or snapshot pressure

Record the symptom and time before changing the system.
