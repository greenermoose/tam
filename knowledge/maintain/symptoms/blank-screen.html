Blank screen after login

This page helps when the screen is blank after you sign in.

Checks

Evidence first: Can you reach a VT with Ctrl+Alt+Fn? Is the panel powered? Which cable and input?

  1. Power and input selection on the panel.
  2. Cable and port (display cables).
  3. Session and compositor (compositors, tam-desktop).
  4. DRM/GPU programming from a working VT.

Teaching: Display and GPU, recovery.

Check yourself

What should you record before changing the system?

The symptom, time, and what you have not yet measured.
