Web links: press Enter twice to open them in your browser.

Answers: Right Arrow or + shows, Left Arrow or - hides, r next, * all.

Shell sandboxes start closed. Move the attention line to a sandbox title (or select it with Tab) and press Enter or Right Arrow. Type at the blinking cursor and press Enter to run a command. Use Up or Down Arrow to recall commands. Press Esc or press Left Arrow at the start of the input to close the sandbox and return to its title.
